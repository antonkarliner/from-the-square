WHAT IT MEASURES The OS-neutral JSONL schema uses milliseconds, normalized pointer coordinates, clicks, and text-arrival counts/sources (typed, on_screen, inserted). It stores no raw text or key names. The CLI can fit and evaluate other recordings in that format, with pointer-only and text-only support when sufficient measurements are present. Per-mode median/MAD profiles use cadence, serial dependence, pointer geometry, correction/chunk behaviour and click timing. Long pauses are censored using a declared rule while retaining only genuinely adjacent inlier pairs; sparse click intervals crossing dense pointer pauses are handled separately. Threshold 3.0 is fixed. Missing required measurements and unseen modes return doubt. WHAT CHANGED DURING DEVELOPMENT The first fake set used uniform slowdown as fatigue. A subsequent explicit stress test made v1 doubt all 9 mild and all 9 strong paused owners. v3 still doubted 1/9 strongly paused owners and failed one overbroad zero-error assertion. v4 fixes the pause treatment and replaces that unjustified universal assertion with an independent, hand-computed regression. The threshold was not raised. Fresh v4 seeds were frozen locally before its results. Both earlier failures and the 3/3 replay limit remain in history/iterations.json and EVALUATION.txt. On the same v4 data, v1 doubts 9/9 mild and 9/9 strong paused owners, v4 0/9 each; both accept exact replays. That comparison changes more than one feature and does not isolate a single cause.
