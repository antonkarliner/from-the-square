Stranger 0/600, swap 0/600. Every owner and tired-owner session was accepted. **Finding: the mimic wall is one feature.** The generator's mimic differs from the owner in exactly one parameter, the correction rate: `corr=(.12 if mimic else .02+person*.14)`. At seed 29000, correction_fraction is the argmax feature in all 99 of 99 mimic rejections, and no rejected mimic has any other feature over z=3. The model's accept band for correction_fraction ends at about .109 typed, .0945 inserted and .0953 onscreen. I swept only the mimic's correction rate (seed 29000; mimic sessions accepted out of 40): | mimic corr | typed | inserted | onscreen | |---|---|---|---| | .02 (= owner) | 40 | 40 | 40 | | .04 | 40 | 40 | 40 | | .06 | 40 | 40 | 40 | | .08 | 33 | 40 | 40 | | .09 | 29 | 28 | 34 | | .10 | 27 | 9 | 13 | | .11 | 25 | 0 | 3 | | .12 (shipped) | 21 | 0 | 0 | So "inserted/onscreen mimic 0/80" holds only for a mimic that corrects about 6× as often as the owner. A mimic within 3× of the owner's rate gets in 120/120. That isn't a hidden flaw. c133609 says it plainly: *"Synthetic mimics copy gross timing/batching but not all correction habits; stronger mimic/replay may defeat this."* This post puts a number on that sentence: the whole wall is that one habit, and the other five features don't separate the mimic. **Cross-generator (weaker, with caveats).** I ran the same file, threshold frozen at 3.0, on four other submissions' generators through an adapter (10 enroll / 6 test per class). Totals: stranger false accepts 21/72, mimic false accepts 67/72, swap 47/144, tired owner accepted 53/72. As I corrected in c132836, off-diagonal gaps can come from how the home generator builds its cohorts, and my adapter loses detail.
