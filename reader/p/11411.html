The method's discrimination rests heavily on handoff; copy that too and it mostly fails. This is the known weakness, and the $25 break-job angle: richer features (sub-movement structure, correction strategy) are what would be needed next. - **(b) Drift-poisoning: full stranger NOT accepted at the end, but stranger accept after poisoning rose 0% → 6/24 (25%).** Forty-one interpolated impostor sessions fed to the live profile dragged the template part-way toward the stranger. Slow template updates are a real attack surface; the mitigation (update only on high-confidence accepts, log the drift) slows it but does not stop it. - **(c) Jittered replay (±50 ms per event): 3/12 accepted (25%).** Hash-based replay detection catches verbatim replays only; a jittered replay scores like the owner because it IS the owner's timing, perturbed. A per-session nonce / challenge would be needed; out of scope for this passive prototype. ## Negative experiments (things tried, that failed, kept on record) - **Widening enrollment with tired sessions:** strangers accepted 24/24 (100%). Not the fix for fatigue. - **One global profile mixing styles (v1):** stranger accept 67%, swap test FAIL. Per-style profiles are required. - **Switch events with fixed 1.0 s steps and no dwell timing (v1):** all switch sessions hashed identically, 33% false replay flags. Dwell timing must be in the events. - **Fatigue adjustment with a collapsed switch feature (v3 first attempt):** pause and handoff were the same dwell quantity, so switch discrimination rested on one feature and strangers accepted 8/8 in switch. Fixed by giving switch two independent timings (scan pause + dwell). - **Fatigue adjustment that also moved correction-rate:** the calibration estimate for it was noisy (2.02 vs the true 1.6); it is not adjusted — correction latency/rate and sub-movements are always scored against the normal template. ## Limitations — stated plainly - **All results are synthetic.** Generator parameters are invented. No real-world security claim is made, and none should be made from this prototype. - The fatigue model assumes fatigue shifts speed/pause/handoff by roughly constant factors. A real caregiver mimic who has watched thousands of sessions would be stronger than this one — and the break test shows what happens when the mimic also copies handoff. - Enrollment is 12 sessions per style.
