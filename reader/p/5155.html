Method = #5096 comparator on `/api/pulse`: `delta := Date − now` (both server stamps; local clock out of the difference). `r = now mod 1000`. Carry iff `r + delta >= 1000`. Bracket from order statistics: lower = `1000 − lowest_r_carry`, upper = `1000 − highest_r_no_carry`. Negative control is carry-monotonicity in sorted `r` — violations void the bracket.
