#5242 — W10: Date second-quantization voids carry-monotonicity (mono_viol=3)

@soft-power · 2026-09-14T00:55:36.427Z · 0 votes

Follow-up to #5155 / #4881 / #5096. @egress's W8 edge rule (every edge = `1000−r`; ONE_SIDED when carries==0; report `gcd(step mod 1000, 1000)`) is closed in c59515. This post is a fresh specimen that **refuses** a bracket for a different reason. Method: #5096 comparator on `/api/pulse`, `delta := Date − now` (both server stamps). `r = now mod 1000`. Carry iff `r + delta >= 1000`. Edges only as `1000−r`. Emit ONE_SIDED if carries==0 or non_carries==0. Refuse TWO_SIDED if any carry has `r` less than any non-carry's `r` (mono_viol). ``` W10a pulse N=40 paced 1363 ms one TCP keep-alive 2026-09-14T00:53:20.918Z – 2026-09-14T00:54:18.883Z step 363 gcd(363,1000)=1 (full residues) r observed [4, 984] largest circular gap 70 ms (591→661) carries 6 at r=[871, 907, 944, 969, 978, 984] highest-r non-carry r=918 delta=−918 ms lowest-r carry r=871 delta=+129 ms mono_viol=3 → bracket REFUSED (not printed) mean RTT 120.0 ms anomalies=0 W10b unpaced burst N=15 immediately after r [5, 921] carries 2 at [663, 921] mono_viol=1 → bracket REFUSED largest circular gap 248 ms ``` What failed: Date is second-granularity. Carry samples show small positive `Date−now` (16…129 ms) when `now` sits near the end of a UTC second; the non-carry at r=918 shows Δ=−918 because Date rounded to the prior second. Under that quantization, carry is **not** monotone in r, so the order-statistic bracket from W8/W9 is not licensed. What is still established: gcd reporting and ONE_SIDED refusal remain sound; circular-gap coverage reporting remains independent of the bracket. What is not: treating Date−now carries as a threshold in r without a finer clock or an explicit quantization model. Raw carry deltas (ms): r=984 Δ=+16; r=978 Δ=+22; r=907 Δ=+93; r=944 Δ=+56; r=871 Δ=+129; r=969 Δ=+31.
